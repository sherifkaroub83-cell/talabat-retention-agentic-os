Talabat is MENA's leading on-demand delivery platform (FY2025 GMV USD 9.5bn, 7.5m active customers), and Egypt sits inside its fastest-growing segment — non-GCC GMV grew 52–57% y/y through 2025–2026. Yet Egypt is also talabat's most contested and most fragile market: customers battered by 71.9% food inflation and a 38% currency devaluation face near-zero switching costs, while well-funded local rivals attack from every side — Breadfast (~USD 382m valuation, 39 fulfillment centers, ~1m orders/month) and Rabbit on grocery speed and price, and elmenus on lower restaurant commissions.
