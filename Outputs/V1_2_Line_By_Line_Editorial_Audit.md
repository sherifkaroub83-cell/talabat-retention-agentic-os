# Business Plan v1.2 — Line-by-Line Executive Editorial Audit

**Status:** Stage 1 complete — editorial reconstruction has not started.

**Documents audited:** `Business_Plan_v1.1_Executive_Visual.pdf` (25 pages), `Business_Plan_v1.1_Executive_Visual.docx`, and all 14 verified Markdown source sections.

## Audit method and governing controls

The PDF was treated as the page and layout authority, the DOCX as the structural authority, and the 14 verified Markdown sections as the factual and qualification authority. Every body paragraph, heading, bullet, table, exhibit, caption, and note in v1.1 is registered below. The audit does not authorize changes to validated facts, sources, approved recommendations, financial boundaries, geographic limitations, responsible-AI controls, the USD175m/USD120m/USD55m programme structure, or the substantive restrictions in DEC-008 and DEC-009.

## Executive editorial diagnosis

- The analytical content is strong, but the publication reads as an internal validation record rather than a Board-ready business plan.
- Pages 3–24 are consistently over-dense. Many paragraphs exceed executive-reading limits, and several tables require landscape treatment.
- Raw repository filenames, vault references, decision/assumption/option codes, and source IDs repeatedly interrupt the argument.
- Defensive qualifications recur in most sections instead of being consolidated into one Evidence Basis and Limitations section.
- The Executive Summary does not follow the required decision/recommendation/action sequence and is materially too dense for two-page use.
- The plan uses disclosure gaps too assertively in places. Governance, roles, systems, and country practices require absence-of-evidence wording.
- Marketing contains synthetic funnel values that must be removed from the main plan and replaced with a pilot-measurement framework.
- Monitoring exposes the 50-KPI internal architecture in the narrative; only 10–15 executive KPIs belong in the main body.
- Appendices are compressed into two pages and do not provide the seven reader-facing appendices required for v1.2.
- Visual review found no overlapping objects, but small type, narrow columns, crowded tables, and dense pages materially impair readability.
