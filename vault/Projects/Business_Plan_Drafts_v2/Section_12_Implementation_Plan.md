# 12. Implementation Plan (Three Horizons)

> **Pipeline provenance note:** Phase 9 pilot draft. Sequencing draws on `DEC-009`'s tiering of the
> five candidate Investment Options (`OPT-001`–`005`) by evidence strength, scalability, and
> reversibility, and `Scenarios_v2.md`'s eight scenario modules' own confidence levels. **`DEC-009`'s
> funding-sequence question is unresolved** — the horizon placement below is this plan's own proposed
> sequencing logic, explicitly labeled as such, not a committed or approved roadmap. Per `DEC-008`'s
> scope (extending to Section 12), no quantified financial exhibit or the ~2.2:1 bucket split appears
> here — sequencing is argued on evidence strength and reversibility, not dollar figures. **Pass 1,
> self-reviewed** — independent citation audit and QA still required.

## Answer, stated first

The five candidate Investment Options this plan has identified do not all belong at the same starting
point. `DEC-009`'s comparison places `OPT-001` (talabat mart densification) and `OPT-005`
(AI/personalisation scaling) in Tier 1 — strongest evidence, broadest market applicability — as
continue-at-pace items requiring no new pilot. `OPT-003` (Food-leadership CVP targeting) is Tier 2 —
strong but narrowly scoped to three GCC markets. `OPT-002` (talabat pro acceleration in Egypt/Iraq) and
`OPT-004` (advertising monetization) are Tier 3 — higher uncertainty, and per `DEC-009`'s own finding,
`OPT-002` is specifically the cheapest, most reversible, and best-suited to genuine piloting. This
plan's Three Horizons sequencing follows that tiering directly, not an assumed Egypt-first default.

## Three Horizons Roadmap

### Horizon 1 (0-6 months) — Continue Tier 1, pilot Tier 3's most reversible option

- **Continue at pace:** `OPT-001` (talabat mart densification) and `OPT-005` (AI/personalisation
  scaling) — both already underway as part of the disclosed Everyday App bucket; H1 requires no new
  decision, only continued monitoring against the KPI Tree's Portfolio and Operational families
  (`KPI_Tree_v2.md`).
- **Pilot:** `OPT-002` (talabat pro acceleration in Egypt and Iraq) — `DEC-009` identifies this as the
  cheapest and most reversible of the five options, and the one where GCC/Group evidence is most
  explicitly an inference rather than a proven Egypt/Iraq-specific effect (`ASM-016`, Low confidence).
  H1's job is to generate the first real Egypt/Iraq-specific pro-adoption evidence this plan currently
  lacks — not to commit to full-scale rollout on GCC evidence alone.
- **Monitor, do not yet fund a change:** `OPT-003` (Food-leadership, UAE/Kuwait/Qatar) — the
  underlying competitive-pressure trend (-4% y/y non-high-value M1 retention) is real and current
  (TLB-019, page 9); H1 confirms whether the already-disclosed ~USD55mn bucket is tracking that risk,
  rather than proposing incremental spend beyond what's already committed.

### Horizon 2 (6-18 months) — Evidence-gated decisions on Tier 2/3

- **`OPT-002` go/no-go:** contingent on H1's Egypt/Iraq pro-adoption evidence. If frequency/retention
  uplift approaches the GCC+Jordan cohort's 20-28%/26-32% range, this supports scaling; if materially
  below, `ASM-016`'s inference is not holding and the option should be reassessed, not scaled on
  optimism.
- **`OPT-003` evaluation:** using the H1 monitoring baseline, assess whether the -4% y/y non-high-value
  retention decline in UAE/Kuwait/Qatar is stabilizing; if not, this is the point at which a
  Decision Log entry proposing incremental Food-leadership spend (beyond the disclosed ~USD55mn) would
  need to be raised — not assumed here.
- **`OPT-004` (advertising monetization gap):** begin, given its longer disclosed time-to-value
  (`DEC-009`) — the ~7%-of-GMV UAE benchmark is a multi-period target, not a quick win.

### Horizon 3 (18+ months) — Scale what H1/H2 evidenced, revisit the cross-market gap

- Scale whichever of `OPT-002`/`OPT-003` H2's evidence-gated decisions supported; hold or reassess
  whichever did not clear its evidence bar.
- Revisit the largest remaining open question from Section 2.2 (problem 3: cross-market allocation) —
  by H3, two full evidence cycles (H1 pilot, H2 evaluation) should exist for at least `OPT-002`,
  materially improving on the public-corpus-only evidence base this plan currently has for Egypt/Iraq
  and non-GCC markets generally.
- `OPT-004`'s advertising monetization gap, if H2 shows progress toward the UAE benchmark, extends to
  additional markets.

## Dependencies and Timelines

H2 decisions are explicitly gated on H1 evidence, not calendar time alone — per the KPI Tree's
Governance family (`KPI_Tree_v2.md`), none of which currently has a baseline. The single largest
dependency across all three horizons is the same one flagged in Section 2.2: no market-level allocation
logic is disclosed anywhere in the corpus, so every horizon's country-specific decision (Egypt/Iraq for
`OPT-002`, UAE/Kuwait/Qatar for `OPT-003`) is this plan's own proposed evidence-gate structure, not a
validated talabat process.

## Marketing Rollout Plan

Not primarily applicable to this plan's actual product (an internal capital-allocation decision-support
system, per Section 5) — the "rollout" that matters is internal: to the extent H1's `OPT-002` pilot
involves customer-facing changes (accelerated pro marketing in Egypt/Iraq), that rollout should follow
the same evidence-gated logic as the funding decision itself, not run ahead of it.

## Team Expansion and Recruitment Goals

Not disclosed anywhere in the corpus for either the underlying AI capability or a capital-allocation
function (`Capital Allocation and Investment Governance.md`'s governance-mechanics gap extends here) —
this plan does not invent a headcount figure. The genuine, evidenced need is process, not headcount: an
owner for the KPI Tree's Governance family and the `DEC-009`/future-decision escalation path, a role this
plan assigns functionally (Decision Steward) rather than as a proposed new hire.
