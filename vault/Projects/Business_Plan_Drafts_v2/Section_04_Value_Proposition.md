## Answer, stated first

The value this plan creates does not come from a single new capability — it comes from applying a
disciplined ranking-and-monitoring layer to five value mechanisms talabat has already proven, at
Group/GCC scale, but has not yet evidenced as deliberately sequenced against its 2026 investment
programme. Multi-vertical engagement (13.0x vs. 3.8x order frequency between multi-vertical and
food-only customers), talabat pro (20-28% frequency uplift, 26-32% retention uplift), targeted
incentives (Rewards +15%, PostPaid +14%), and AI/personalisation (embedded, not a separate budget line)
are each independently evidenced value drivers — this section's contribution is showing how they trace,
one by one, through `Investment_Relationship_Map.md`'s chain to GMV, revenue, gross profit, and EBITDA,
and where each one still lacks Egypt/non-GCC-specific proof.

## 4.1 Unique Selling Proposition (USP)

The USP is not a new customer-facing product — it is **evidence-ranked, stage-gated capital allocation**
applied to an already-committed USD175mn programme. talabat's own disclosed rationale for the programme
is qualitative ("investments rather than costs... expected to offset the margin impact over time,"
TLB-020, page 16); no competitor benchmark or internal capital-allocation methodology is disclosed
anywhere in the corpus (`Topics/Capital Allocation and Investment Governance.md`). The USP is therefore
comparative in kind, not degree: a transparent, evidence-traceable allocation framework where every
recommended dollar amount resolves to a named Fact, Decision, or labeled Assumption — versus the
undisclosed status quo. Argued narratively (per `DEC-008`, not as a quantified exhibit): the disclosed
~2.2:1 weighting toward Everyday App over Food-leadership is itself a real, evidenced management
judgment (`Topics/2026 Investment Programme.md`) — this plan's initiative-level ranking (`DEC-009`)
engages with, rather than second-guesses, that top-level weighting.

## 4.2 Value Creation Mechanisms — Each Quantified

**Revenue lift — multi-vertical engagement.** Multi-vertical customers show 13.0x order frequency vs.
3.8x for food-only customers (`Topics/Multi-Verticality.md`; `Value_Driver_Tree_v2.md` N-14, July 2024,
not refreshed since), and multi-vertical GMV share has risen 68%→73%→76% across Dec'24→Dec'25→Mar'26
(`Value_Driver_Tree_v2.md` N-11). This is the single
strongest quantified mechanism in the corpus and the direct evidence base for `OPT-001` (talabat mart
densification, the primary lever for driving multi-vertical adoption).

**Revenue lift — talabat pro.** Subscribers show a 20-28% order-frequency uplift and 26-32% retention
uplift vs. matched non-subscribers, measured across the GCC+Jordan cohort where the programme was live
before December 2024 (`ASM-016`, Low confidence for extension beyond that cohort). talabat pro subscriber
GMV share has grown from 32% to 49% of platform GMV (FY2024→Q1 2026). This is the direct evidence base
for `OPT-002` (accelerating pro adoption in Egypt and Iraq, where the programme has not yet reached
comparable maturity).

**Revenue lift — targeted incentives.** talabat rewards redeemers show >15% order-frequency increase
within 30 days; PostPaid adopters show a 14% frequency increase, funded by CARC rising from USD89mn
(1.5% of GMV, 2023) to USD155mn (1.6% of GMV, 2025) (`Topics/Customer Economics.md`;
`Facts/Customer_Growth.md`). Unlike pro,
PostPaid has a directly evidenced non-GCC rollout precedent (Egypt PostPaid + CIB co-branded card,
`Topics/FinTech.md`) — the strongest single Egypt-specific value-mechanism data point in this section.

**Efficiency — AI/personalisation.** Embedded (not a discrete budget line) across recommendation
ranking, offer timing, and dispatch optimisation; Group-level estimated EBITDA contribution rose from
>USD14mn (FY2024) to >USD30mn (FY2025) (`Topics/AI.md`; `Value_Driver_Tree_v2.md` N-09). This is the
direct evidence base for `OPT-005`.

**Cost savings — AI-driven fraud detection.** A distinct AI application from personalisation above:
Group-level estimated savings of ~USD6mn/year from efficient, ML-based fraud detection
(`Facts/AI_Facts.md`, TLB-015 page 21). Smaller in scale than the personalisation EBITDA contribution,
but a genuine, separately-quantified cost-savings mechanism, not a restatement of the efficiency bullet
above — closes the template's fifth value-creation-mechanism category on a real, cited figure rather
than an invented one.

**Revenue lift — advertising monetization.** Advertising & listing fees revenue grew USD246mn→USD323mn
(FY2024→FY2025, +32% y/y); talabat's own Group-wide AdTech penetration (3.3-3.5% of GMV) remains well
below the Group's own stated **global medium-term target of ~7% of GMV** (TLB-001, page 21;
`Facts/Advertising_Facts.md`). Separately — a different geography and a narrower product scope, not the
same figure — **UAE talabat mart advertising margins have already reached ~7% of GMV** (TLB-002, page 10;
TLB-014, page 19), a UAE-and-tMart-specific *achieved* result, not a Group-wide benchmark. Both point the
same direction (headroom exists to close the Group AdTech gap) but are two distinct facts — the direct
evidence base for `OPT-004`.

**Risk reduction — Food-leadership CVP investment.** Non-high-value customer M1 retention declined 4%
y/y in UAE/Kuwait/Qatar specifically, attributed to "competitive pressure" (TLB-019, page 9;
`Topics/Food Leadership.md`) — the ~USD55mn Food-leadership bucket is management's own stated response.
This mechanism is explicitly a defensive (risk-reduction), not growth, value category — the direct
evidence base for `OPT-003`.

**Insight — Governance/measurement infrastructure.** The KPI Tree's Governance family (7 KPIs, all
newly-instrumented — `KPI_Tree_v2.md`) is itself a value mechanism: it converts an undisclosed process
into a measurable one, the precondition for any of the above mechanisms being monitored rather than
assumed to work.

## 4.3 Impact on Customer Outcomes and ROI — Calculation Logic Shown

**Customer outcome, stated honestly:** no absolute customer lifetime value figure is disclosed anywhere
in the corpus (`Topics/Customer Lifetime Value.md`); the mechanisms above are evidenced as relative
uplifts and GMV-share trends, not an absolute CLV number this section can cite. `Topics/Customer
Economics.md`'s clearest quantified customer-outcome proxy is the >4x gap in monthly spend between
multi-vertical and food-only customers — this is the number this plan uses to argue customer-level
value creation, not an invented CLV figure.

**ROI, calculation logic shown, not a single point estimate:** the corpus discloses the programme's
*cost* precisely (the FY2026 Adjusted EBITDA margin bridge, 6.0%→4.6% of GMV guided, realized 4.8% in
Q1 2026) but **no return figure for any bucket or initiative** (`Investment_Relationship_Map.md`,
"What this note deliberately does not claim"). The calculation logic this plan uses instead: each
mechanism's historical uplift statistic (e.g. talabat pro's 20-28% frequency uplift) is the input to
`Scenarios_v2.md`'s Assumption-tagged nodes (`ASM-021`–`028`), which project forward
directionally with a stated confidence level, never a single-point ROI. Per `DEC-008`, these
range-based projections are shown in Section 9 at the Group base-case level only — this section shows
the mechanism, Section 9 shows the (appropriately bounded) financial consequence.
