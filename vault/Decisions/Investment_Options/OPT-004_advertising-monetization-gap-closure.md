# OPT-004 — Close the advertising monetization gap against the disclosed UAE benchmark

## Rationale
Advertising & listing-fees revenue is the corpus's most consistently disclosed, multi-year-growing
revenue line (USD246mn → USD323mn, +32% y/y, FY2024→FY2025) — and it carries a materially better
margin profile than G&R, since it does not share G&R's disclosed EBITDA-margin drag. UAE talabat mart
specifically has already reached a ~7% of GMV medium-term advertising benchmark that the Group
overall has not (TLB-001 p.21; TLB-002 p.10). This option proposes investing in the capability to
close that Group-vs-UAE monetization gap — a genuine, management-validated ceiling reference, though
not a disclosed Group-wide target or committed timeline.

## Evidence
- **Fact (Group):** advertising & listing-fees revenue USD246mn (FY2024) → USD323mn (FY2025), +32%
  y/y (TLB-002 p.20; TLB-009 p.4).
- **Fact (country-specific, UAE):** UAE talabat mart already at ~7% of GMV medium-term benchmark
  (TLB-001 p.21; TLB-002 p.10; TLB-014 p.19).
- **Fact (Group):** AI/personalisation deployment (item-level recommendations, 235TB/day data
  infrastructure) is the mechanism management credits with driving ad/offer targeting (TLB-002
  p.15-16).
- **Forecast (Medium confidence base, Low upside/downside):** `Scenarios_v2.md` advertising module,
  `ASM-025` (base); UAE-benchmark-reached-Group-wide upside (explicitly labeled inferred-applicability
  extrapolation); Partner-economics-tension-with-Food-leadership downside.

## Geography
**Group** intended target; the underlying 7%-of-GMV benchmark itself is **country-specific (UAE)** —
applying it as a Group-level target is an explicit inferred-applicability extrapolation, not a
disclosed Group commitment or timeline.

## Value drivers
AI/personalisation capability (N-06) supports ad-targeting infrastructure → advertising revenue
(N-29) → Revenue / gross profit / EBITDA, without incurring G&R's disclosed margin-drag trade-off,
since advertising revenue is not shown anywhere in the corpus to carry a comparable margin penalty.

## Required capabilities
A Partner-facing ad-sales/adtech capability build (the GEMs-type product referenced in the corpus),
item-level ad-targeting infrastructure (shared with OPT-005), and sales capacity to sell ad inventory
to Partners across all 8 markets, not just the UAE where the benchmark is proven.

## Cost
Cross-cutting; drawn primarily from Everyday App opex (adtech/data infrastructure), since advertising
is not itself one of the two disclosed buckets' named components. Range is this OS's own proposed
judgment (`ASM-032`) — explicitly not a disclosed bucket line.

## Time to impact
1-2 years — adtech capability build and Partner sales-cycle ramp are longer-horizon than a pure
incentive-spend option (contrast with OPT-002/OPT-003).

## Complexity
**Medium-High** — requires a Partner-facing commercial capability build, not just a budget increase;
GEMs' actual effectiveness at winning back churned Partner customers is unquantified anywhere in the
corpus (`Advertising.md` Open Questions).

## Dependencies
Shares the personalisation/data-infrastructure layer with OPT-005. Competes for the same Partner
economic relationship as OPT-003's commission-rate investment — the Scenarios downside case names
this tension explicitly; the plan should flag it, not silently net it out.

## Risks
GEMs' effectiveness at winning back churned Partners is unquantified. Partner-side ad spend could
soften if Food-leadership's commission investment (OPT-003) competes for the same Partner budget
relationship at the same time.

## Confidence
**Medium** for the base-case revenue-trend continuation (a strong, multi-year disclosed trajectory,
USD84mn→USD323mn 2021-2025); **Low** for the UAE-benchmark-reached-Group-wide upside — no source
states a timeline or commitment for the Group to reach the UAE-proven benchmark.

## Recommended allocation range
**USD 8-12mn (base) / USD 12-18mn (upside) / USD 3-6mn (downside)**, cross-cutting, drawn primarily
from Everyday App opex. Basis: `ASM-032` — team judgment, unvalidated, pending `DEC-009`.

## Pilot recommendation
Recommend piloting the UAE-proven ad-product/sales-process in one or two additional high-GMV markets
before an 8-market rollout. The corpus does not specify which markets would be next-best candidates —
that choice itself would require a follow-on Decision if this option is pursued.

## Stage gates
Advertising revenue as % of GMV (F6) trending measurably toward the 7%-of-GMV UAE benchmark in the
pilot market(s) within 2-3 quarters before further scale-up funding.
