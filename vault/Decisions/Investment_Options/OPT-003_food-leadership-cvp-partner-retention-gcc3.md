# OPT-003 — Target Food-leadership CVP/partner-retention investment at UAE, Kuwait, and Qatar specifically

## Rationale
The only disclosed, quantified competitive-pressure evidence in the entire corpus motivating the
~USD55mn Food-leadership bucket — a non-high-value M1 retention decline of -4% y/y — is scoped
specifically to UAE, Kuwait, and Qatar (TLB-019 p.9). No source discloses how the ~USD55mn bucket is
or will be allocated across talabat's 8 markets, or specifically to these three evidenced markets
(`Food Leadership.md` Open Questions). This option proposes explicitly weighting Food-leadership
CVP/partner-retention spend toward the three markets where the evidence for the problem actually
exists, rather than an unstated (and possibly uniform, possibly diluted) 8-market spread.

## Evidence
- **Fact (country-specific/market-comparison, UAE/Kuwait/Qatar):** non-high-value M1 retention -4%
  y/y (TLB-019 p.9).
- **Fact (Group):** CVP-over-discounting mechanism — "rather than matching competitor discounts and
  vouchers, we invest in the consumer value proposition... on the partner side, we invest in
  retaining, winning back, and acquiring high-demand food partners... reflected in commission rate
  investments" (TLB-020 p.16; TLB-014 p.19).
- **Fact (Group):** "meaningful dry powder" retained to defend Food leadership "if required," with no
  disclosed dollar amount or trigger condition (TLB-014 p.9; TLB-020 p.14).
- **Fact (Group):** Food GMV USD6.65bn, +20% y/y vs. G&R's USD2.77bn (TLB-002 p.18; TLB-019 p.14) —
  Food remains the majority of Group GMV despite receiving less than a third of the total programme.
- **Forecast (Medium confidence base, Low upside/downside):** `Scenarios_v2.md` Food-leadership
  module, `ASM-027`.

## Geography
**country-specific/market-comparison** for the competitive-pressure evidence itself (UAE, Kuwait,
Qatar); the funding source (the Food-leadership bucket) is disclosed only at Group level. This option
deliberately does **not** extend the competitive-pressure rationale to Egypt or other non-GCC
markets — `Food Leadership.md`'s own Open Questions confirm that evidence does not exist anywhere in
the corpus, and this option is designed to avoid that specific unevidenced extrapolation (contrast
with OPT-002, which extends different Group/GCC evidence to Egypt/Iraq explicitly and labels that
inference).

## Value drivers
Capability (N-05, CVP/partner retention/commission investment) → customer behaviour (N-12, medium/
high-value customer retention via subscription + targeted incentives) → Food GMV (N-26) → gross
profit / EBITDA.

## Required capabilities
No new capability build — subscription and targeted-incentive infrastructure (talabat pro, Rewards)
already operates in all three markets. Requires commission-rate negotiation capacity with high-demand
Partners specifically in UAE, Kuwait, and Qatar.

## Cost
Draws from the Food-leadership bucket (~USD55mn). Range is this OS's own proposed judgment
(`ASM-031`); no market-level allocation of this bucket is disclosed anywhere in the corpus.

## Time to impact
Quarters — non-high-value M1 retention is a metric talabat already tracks and discloses for these
three markets, so near-term measurement is plausible.

## Complexity
**Medium** — no new capability build required, but Partner commission renegotiation and timing a
response to competitive pressure are operationally and commercially sensitive.

## Dependencies
Shares Partner-funded economic capacity with OPT-002 (talabat pro Egypt/Iraq acceleration) — both
options draw on the same underlying Partner relationship and commission economics.

## Risks
"Dry powder" carries no disclosed trigger condition or dollar amount (`Food Leadership.md` Open
Questions) — deploying capital against an undefined reserve mechanism is itself a governance gap
this option surfaces rather than resolves (see KPI G1/G5). Competitive response from named/generic
rivals could outpace CVP investment. Extending this option's logic to non-GCC markets would be an
unevidenced extrapolation this option explicitly avoids making.

## Confidence
**Medium** for the competitive-pressure Fact itself (real, quantified, for exactly these three
markets) and for the GMV-continuation base case; **Low** for any assumption that CVP investment
reverses (rather than merely holds) the observed retention decline — that causal link is not
disclosed anywhere in the corpus.

## Recommended allocation range
**USD 25-32mn (base) / USD 32-40mn (upside, dry powder deployed) / USD 15-22mn (downside)**, within
the Food-leadership bucket. Basis: `ASM-031` — team judgment, unvalidated, pending `DEC-009`.

## Pilot recommendation
Not a pilot — this is an already-evidenced, already-operating mechanism (subscription/incentive
infrastructure exists). Recommend a targeted budget weighting toward these three markets within the
existing bucket, plus a formally documented dry-powder deployment trigger (currently absent) rather
than an open-ended reserve.

## Stage gates (verified 2026-07-24)
Verification pass per Pipeline Stage 12. The sketch above stated the right substance but its primary
outcome metric has **no KPI_Tree_v2 ID** (gap re-confirmed below), and it lacked review points and
funding consequences. Review-cadence and threshold discipline follows the carried-forward
methodology of `DEC-007` (superseded pre-pivot record; quarterly review cadence, qualitative
trajectory-relative criteria where no baseline series exists, numeric thresholds only via a future
Decision Log entry at first real baseline reading). Funding consequences follow `DEC-009`'s approved
sequence: OPT-003 is a **near-term priority** alongside OPT-002.

1. **Gate 1 — Retention-trend stabilization (flagged-gap metric).** Metric: country-level
   non-high-value M1 retention for UAE/Kuwait/Qatar — **no KPI_Tree_v2 ID exists for this metric**
   (genuine KPI gap, flagged below; not added to the tree by this verification pass). Threshold:
   qualitative/directional against the single disclosed anchor (-4% y/y, TLB-019 p.9) — trend
   stabilizing or improving within 2-3 quarters of targeted spend. The disclosed -4% is a one-off
   anchor, not a baseline series; **threshold to be set at first recurring baseline reading** via a
   Decision Log entry. Review: quarterly from first targeted-spend quarter; decisive read at
   quarter 3 (approx. Q4 2026 per this record's review date). Consequence: stabilizing/improving →
   **continue** targeted GCC-3 weighting; continued deterioration through quarter 3 → **hold** the
   weighting and escalate a reassessment (targeted-weighting vs. 8-market-spread) as a new Decision
   Log entry — do not silently expand spend against a failing disclosed trend.
2. **Gate 2 — Dry-powder governance precondition.** KPIs: **G5** (ROI/payback hurdle-rate
   compliance) and **G1** (% of spend passing a documented stage-gate) — both **newly-instrumented**
   Governance-family KPIs with no corpus baseline (talabat discloses almost no allocation-governance
   mechanics; this is a mechanism the programme must start measuring, not improve against a known
   number). Threshold: qualitative — a documented dry-powder deployment trigger and hurdle logic
   exists *before* any reserve deployment; no numeric trigger is invented here. Review: ad hoc,
   before any reserve release, plus quarterly G1/G5 tracking. Consequence: no documented trigger →
   **hold** — zero dry-powder deployment regardless of competitive conditions (the upside range's
   "dry powder deployed" case stays unfunded).
3. **Gate 3 — Bucket reallocation transparency and Food-GMV confirmation.** KPIs: **G2** (intra-year
   reallocation amount/frequency between buckets — newly-instrumented) and **F3** (Food vs. G&R GMV
   split and growth — baseline-exists, Food +20% y/y FY2025). Threshold: qualitative — any
   GCC-3-weighting reallocation is documented via G2; Food GMV growth shows no material sustained
   deceleration below the disclosed trend (lagging confirmation, trajectory-relative, no invented
   floor). Review: quarterly at results disclosures. Consequence: undocumented reallocation or
   sustained Food-GMV deterioration despite spend → **hold** further weighting increases and
   escalate.
