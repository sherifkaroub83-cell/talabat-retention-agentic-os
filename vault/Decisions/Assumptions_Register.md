> that work: each row is read and reclassified on its own merits against the new Group-wide
> capital-allocation problem (`Problem_Charter.md`), not relabeled wholesale. **This section does not
> alter the 14 rows above, their `Status` cells, or the blanket-superseded banner** — it is a separate,
> additive analytical layer. Full source: the 14 rows' own `Statement`/`Source` cells above and the 7
> Decision Log entries (`DEC-001` through `DEC-007`) they trace to, all read in full for this pass.
>
> **Classifications used:** *still-valid* (holds regardless of which business problem is being asked —
> a disclosed, Group-level fact independent of the retention-vs-allocation framing) · *requires-revision*
> (the underlying evidence remains relevant and reusable, but its statement/use needs restating for the
> Group-wide, multi-market capital-allocation framing rather than the Egypt-only retention framing) ·
> *obsolete* (specific to the old Egypt-retention question; no bearing on the new problem) ·
> *retained-as-historical* (worth keeping visible as a record of what the OS already established, but
> should not be cited forward into any new forecast or decision — the structure it belonged to is being
> fully rebuilt, not edited, per `vault/Architecture/Repository_Impact_Assessment.md` §5). None of the
> four `requires-revision` or `still-valid` rows below may be cited in new Business Plan content as-is —
> under this register's own rule, a fresh `Decision`/`Assumption` row (ASM-015 onward) must formally
> re-establish each one under the new problem before it is citable; this table only determines which old
