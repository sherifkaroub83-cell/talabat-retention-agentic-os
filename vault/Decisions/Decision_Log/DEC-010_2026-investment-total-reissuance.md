# DEC-010 — Procedural re-issuance of the USD175mn = ~USD120mn Everyday App + ~USD55mn Food-leadership reconciliation under the active problem

## Tooling note (read first)
Drafted directly by the top-level session (not a subagent) during the Final Execution Readiness pass,
closing a governance-hygiene gap `Assumptions_Register.md`'s own "Pivot Reclassification" section
identified but left open on 2026-07-23: `ASM-006` classifies the USD175mn/120mn/55mn reconciliation as
**still-valid** under the new Group-wide capital-allocation problem (a disclosed, Group-level fact
independent of the retention-vs-allocation framing), but its formal Decision Log container, `DEC-004`,
is marked `status: superseded` — a container-status/content-validity mismatch, not a content error. The
register explicitly recommended "procedural re-issuance under a new ID for governance completeness, not
a content revision." This is that re-issuance.

## Question
Should the USD175mn = ~USD120mn Everyday App + ~USD55mn Food-leadership reconciliation (originally
established in `DEC-004`) have a Decision Log record whose own `status` field is not itself superseded,
given the reconciliation is still actively cited under the current governing problem?

## Decision
**Yes.** `DEC-010` formally re-establishes `DEC-004`'s reconciliation, unchanged in substance, as an
active decision under the current problem. No new evidence was examined and no figure was
re-derived — this record reissues `DEC-004`'s own Evidence re-examination and Decision sections by
reference, not by rewriting them (`DEC-004`'s content is preserved exactly as originally approved, per
this project's standing rule against editing a superseded decision's Decision/Rationale fields).

**The reconciliation, restated:** the USD175mn Board-approved 2026 total (TLB-020, p.16) decomposes into
~USD120mn Everyday App (~USD75mn opex + ~USD45mn capex) + ~USD55mn Food-leadership; TLB-019's
">USD100mn" (p.11) is an earlier, same-scope, lower-precision disclosure of the Everyday App component
only. High confidence for the 175=120+55 decomposition (directly stated in one source document); Medium
confidence for the TLB-019-to-TLB-020 linkage inference.

## Rationale
This is a container-level fix, not a content decision — the underlying fact never stopped being true
when the problem pivoted; only the record documenting it was swept into a blanket `superseded` flag
applied to all of `DEC-001`–`007` on 2026-07-23 without individual re-examination (the same blanket
action the Assumptions Register's Pivot Reclassification section performed and then individually
re-examined for its own 14 rows). `DEC-004` itself stays `superseded` and unedited, as the historical
record of the original Section 9 pilot decision; `DEC-010` is the fresh, active container the current
problem's citations should point to going forward.
