# Investment Portfolio Register

The envelope-level rollup for talabat's 2026 investment programme — the register the capital-allocation
business question is actually about. Distinct from `Investment_Options_Register.md` (candidate
sub-initiatives) and `Assumptions_Register.md` (individual numeric assumptions): this register holds
the **portfolio structure** those feed into.

## Total envelope

**USD 175 million**, Board-approved February 2026, fully funded by internal cash (no external
financing) *(Group; TLB-019, page 11; TLB-020, page 16)*.

## Disclosed components (mandatory — talabat has already committed to this split)

| Component | Amount | % of GMV | Composition | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Everyday App** | ~USD 120mn | ~0.7pp | ~USD 75mn opex + ~USD 45mn capex — talabat mart (dark-store densification), talabat pro (loyalty scaling), new verticals/ventures | TLB-020, pages 12, 16; TLB-014, pages 6, 19 |
| **Food-leadership** | ~USD 55mn | ~0.5pp | Consumer value proposition (selection, experience, affordability); partner retention/win-back/acquisition via commission-rate investment; customer subscription + targeted incentives | TLB-020, page 16 |

**These two figures are disclosed, not decided by this OS** — they are the input the capital-allocation
question operates on, not an output of it. See `Topics/2026 Investment Programme.md` for the
full disclosure-evolution trace (the split sharpened across three documents between Feb and May 2026).

**Optional / not-yet-disclosed components:** the corpus discloses no further mandatory/optional
split within either bucket, no separate "pilot funding" or "reserve" line, and no
reallocation-pool mechanism — see `Topics/Capital Allocation and Investment Governance.md`'s Open
Questions. Any such sub-structure proposed later by this OS (e.g. a recommended pilot-funding
carve-out) must be logged as a `DEC-` decision and an `OPT-` option, not silently added here as if
disclosed.

## Base / downside / upside allocation ranges

**Resolved 2026-07-23 by [[DEC-009_investment-options-ranking-and-allocation-priority|DEC-009]]
(status: `approved` — middle path).** The disclosed USD175mn/120mn/55mn figures themselves remain point
figures from talabat's own Board approval, unchanged by this section — they need no "base case" because
they are already the approved case. What follows is **this OS's own analytical sub-bucket allocation
logic** for five evidence-grounded candidate Investment Options (`OPT-001`–`OPT-005`, registered in
`Investment_Options_Register.md`, compared in `DEC-009`), not a disclosed talabat allocation at this
granularity. Every range below is sourced to a Decision-tier, Low-confidence Assumption Register row
(`ASM-029`–`ASM-033`), now `Approved` per `DEC-009`'s resolution.

**Mandatory placement/labeling rule (DEC-009):** the Business Plan shall make a clear recommended
funding sequence, argued narratively on the 11-criterion comparison in `DEC-009` — that is the plan's
actual recommendation. The dollar ranges below shall **never** be presented as a headline financial
commitment; they may appear only as a clearly labeled illustrative sensitivity/appendix exhibit
(Section 14 Appendix, or a footnoted supporting exhibit in Section 9/12), and every appearance must
carry the sentence: **"This is an Agentic OS analytical recommendation, not a disclosed talabat
allocation."** This rule applies identically to Business Plan drafting, forecasting outputs, and the
Publication Layer.

| Option | Bucket | Base | Upside | Downside | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| OPT-001 — talabat mart dark-store densification | Everyday App | USD 45-55mn | USD 55-70mn | USD 30-40mn | `ASM-029` |
| OPT-002 — talabat pro acceleration (Egypt, Iraq) | Everyday App (opex) | USD 8-12mn | USD 12-20mn | USD 3-6mn | `ASM-030` |
| OPT-003 — Food-leadership CVP/partner-retention (UAE, Kuwait, Qatar) | Food-leadership | USD 25-32mn | USD 32-40mn | USD 15-22mn | `ASM-031` |
| OPT-004 — Advertising monetization gap closure | Enabling capability (cross-cutting, drawn from Everyday App opex) | USD 8-12mn | USD 12-18mn | USD 3-6mn | `ASM-032` |
| OPT-005 — AI/personalisation capability scaling | Enabling capability (cross-cutting, drawn from Everyday App opex) | USD 10-15mn | USD 15-22mn | USD 5-8mn | `ASM-033` |

**Read this table with two explicit caveats, both required to avoid a Financial Integrity Gate failure:**
1. **Non-additive across the envelope.** OPT-004 and OPT-005 are cross-cutting Enabling-capability
   options that may overlap with, rather than add on top of, the Everyday App figures already implied
   by OPT-001/OPT-002 — the corpus itself states AI/personalisation is "not named as a discrete dollar
   line within either bucket... an embedded capability, not a separate budget line" (N-06). Summing all
   five ranges' upper bounds would overstate the disclosed USD175mn envelope if read as strictly
   additive; it is not intended to be read that way.
2. **Partial coverage, not a full allocation.** These five options do not claim to allocate 100% of
   either bucket — Everyday App's disclosed "new verticals" component and Food-leadership's spend
   outside UAE/Kuwait/Qatar are real, funded parts of the envelope this table does not attempt to size,
   since no evidence-grounded candidate option exists yet for them.

This still leaves genuinely unset only: scenario ranges for the *expected financial return* of the full
envelope (GMV/revenue/EBITDA impact) — see `vault/Forecasts/Scenarios_v2.md`'s top-level base/upside/
downside scenarios for that, resolved separately by `DEC-008` (Section 9's headline case is base-case-
only). Whether this table is shown in the Business Plan, and how, is now resolved by `DEC-009` — see the
mandatory placement/labeling rule above.

## Pilot funding / reserve / reallocation pool

**Not disclosed by talabat; not yet proposed by this OS.** Per
`Topics/Capital Allocation and Investment Governance.md`, the corpus names no internal
stage-gate, reserve, or reallocation mechanism for this programme. If this OS's eventual
recommendation (Business Plan Section 9/12) proposes one, it must be registered as a `DEC-`
decision, with the mechanism explicitly labeled as this OS's proposal rather than talabat's actual
practice.

## Market-level logic

**Not disclosed.** No document in the corpus breaks either bucket out by country, GCC/non-GCC, or
Egypt-standalone. This is the single largest evidence gap the business plan's investment
recommendation has to work around — see `Topics/Capital Allocation and Investment Governance.md`
Open Questions and `Topics/GCC vs non-GCC.md`.

## Expected outcomes and confidence

**Directional only.** talabat states the investments are expected to be net-accretive over time
("these are investments rather than costs because the growth they generate is expected to offset
the margin impact over time," TLB-020, page 16) with the stated qualitative exit criterion of "a
more loyal customer base" (TLB-002, page 10). No quantified ROI, payback period, or targeted
GMV/EBITDA uplift is disclosed for either bucket. Confidence in the *existence and size* of the
envelope: High (Board-approved, consistently disclosed across TLB-014/TLB-019/TLB-020). Confidence
in any *return* figure: not applicable — none is disclosed to have a confidence level about.

## Owner

This register is owned by the Decision Steward Agent, with capital-allocation recommendations
ultimately requiring human/team approval before being presented as a Business Plan
recommendation — per the Responsible-AI principle that this OS may recommend allocation, it must
not represent that recommendation as an automatic management decision (formalized in the
agent/skill definitions, Phase 6 of the 2026-07-23 pivot).
