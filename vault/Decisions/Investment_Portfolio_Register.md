# Investment Portfolio Register

The envelope-level rollup for talabat's 2026 investment programme — the register the capital-allocation
business question is actually about. Distinct from `Investment_Options_Register.md` (candidate
sub-initiatives) and `Assumptions_Register.md` (individual numeric assumptions): this register holds
the **portfolio structure** those feed into.

## Total envelope

**USD 175 million**, Board-approved February 2026, fully funded by internal cash (no external
financing) *(Group; TLB-019, page 11; TLB-020, page 16)*.

## Disclosed components (mandatory — talabat has already committed to this split)

| Component | Amount | % of GMV | Composition | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Everyday App** | ~USD 120mn | ~0.7pp | ~USD 75mn opex + ~USD 45mn capex — talabat mart (dark-store densification), talabat pro (loyalty scaling), new verticals/ventures | TLB-020, pages 12, 16; TLB-014, pages 6, 19 |
| **Food-leadership** | ~USD 55mn | ~0.5pp | Consumer value proposition (selection, experience, affordability); partner retention/win-back/acquisition via commission-rate investment; customer subscription + targeted incentives | TLB-020, page 16 |

**These two figures are disclosed, not decided by this OS** — they are the input the capital-allocation
question operates on, not an output of it. See `Topics/2026 Investment Programme.md` for the
full disclosure-evolution trace (the split sharpened across three documents between Feb and May 2026).

**Optional / not-yet-disclosed components:** the corpus discloses no further mandatory/optional
split within either bucket, no separate "pilot funding" or "reserve" line, and no
reallocation-pool mechanism — see `Topics/Capital Allocation and Investment Governance.md`'s Open
Questions. Any such sub-structure proposed later by this OS (e.g. a recommended pilot-funding
carve-out) must be logged as a `DEC-` decision and an `OPT-` option, not silently added here as if
disclosed.

## Base / downside / upside allocation ranges

**Not yet set.** The disclosed USD175mn/120mn/55mn figures are point figures from talabat's own
Board approval, not scenario ranges — they need no "base case" because they are already the
approved case. What this OS still needs to build (Phase 5, Forecasting layer) is: (a) scenario
ranges for the *expected financial return* of that already-approved envelope (GMV/revenue/EBITDA
impact under base/upside/downside assumptions), and (b) if this OS ultimately recommends a
different intra-programme allocation than talabat's own, ranges for that recommendation specifically.
Neither exists yet. Populating this section with placeholder numbers now would be the exact
false-precision error the pivot's standing instructions forbid.

## Pilot funding / reserve / reallocation pool

**Not disclosed by talabat; not yet proposed by this OS.** Per
`Topics/Capital Allocation and Investment Governance.md`, the corpus names no internal
stage-gate, reserve, or reallocation mechanism for this programme. If this OS's eventual
recommendation (Business Plan Section 9/12) proposes one, it must be registered as a `DEC-`
decision, with the mechanism explicitly labeled as this OS's proposal rather than talabat's actual
practice.

## Market-level logic

**Not disclosed.** No document in the corpus breaks either bucket out by country, GCC/non-GCC, or
Egypt-standalone. This is the single largest evidence gap the business plan's investment
recommendation has to work around — see `Topics/Capital Allocation and Investment Governance.md`
Open Questions and `Topics/GCC vs non-GCC.md`.

## Expected outcomes and confidence

**Directional only.** talabat states the investments are expected to be net-accretive over time
("these are investments rather than costs because the growth they generate is expected to offset
the margin impact over time," TLB-020, page 16) with the stated qualitative exit criterion of "a
more loyal customer base" (TLB-002, page 10). No quantified ROI, payback period, or targeted
GMV/EBITDA uplift is disclosed for either bucket. Confidence in the *existence and size* of the
envelope: High (Board-approved, consistently disclosed across TLB-014/TLB-019/TLB-020). Confidence
in any *return* figure: not applicable — none is disclosed to have a confidence level about.

## Owner

This register is owned by the Decision Steward Agent, with capital-allocation recommendations
ultimately requiring human (instructor/team) approval before being presented as a Business Plan
recommendation — per the Responsible-AI principle that this OS may recommend allocation, it must
not represent that recommendation as an automatic management decision (Phase 6 will formalize this
in the agent/skill definitions).

## Review date

To be set once Phase 5 (Forecasting) produces the first scenario-based return estimates this
register's "expected outcomes" section currently lacks.
