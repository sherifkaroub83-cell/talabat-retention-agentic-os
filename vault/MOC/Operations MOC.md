## Known gap (flagged across every Operations Topic Note)
No Partner/restaurant, rider, delivery-time, or safety-score figure in the corpus is broken out for Egypt specifically — every operational metric is a platform-wide (8-country) aggregate. The only Egypt-specific operational data point found anywhere is a 200-EV pilot fleet (end-2025).
