# AI

## Overview
AI/ML at talabat is a horizontal capability that shows up across four distinct functions in the corpus: customer-facing personalisation (recommendations and offer timing), logistics (predictive dispatch and route optimisation), advertising (AI-driven ad-targeting and automated ad-buying), and internal operations (invoice matching, sales-lead qualification, fraud detection) (TLB-001, page 10, 13, 22-23; TLB-002, page 15-16). Much of this capability is inherited from parent Delivery Hero's shared technology stack rather than built standalone (TLB-001, page 10; TLB-026, page 121, 131-132), and talabat now processes over 235 terabytes of data daily to run it (TLB-002, page 16). This note covers AI as the umbrella capability; see [[Personalization]] and [[Recommendation Systems]] for the customer-facing recommendation mechanics specifically.

## Why It Matters
AI is the only lever in the corpus with a directly quantified, multi-year EBITDA trail: the personalisation/ranking algorithm's estimated contribution rose from USD 14mn+ p.a. (FY2024) to USD 30mn+ p.a. (FY2025) (TLB-001, page 23; TLB-002, page 15), and fraud detection alone is credited with ~USD 6mn/year in savings (TLB-015, page 21). For an Egypt-focused retention strategy, AI matters not just as the personalisation engine but as the infrastructure that makes logistics cost-efficient and ad-targeting sharper — both of which indirectly support the customer experience that keeps subscribers ordering.

## Open Questions
- No document discloses whether talabat's AI/ML models are trained on or tuned separately for the Egyptian market, or whether Egypt currently runs on the same regional/Delivery Hero-inherited models used in the GCC.
- The USD 14mn→30mn+ EBITDA figures and the ~USD 6mn/year fraud-detection savings are reported at the whole-company or regional level; none is broken out by country, so Egypt's specific contribution or benefit from AI investment is unstated.
- The corpus does not disclose AI/ML headcount, R&D spend, or infrastructure located in or dedicated to Egypt specifically (TLB-015's ~440 tech FTEs and 2 R&D centres are company-wide, undated by location).

## Business Implications
- The quantified EBITDA trail (USD 14mn→30mn+) gives the capstone a defensible, cited basis for arguing AI-driven personalisation has a proven, scalable financial return — but any Egypt-specific dollar projection must be presented as an extrapolation, not a disclosed fact.
- Because AI-driven logistics and ad-targeting both feed indirectly into customer experience, a retention strategy can reasonably argue for continued investment in these "invisible" AI layers, not just customer-facing recommendation features.
- The absence of Egypt-specific AI deployment data is itself a strategic finding: the capstone can recommend that talabat Egypt's next data investment be instrumenting and disclosing country-level AI impact, to close this exact evidence gap.
