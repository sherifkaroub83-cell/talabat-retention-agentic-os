# Customer Journey

## Overview
talabat's own investor materials describe an explicit customer journey arc: "Food → Grocery & Retail → talabat pro sign-up" (TLB-013, page 6), reflecting the company's strategy of using multi-vertical adoption as a stepping stone to subscription lock-in. This is reinforced by the AI-personalization mechanic — it takes roughly six orders for talabat's machine-learning models to build a personalized profile, after which customers "benefit from relevant deals, and ultimately drive higher retention and order frequency" (TLB-002, page 15). talabat's own narrative framing of its evolution — "Delivering food to your doorstep" (2016) → "Delivering food and a lot more" (2021) → "It's not just talabat app, it's your app" (2025) (TLB-016, page 7) — describes the company's intended trajectory for how a customer's relationship with the platform is meant to deepen over time, from single-vertical transactional use toward habitual, multi-vertical, subscribed engagement.

## Why It Matters
The customer journey concept gives the capstone a structural, source-grounded sequence (order frequency → multi-vertical adoption → personalization threshold → subscription) to map an AI-driven retention intervention onto specific journey stages, rather than treating retention as a single undifferentiated push. Multi-vertical customers already show materially higher order frequency and retention than food-only customers (TLB-002, pages 7–8, 12), and 2024's GMV growth was explicitly decomposed into user-base expansion and an 8% order-frequency uplift (TLB-001, page 26) — both are journey-stage metrics an Egypt strategy can target directly.

## Open Questions
- The vault does not disclose a formal, quantified journey/funnel model (e.g., what percentage of food-only customers progress to multi-vertical, or from multi-vertical to talabat pro, within what time frame) — the "Food → Grocery & Retail → talabat pro" sequence is stated qualitatively, not as a measured conversion funnel.
- No Egypt-specific journey or conversion data exists in the corpus; talabat pro's Egypt launch (February 2025) is too recent for the corpus's cohort-based journey metrics to have matured there.
- It is unclear from the vault whether the journey sequence is causal (grocery adoption drives subscription) or merely correlational (customers who adopt both are simply more engaged overall).

## Business Implications
- The disclosed journey sequence gives the capstone a ready-made, source-grounded framework for staging an Egypt AI-retention intervention: first drive multi-vertical (grocery/retail) adoption among food-only Egypt customers, then convert that increased engagement into talabat pro subscription.
- The ~6-order personalization threshold is a concrete, actionable target for an AI-driven Egypt strategy — e.g., front-loading personalized offers/incentives in customers' first six orders to accelerate the point at which retention mechanics engage.
- Because Egypt-specific journey-stage data doesn't yet exist, the capstone's Egypt journey map will necessarily be an extrapolation from group-wide patterns and should be presented as such rather than as an Egypt-verified funnel.
