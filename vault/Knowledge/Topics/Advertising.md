# Advertising

## Overview
Advertising (AdTech) is talabat's Partner-facing monetisation layer — Sponsored Listings, Keyword Ads, Deal Targeting, banner/premium placements, and the GEMs (Guaranteed Engagement Metrics) product — sold to restaurants and shops to buy visibility and customer acquisition on the marketplace (TLB-001, page 13; TLB-015, page 49; TLB-026, page 138). It has grown from USD 84mn (2.1% of GMV) in 2021 to USD 323mn (3.4-3.5% of GMV) in FY2025 (TLB-015, page 26; TLB-002, page 11, 14, 19), with management targeting a ~7% of GMV medium-term benchmark already reached in the UAE for talabat mart specifically (TLB-001, page 21; TLB-002, page 10; TLB-014, page 19).

## Why It Matters
Advertising is powered by the same AI-driven targeting and audience-segmentation tools that drive customer-facing personalisation, and one AdTech product — GEMs — is explicitly designed to help Partners with "low/declining customer acquisition or retention rates" win back customers, charging per new or won-back customer acquired (TLB-026, page 138). This makes advertising not purely a Partner-revenue story but a mechanism with a direct, if Partner-side, retention/win-back function — relevant to an Egypt retention strategy insofar as it shows talabat already has a productised, AI-supported tool for reversing customer attrition at the Partner level.

## Open Questions
- No document discloses Egypt-specific AdTech revenue, penetration (% of GMV), or ROAS — all reported figures are company-wide or UAE-specific (e.g., the 7% of GMV talabat mart milestone is UAE-only).
- The GEMs product's actual effectiveness at winning back churned customers is not quantified anywhere in the corpus — only its charging mechanism and target-Partner profile are described.
- It is unclear whether the ad-personalisation USD 14mn EBITDA estimate (TLB-026, page 132-133) is the same figure as, overlapping with, or additive to the broader USD 14mn→30mn+ personalisation EBITDA estimate discussed in [[AI]] and [[Personalization]] — the corpus does not clarify whether these are one estimate or two.

## Business Implications
- GEMs is an existing, evidenced, AI-supported win-back tool at the Partner level — the retention strategy can reference it as a proof point that talabat already operationalises "churn-risk targeting," and can propose an analogous customer-level (rather than Partner-level) win-back product for Egypt.
- The 7% of GMV benchmark, already reached for talabat mart in the UAE, gives the strategy a validated target ceiling to reference when framing Egypt's AdTech monetisation headroom (Egypt's own figure is undisclosed, per Open Questions).
- Because advertising revenue partly funds Partner-side incentives that in turn fund customer-facing benefits like talabat pro's free delivery, the strategy can frame AdTech growth as an indirect enabler of retention economics, not just a separate revenue stream.
