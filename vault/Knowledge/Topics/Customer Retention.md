# Customer Retention

## Overview
Customer retention is talabat's explicit, named strategic pillar — "turbo-charging customer loyalty" — and is treated as a distinct lever from raw customer acquisition. In talabat's own language, retention means keeping medium- and high-value customers ordering, and doing so primarily through subscription (talabat pro), points-based rewards, embedded FinTech (PostPaid, co-branded card), and AI-timed personalization rather than blanket discounting. By 2026 this has hardened into an explicit strategic objective: "deploy targeted capital to defend core markets and retain high and medium value customers against partial or complete churn to competition" (TLB-002, page 14). The Qatar five-day administrative closure and rapid order-volume recovery in September 2025 is cited by talabat itself as evidence of platform "stickiness" and customer loyalty (TLB-002, page 9).

## Why It Matters
This is the central construct for the capstone: talabat's own disclosures already frame retention as a quantified, ROI-linked lever (e.g., talabat pro's +26–32% retention uplift, +28% frequency uplift) rather than a soft aspiration, which gives the AI-driven Egypt retention strategy a directly comparable, evidenced benchmark to build on. Because talabat pro only launched in Egypt in February 2025, Egypt is the one major market where this retention machinery is newest and least mature — meaning the capstone's recommendation sits exactly where the evidenced retention playbook (subscription, rewards, AI personalization) has the most unexploited room to compound (see [[Egypt]]).

## Open Questions

- The vault does not state whether talabat pro's retention uplift in Egypt specifically differs from the group-wide 26–32% figures reported for markets where the programme has operated longer.

## Business Implications
- talabat's own investor materials already treat subscription and AI-timed personalization as the primary retention levers, which supports framing the capstone's Egypt recommendation around deepening talabat pro adoption and personalization rather than proposing an unproven new mechanism.
- The 2026 "retain high and medium value customers against...churn to competition" objective (TLB-002, page 14) gives the capstone a directly citable, board-level mandate to anchor an Egypt-specific version of the same strategy.
- Because Egypt's retention infrastructure (talabat pro) is barely a year old as of this corpus, the capstone can position Egypt as the market with the largest unrealized upside from applying the already-proven group playbook, while flagging that Egypt-specific uplift data does not yet exist to validate this.
