# Talabat Pro

## Overview
talabat pro is talabat's paid subscription loyalty programme — free delivery above a minimum basket plus exclusive discounts for a monthly or annual fee — launched in the UAE in March 2022 and rolled out market-by-market until it reached all eight operating countries in 2025, with Egypt live from February 2025 and Iraq from September 2025 (TLB-001, page 19; TLB-002, page 14). It has grown from a UAE-only pilot with 8.3% customer penetration in September 2024 (TLB-026, page 30) into the company's single largest disclosed retention lever, with subscribers accounting for roughly half of platform GMV by Q1 2026 (TLB-020, page 8). See [[Talabat_Pro]] for the full source-by-source data.

## Why It Matters
talabat pro is the most directly quantified retention mechanism in the entire corpus: subscribers show a consistently measured order-frequency uplift (20-28% across multiple periods) and, more recently, a directly measured retention uplift (+26% to +32% depending on cohort and period) versus lookalike non-subscribers (TLB-001, page 18; TLB-015, page 78; TLB-019, page 11). Because Egypt only joined the programme in February 2025, it sits at exactly the point in its lifecycle — a large, newly-onboarded user base with a not-yet-mature subscription penetration — where the capstone's AI-driven retention recommendation has the most headroom to compound, per the "future focus" language explicitly naming Egypt (TLB-001, page 21).

## Open Questions
- The corpus's headline adoption and uplift figures (2.1x adoption growth, 28% frequency uplift, 32% retention uplift) are explicitly measured on the six GCC/Jordan markets live before December 2024 and **exclude Egypt and Iraq** (TLB-012, page 17; TLB-013, page 8; TLB-016, page 9) — no Egypt-specific pro adoption rate, frequency uplift, or retention uplift figure appears anywhere in the corpus.
- No figure discloses what share of Egypt's active customers have subscribed to talabat pro since its February 2025 launch, or how quickly Egypt is converging toward the ~49% platform-wide GMV share reported for Q1 2026.
- The Family Plan's >60% retention premium (TLB-018, page 6) is not broken out by country, so it is unclear whether this product is live or performing similarly in Egypt.

## Business Implications
- Egypt's talabat pro programme is roughly 18 months into its life at the point of this analysis (launched Feb 2025) — any recommendation should treat Egypt-specific pro metrics as an evidence gap to be addressed with primary research, not assumed to match GCC uplift rates.
- The programme's own "future focus" language (grow the subscription base in GCC **and Egypt**, TLB-001 page 21) gives direct textual support for a retention strategy that prioritizes accelerating Egypt pro-conversion.
- The Family Plan and ADCB-style bank partnerships are evidenced, transferable retention mechanisms the strategy can propose extending to Egypt, since both are already validated (if not yet Egypt-confirmed) in the corpus.
- Because pro benefits are partner-funded (TLB-026, page 134), any Egypt expansion recommendation should account for the need to secure local Partner buy-in, not treat the subsidy as company-funded.
