# Implementation (Three Horizons)

## Overview
No dedicated Knowledge-layer note existed for implementation sequencing before this one — the
substance lives in `vault/Decisions/Investment_Options_Register.md`/`DEC-009` (which options, in what
order) and `vault/Forecasts/Scenarios_v2.md` (which scenario modules are strong enough to be H1 quick
wins vs. later/monitoring-only), with Section 12 of the Business Plan the place these get assembled
into an actual roadmap.

## The sequencing logic (per `DEC-009`, resolved 2026-07-23)
- **Near-term (H1) priority:** OPT-002 (talabat pro Egypt/Iraq — cheap, fast, reversible pilot),
  OPT-003 (Food-leadership GCC-3 — tightly evidenced, high feasibility)
- **Continue-at-pace:** OPT-001 (dark-store densification — strong evidence, low reversibility, pending
  the margin-drag stage gate), OPT-005 (AI/personalisation — strong EBITDA trail, weakest measurement
  quality)
- **Longer-horizon build:** OPT-004 (advertising monetization gap — requires a commercial capability
  build)
- Scenario-module confidence (`Scenarios_v2.md`) reinforces this: Medium-confidence modules riding
  strong observed trends (talabat pro, multi-vertical adoption) are natural H1 candidates; Low-confidence,
  thinly-evidenced modules (logistics) belong later or as monitoring-only.

## Why It Matters
Section 12 is where `DEC-009`'s funding-sequence recommendation actually becomes a timeline — but per
that same decision, any milestone exhibit showing dollar figures from `ASM-029`–`033` must carry the
mandatory disclosure sentence and stay out of the primary roadmap graphic (see
`vault/Templates/_TEMPLATE-visual-exhibit-standard.md`).
