# Monitoring (KPI Tree)

## Overview
No dedicated Knowledge-layer note existed for monitoring/KPIs before this one — the substance is
`vault/Forecasts/KPI_Tree_v2.md` (50 KPIs across 5 families: Portfolio, Customer & growth, Financial,
Operational, Governance; 30 baseline-exists, 20 newly-instrumented), which Section 13 of the Business
Plan turns into the plan's leading/lagging split, review cadence, and kill/pivot criteria.

## Why It Matters
Two gaps dominate this area and must be stated explicitly, not smoothed over: (1) **the entire
Governance family (7 KPIs, G1-G7) is newly-instrumented** — talabat discloses zero internal
capital-allocation governance mechanics, so any stage-gate percentage, reallocation threshold, or ROI
hurdle rate is this OS's own proposed mechanism, not a description of talabat's actual process (see
[[Capital Allocation and Investment Governance]]); (2) **churn remains the single biggest customer-side
gap** — no churn rate, definition, or cohort analysis exists anywhere in the corpus, Group or Egypt, so
it becomes a metric the programme must *start* measuring from zero.
