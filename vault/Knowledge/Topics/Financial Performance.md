# Financial Performance

## Overview
Financial Performance is the holistic, guidance-versus-actual view of talabat's results across the corpus's timeline (FY2021–Q1 2026): GMV, revenue, and profitability all grew at high double-digit rates through FY2025, capital structure strengthened (net cash of USD 591mn, +83% vs. end-2024, with zero external financial debt, TLB-002 page 23), and the company began paying dividends (USD 421mn recommended for FY2025, a 90% payout of reported net income, TLB-002 page 8). talabat's management has repeatedly issued and then revised GMV/revenue growth guidance intra-year — most notably raising FY2025 GMV guidance from 17–18% to 27–29% cFX after a strong first half (TLB-018, page 10) — and every full-year result in the corpus either met or exceeded its original guidance range. FY2026 guidance steps down to 11–14% GMV growth and 14–17% revenue growth, reflecting a larger base, more competition, macro headwinds, and InstaShop dilution (TLB-019, page 18).

## Why It Matters
The consistent guidance-beat pattern and strengthening balance sheet establish that talabat has both the financial credibility and the capital capacity to fund a retention-focused AI investment program — the FY2026 guidance explicitly earmarks over USD 100mn in combined opex/capex toward talabat mart and talabat pro (TLB-002, page 10; TLB-020, page 12), i.e., retention infrastructure is already a stated capital priority at the group level, which the capstone's Egypt-specific recommendation can build on rather than have to justify from scratch.

## Open Questions
- The corpus does not explain why FY2025 GMV guidance was revised twice within the year (17–18% → 27–29% → 28% actual) beyond citing a "strong first half" — the underlying demand drivers (Egypt vs. GCC, organic vs. InstaShop, seasonality) are not decomposed.
- TLB-014 flags that from 2026 talabat will report IFRS revenue only, discontinuing "Management Revenue" — the vault does not indicate how prior-period guidance and consensus (built on Management Revenue) will be restated or compared going forward.
- Consensus dividend and free-cash-flow forecasts beyond FY2025 actuals exist only in unconsolidated `_raw` files (TLB-027/TLB-028), not in the citable Facts layer — this note cannot state a forward capital-returns trajectory with a proper citation.

## Business Implications
- talabat's demonstrated pattern of meeting or beating its own guidance gives the business plan's financial projections for an Egypt retention initiative a credible peer benchmark for conservatism/ambition calibration.
- The explicit FY2026 capital allocation toward talabat pro and talabat mart (>USD 100mn) is direct evidence that group management already treats retention-adjacent investment as a funded strategic priority — the capstone's Egypt recommendation can be framed as a natural extension of an existing capital commitment, not a new ask.
- The step-down in FY2026 growth guidance (attributed partly to "a larger base" and "more competitive environment") strengthens the case that retention — not just acquisition — needs to be a bigger share of growth strategy going forward, which is the core premise of the capstone.
