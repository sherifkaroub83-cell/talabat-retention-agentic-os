# Corporate Structure

## Overview
Talabat Holding PLC is the DFM-listed entity (ADGM-incorporated, Registration Number 20827), sitting beneath **Delivery Hero SE** (Frankfurt-listed ultimate parent) via an intermediate holding chain of **Delivery Hero MENA Holding GmbH** (the pre-IPO sole shareholder and IPO Selling Shareholder) and **Delivery Hero FZ-LLC** (the MENA regional operating/holding entity) ([[Companies]]). Beneath the listed entity sits a dense web of country-level operating subsidiaries — including two Egypt-incorporated entities, **Delivery Hero Egypt SAE** and **Delivery Hero Dmart Egypt LLC** — plus the recently-acquired InstaShop group of companies and an equity-accounted UAE logistics associate, Zone Elite Investment LLC ([[Companies]]). Governance sits with a five-to-six-member Board chaired by Pieter-Jan Vandepitte (also Delivery Hero SE's COO) and an executive management team led, at different points in the corpus, by two different CEOs — Tomaso Rodriguez through mid/late-2025 and Toon Gyssels from early 2026 ([[Executives]]).

## Why It Matters
The corporate structure matters to an Egypt retention strategy because it shows where authority actually sits: Talabat Holding PLC's board is dominated by Delivery Hero SE officers (the Chairperson, Vice-Chairperson, and a Non-Executive Director are simultaneously DH SE's COO, General Counsel, and interim CFO), and Egypt operates through two dedicated legal entities under services agreements with Delivery Hero rather than as a standalone reporting unit ([[Companies]], [[Executives]], TLB-026). Any AI-driven retention investment case for Egypt will need to be justified through this same parent-company-influenced governance structure and will compete for capital against seven other country operations and Delivery Hero SE's broader group priorities.

## Open Questions
- The corpus shows a CEO transition from Tomaso Rodriguez to Toon Gyssels between Aug 2025 (TLB-018) and Feb 2026 (TLB-009), and a board seat change from Muhammad Hussain Ghati Al Jbori to Abdul Wahab Al-Halabi between TLB-004/TLB-026 and TLB-008 — no document in the corpus narrates either change. Both are flagged as genuinely open, not resolved by any source read for this note.
- TLB-008 lists Rodriguez as "Executive Director" the same reporting period Gyssels is named CEO elsewhere, suggesting an internal move rather than a departure — but no source confirms this explicitly.
- The vault does not show whether Egypt's two legal entities (Delivery Hero Egypt SAE, Delivery Hero Dmart Egypt LLC) have any local board representation, advisory committee, or decision rights distinct from the Talabat Holding PLC board — i.e., whether an Egypt retention initiative would be approved locally or only at group level.
- No source explains the commercial logic or valuation basis behind moving Delivery Hero Egypt SAE into the "Extended Perimeter Group" as part of the pre-IPO reorganisation (TLB-026 states the fact of the transfer but not the rationale).

## Business Implications
- Because Talabat Holding PLC's board overlaps heavily with Delivery Hero SE's own C-suite, an Egypt retention business case will likely need to satisfy group-level (not just country-level) investment criteria.
- Egypt's retention strategy would be executed through Delivery Hero Egypt SAE and Delivery Hero Dmart Egypt LLC — the capstone's implementation section should anchor any org-design or accountability recommendations to these two named legal entities rather than an abstract "Egypt business unit."
- The unexplained CEO and board changes are a governance-stability caveat worth flagging in the business plan's risk section: strategic continuity for a multi-year Egypt retention program depends on leadership that has recently turned over without public explanation.
