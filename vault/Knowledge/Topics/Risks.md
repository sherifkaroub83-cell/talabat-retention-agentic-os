# Risks (2026 Investment Programme)

## Overview
No dedicated, Group-wide, capital-allocation-framed risk synthesis existed at the Knowledge layer
before this note — risk content instead lived in two pre-pivot places: `Strategic/Strategic Risks.md`
(Egypt-retention-framed, still evidence-reusable per the 2026-07-23 pivot's own classification) and
`Business_Plan_Drafts_v2/Section_10_Risk_Analysis.md` (not yet drafted). This note is a thin bridge
between them, not a replacement for either.

## Reusable evidence (from `Strategic Risks.md`, Group-level facts unaffected by the pivot)
- FX/currency exposure (Egypt-specific instance, generalizable framing)
- The disclosed FY2026 Adjusted EBITDA margin step-down (6.0% → 4.4-4.8% guided, already 4.8% actual
  Q1 2026) — a real, evidenced financial risk with a documented trail
- Competitive-pressure risk: the -4% y/y non-high-value M1 retention decline (UAE/Kuwait/Qatar) — see
  [[Food Leadership]]
- Governance/disclosure risk: talabat discloses almost no internal capital-allocation approval
  mechanics — see [[Capital Allocation and Investment Governance]]

## New risk category the pivot introduces
- **False-precision risk on this OS's own constructed allocation ranges** — the specific failure mode
  `DEC-008` and `DEC-009` were each escalated to guard against (an illustrative, Low-confidence
  construction being read as a disclosed commitment). Section 10 should name this explicitly as an
  organizational/governance risk of the plan's own recommendation process, not just an evidence gap.

## Why It Matters
Section 10 (Risk Analysis) needs a MECE risk taxonomy — technical/market/financial/organizational/
regulatory — built fresh for the capital-allocation problem, reusing the Group-level facts above but
not importing `Strategic Risks.md`'s Egypt-retention risk *prioritization* wholesale.
