# Orders

## Overview
Orders is talabat's core unit-of-activity metric — the corpus tracks average order frequency per active customer (rising from 6.0x in Sept 2023 to 6.7x in Dec 2024, and holding at 6.7x with a stated 5% YoY frequency increase through 2025), total platform order volume (585 million orders placed in 2025), and operational quality metrics like delivery time (<30 minutes) and order success rate (~99%) (TLB-002, page 12; TLB-012, page 8; TLB-026, page 27). The most consequential order-level split in the corpus is by customer type: multi-vertical (Food + Groceries & Retail) customers order 12.8–13.0 times per month versus 3.8 times per month for food-only customers — a roughly 3.4x frequency multiple that recurs across several documents (TLB-013, page 6; TLB-015, page 18; TLB-026, page 122).

## Why It Matters
Order frequency is the single most direct, quantified link between retention mechanics and financial outcomes in the entire corpus: talabat pro subscribers show a 28% order-frequency uplift, rewards redeemers show >15%, and PostPaid adopters show 14% (all from TLB-001), and 2024's headline GMV growth is explicitly decomposed into MAU expansion plus an 8% order-frequency uplift. For an AI-driven retention strategy, Orders is the metric where "retention" stops being an abstract concept and becomes a directly measurable, GMV-linked outcome — and the multi-vertical frequency multiple (3.4x) gives a concrete, evidenced target for what a successful Egypt retention/cross-sell strategy should aim to replicate.

## Open Questions
- The corpus gives no Egypt-specific order-frequency figure — all frequency data is either group-wide or split only by GCC/non-GCC, so Egypt's current order frequency relative to the 6.7x group average or the 1.28x GCC per-capita benchmark cannot be cited directly.
- No document reports a true customer-level churn or repeat-order-rate metric; order frequency is the closest available proxy, but it measures intensity among active customers, not retention/attrition of the customer base itself (see [[Customer Retention]] and [[Customer Churn]] for the related gap).
- The multi-vertical vs. food-only frequency comparison is a snapshot (as of July 2024 / September 2024 depending on source) rather than a trend — the vault does not show whether this frequency gap is widening or narrowing over time.

## Business Implications
- The 3.4x order-frequency multiple for multi-vertical customers is the strongest single evidenced argument in the corpus for an Egypt retention strategy that actively cross-sells food customers into groceries/G&R, rather than treating retention and category expansion as separate workstreams.
- Because talabat pro's order-frequency uplift (28%) is already proven at group level and talabat pro only launched in Egypt in February 2025, an AI-personalized push to accelerate Egypt talabat pro adoption has a directly transferable, already-quantified benchmark to model expected impact against.
- The absence of any Egypt-specific frequency data is itself a finding the business plan should state plainly: recommendations for Egypt order-frequency targets will need to be modeled/estimated from group and non-GCC proxies, clearly labeled as such per the project's data-transparency standard.
