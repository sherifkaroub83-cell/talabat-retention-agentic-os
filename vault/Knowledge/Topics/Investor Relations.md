# Investor Relations

## Overview
talabat's investor relations function operates on a recognisable public-company cadence: annual reports (TLB-001, TLB-002), quarterly preliminary/audited financial statements (TLB-003 through TLB-008), quarterly results press releases and presentations (e.g. TLB-009, TLB-018, TLB-019, TLB-020), a Capital Markets Day (TLB-015), and ad hoc DFM regulatory notices such as the 2026 share buyback disclosure (TLB-021). The corpus's own composition — 29 primary documents spanning press releases, audited financials, and presentations, plus two independent sell-side consensus-estimate compilations (TLB-027, TLB-028) — is itself evidence of this IR cadence rather than a separate, single-document disclosure. **Shadi Salman** (also appearing as "Shady Hamid" in IPO press-release PDF metadata, per [[Executives]]) recurs as the Head of Investor Relations / IR contact/presenter across earnings materials, and **Teneo** served as external financial communications advisor for the IPO specifically (TLB-022–TLB-026).

## Why It Matters
Investor relations is the mechanism through which talabat's retention-relevant initiatives (talabat pro, talabat Rewards, AI-driven personalisation) get quantified and made public in the first place — nearly every retention metric available in this vault (order-frequency uplift, subscriber CAGR, retention-lift-versus-lookalike-customers) exists because it was disclosed to investors, not because it was published as an internal operations report. For the capstone, this means the IR cadence is a *source of evidence*, not just a corporate function: understanding how and when talabat reports (quarterly, at Capital Markets Days, in the IOM) tells the analyst where to look for updated Egypt-relevant retention data in future disclosures.

## Business Implications
- Because retention metrics (talabat pro uplift, Rewards adoption, retention-vs-lookalike figures) are IR-disclosed rather than internally sourced, the capstone's data should be understood as investor-grade, top-line evidence — sufficient for strategic framing but not a substitute for the operational, customer-level data flagged as absent in [[Customer Churn]] and elsewhere.
- The quarterly/annual IR cadence gives the capstone a natural cadence to recommend for tracking Egypt retention KPIs post-implementation (e.g., aligning an Egypt retention dashboard to the same reporting periods as group results).
- Because Egypt has never been given IR-level segment disclosure beyond the single FY2023 "6%/1%" data point, the business plan should explicitly flag that measuring the success of an Egypt retention strategy will require internal (not IR-sourced) reporting infrastructure — the group's existing IR outputs will not surface it.
