# Restaurants

## Overview
Restaurants are the largest single Partner category in talabat's three-sided marketplace, numbering 58,700+ as of December 2024 out of 68,000+ total Active Partners, alongside 10,000+ Local Shops offering 700,000+ SKUs ([[Marketplace|Facts/Marketplace]], TLB-001, page 4, page 17). The corpus tiers restaurants by GMV contribution: as of July 2024, 4,000+ "global leading" restaurants generated 15% of GMV, 17,000+ "regional favourites" generated 44%, and 34,000+ "boutique" restaurants generated 17%, with 9,000+ Grocery & Retail branches contributing the remaining 24% (TLB-015, page 40, 46). talabat operates the restaurant relationship as an agent, earning commission fees rather than acting as principal (TLB-004, page 24).

## Why It Matters
Restaurant selection breadth and quality are a direct driver of the customer-side flywheel — more restaurant choice attracts and retains customers — making the health of this Partner base a precondition for any AI-driven retention strategy in Egypt. The revenue-tiering data (a small number of "global leading" restaurants driving disproportionate GMV) also suggests that Partner-side retention/curation could be as strategically relevant as customer-side retention, though the corpus's evidence here is aggregate/platform-wide rather than Egypt-specific.

## Open Questions
- No Egypt-specific restaurant count, tiering, or GMV contribution is disclosed anywhere in the corpus — all restaurant figures are reported at the platform-wide (8-country) level.
- The corpus does not evidence restaurant-side churn, onboarding, or retention metrics (e.g., how many restaurants leave the platform annually), only net Active Partner growth.
- No fact links restaurant tiering/GMV concentration to customer retention outcomes directly (e.g., whether customers who order from "global leading" restaurants exhibit different retention behavior).

## Business Implications
- Because restaurant-level and Egypt-specific data are both absent, any Egypt-specific restaurant-side recommendation in the business plan must be flagged as inference from platform-wide patterns, not direct evidence, per the corpus's standing citation discipline.
- The GMV concentration in a relatively small "global leading" and "regional favourites" tier (59% of GMV from ~21,000 restaurants) suggests that AI-driven personalization/recommendation efforts may have outsized retention impact if they optimize discovery toward these high-performing Partners — a hypothesis for the business plan to test rather than a proven finding.
- The agent/commission accounting model (talabat does not own restaurant inventory) means any Partner-side retention investment (e.g., subsidies, marketing support) has different margin implications than customer-side retention spend, relevant when building the value-driver-tree financials.
