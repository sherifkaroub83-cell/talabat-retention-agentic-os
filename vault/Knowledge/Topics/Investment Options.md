# Investment Options

## Overview
Five evidence-grounded candidate sub-initiatives exist within the disclosed USD175mn 2026 envelope —
`OPT-001` through `OPT-005`, registered in `vault/Decisions/Investment_Options_Register.md` and each
with its own full record under `vault/Decisions/Investment_Options/`. They are not disclosed by talabat
at this granularity (the corpus discloses only the two top-level bucket totals, ~USD120mn Everyday App
and ~USD55mn Food-leadership) — every option is this OS's own evidence-grounded construction, compared
in `DEC-009` (status: `approved`, 2026-07-23, middle path).

| ID | Name | Bucket | Geography |
|---|---|---|---|
| OPT-001 | talabat mart dark-store densification | Everyday App | Group |
| OPT-002 | talabat pro acceleration (Egypt, Iraq) | Everyday App (opex) | inferred-applicability |
| OPT-003 | Food-leadership CVP/partner-retention | Food-leadership | UAE, Kuwait, Qatar |
| OPT-004 | Advertising monetization gap closure | Enabling capability | Group (UAE benchmark) |
| OPT-005 | AI/personalisation capability scaling | Enabling capability | Group |

## Why It Matters
This is the level at which the Business Plan's actual recommendation lives — Sections 9 and 12 operate
at this option level, not the two-bucket level, since the two buckets themselves are already
talabat-decided. `DEC-009` requires the plan to commit to a recommended funding sequence across these
five (see the Decision record for the 11-criterion comparison and tiering), while keeping every
constructed dollar range out of the headline financial case per the same decision's mandatory
disclosure rule.
