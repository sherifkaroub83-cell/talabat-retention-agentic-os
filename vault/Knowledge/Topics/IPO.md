# IPO

## Overview
Talabat Holding plc listed on the Dubai Financial Market (DFM) in November–December 2024 through a four-stage public process documented end-to-end in the corpus: an "intention to float" announcement (11 Nov 2024), an offer-price-range/subscription-opening announcement (19 Nov 2024, AED 1.50–1.60/share), a mid-bookbuild offering-size increase (27 Nov 2024, 15%→20% of issued capital) driven by anchor-investor demand, and final pricing at the top of the range (29 Nov 2024, AED 1.60/share), raising gross proceeds of c. AED 7.5 billion (c. USD 2.0 billion) — publicly billed as "the largest global technology IPO in 2024 to date" and the first technology-sector IPO on DFM (TLB-023, TLB-022, TLB-024, TLB-025). The entire offering was a **secondary sale**: all shares were sold by sole shareholder Delivery Hero MENA Holding GmbH, meaning the IPO monetized Delivery Hero's existing stake rather than raising new primary capital for Talabat itself (TLB-022, page 2). A 17-bank global syndicate led the deal, and the transaction was backed by a full International Offering Memorandum (IOM) disclosing audited FY2021–FY2023 financials, risk factors, and an independent OC&C/Redseer industry report (TLB-026).

## Why It Matters
The IPO is the origin event for talabat's current governance, disclosure, and capital-allocation regime, and its associated documents (especially the IOM, TLB-026) are the single richest source in the corpus for retention-relevant strategic commitments — the IPO prospectus is where talabat first quantified talabat pro's order-frequency uplift and explicitly stated that talabat pro was **not yet available in Egypt** as of the filing date, a direct data point for sequencing an Egypt retention roadmap (TLB-026, page 133). The IPO's "About talabat" boilerplate and CEO commentary also repeatedly named "loyalty programmes" and "FinTech" as explicit growth-strategy pillars to public investors, giving the capstone's retention recommendation a pre-existing, investor-facing strategic mandate to build on.

## Open Questions
- No document in the corpus states what Talabat Holding PLC itself received from the IPO, since it was structured entirely as a secondary sale by Delivery Hero MENA Holding GmbH — the vault does not clarify whether any indirect capital benefit (e.g., balance-sheet strengthening, debt paydown) accrued to the operating company.
- The corpus does not disclose exactly when talabat pro launched in Egypt after the IOM's Nov 2024 "not available in Egypt" disclosure, or what performance it has since achieved there — this is a direct gap for the capstone's retention roadmap.
- No source in the corpus explains the strategic rationale for choosing DFM over another regional or international exchange.

## Business Implications
- The IOM's explicit statement that talabat pro excluded Egypt at IPO (Nov 2024) is strong evidence that Egypt's subscription/loyalty program is comparatively young relative to GCC markets — the retention strategy should treat Egypt as a "catch-up" market rather than assume GCC-level program maturity.
- The IPO's public "loyalty programmes" growth pillar gives the AI-driven retention recommendation an existing, board-endorsed strategic hook rather than requiring a net-new mandate.
- Because Egypt is not broken out financially anywhere in the IPO disclosures beyond the single "6% of revenue / 1% of EBITDA" data point, any retention business case for Egypt will need to build its own financial model from the Non-GCC segment and clearly label the estimation logic (see [[Segment Reporting]]), consistent with the project's standing instruction on estimating Egypt-only figures.
