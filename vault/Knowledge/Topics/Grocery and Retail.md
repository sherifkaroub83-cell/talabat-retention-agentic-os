# Grocery and Retail

## Overview
Groceries and Retail (G&R) is talabat's second major vertical alongside Food, comprising the
commission-based Local Shops marketplace and the principal-model talabat mart (tMart) dark-store
network, plus InstaShop post-acquisition (TLB-001, page 17; TLB-002, page 12; see [[Delivery_Models]]).
Commercially, G&R is talabat's fastest-growing but structurally lower-margin vertical: G&R GMV grew 47%
y/y to USD 2.77 billion in FY2025 (32% of total GMV in Q4'25) versus Food's 20% y/y growth to USD 6.65
billion (TLB-002, page 18; TLB-019, page 14), while G&R's product-mix shift is explicitly flagged as a
(0.5%)-(0.7%) drag on Adjusted EBITDA margin in the 2026 guidance bridge, reflecting a lower
commission/take-rate on grocery than food (TLB-014, page 6, page 8). This note covers G&R's commercial
angle — growth, margin, competitive dynamics, and its role in the 2026 investment programme; see
[[Quick Commerce]] for the operational/dark-store execution detail (store counts, fulfilment speed,
InstaShop's specific unit economics) that this note deliberately does not repeat.

## Why It Matters
G&R is the largest single named line item within the ~USD 120 million Everyday App component of the
2026 investment programme — talabat mart scaling alone accounts for roughly USD 45 million of capex
(TLB-014, page 19; TLB-020, page 12) — making it directly load-bearing for the capital-allocation
decision this plan answers. Because G&R growth is disclosed as margin-dilutive even as it drives
headline GMV growth, any allocation recommendation that favours further G&R investment must explicitly
account for this trade-off rather than treating G&R GMV growth as equivalent in value to Food GMV
growth.

## Open Questions
- No source discloses G&R gross margin or take rate broken out from tMart specifically versus Local Shops specifically — the 5.7-5.9% gross-profit-as-%-of-GMV figures in TLB-026 are FY2023, IPO-era, and not refreshed for FY2025.
- No country-level G&R GMV, margin, or penetration figure exists anywhere in the corpus, including for Egypt.
- The corpus states grocery TAC penetration is "low single-digit" (3%, TLB-014, page 5) but does not disclose a target penetration rate or timeline the 2026 investment is meant to achieve.
- It is not stated whether the disclosed (0.5%)-(0.7%) EBITDA margin drag from G&R product-mix shift is expected to persist, narrow, or reverse as G&R scales — the corpus gives only the current-year bridge, not a multi-year trajectory.

## Business Implications
- Any recommendation to increase G&R/talabat mart investment should explicitly quantify the margin trade-off already disclosed by talabat itself (a real EBITDA drag, not a hypothetical one), rather than presenting G&R growth and Food growth as interchangeable GMV levers.
- The USD 150bn+ TAM against ~3% current adoption (TLB-014, page 19; TLB-020, page 16) is talabat's own evidenced justification for continued G&R capital allocation despite the margin drag — a legitimate growth-versus-margin trade-off the plan should present explicitly rather than resolve silently in either direction.
- Because G&R data is disclosed only at Group level, an Egypt-specific G&R allocation recommendation cannot be grounded in disclosed Egypt G&R evidence and should be flagged as a staged, evidence-light proposal if included.
- G&R's role as the vertical that converts food-only customers into higher-frequency, higher-retention multi-vertical customers (see [[Multi-Verticality]]) means its value to the plan's allocation decision should be assessed on its full retention/LTV contribution, not GMV growth or margin in isolation.
