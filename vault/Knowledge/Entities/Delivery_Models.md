# Delivery Models — Consolidated Entity List

Deduplicated from all 29 documents' raw facts fragments. Four distinct models recur consistently across the corpus.

## 1. Marketplace (agent model, commission-based)

Third-party restaurants and "Local Shops" list on the talabat platform; talabat earns commission fees as agent, not principal. This is the foundational model across Food and G&R verticals. (Nearly all documents)

## 2. Own-delivery / logistics (principal model, delivery fees)

talabat-managed riders plus third-party logistics (3PL) providers — per the IPO Offering Memorandum (TLB-026), **approximately 95% of riders operate via a 3PL or freelance model** rather than direct employment, flagged as an operational risk (limited control over labour compliance/service quality). talabat earns delivery fees as principal. (TLB-001, 004, 008, 012–020, 023, 026)

## 3. Quick commerce / dark stores (principal model, GMV-based)

talabat mart / tMarts — talabat-owned delivery-only dark stores targeting <30-minute grocery delivery; ~130 locations across MENA as of end-2024 (TLB-001). InstaShop operates a related hyper-local grocery-delivery model (own-fleet: pickers, cars, bikes, walkers) in UAE and Egypt. (TLB-001–003, 008–020, 023, 026)

## 4. Cloud kitchens

"Kitchens" / "talabat Kitchens" — delivery-only restaurant kitchen space product, a smaller but recurring model across the corpus. (TLB-001, 012, 013, 016, 018, 026)

## Overlay: subscription and advertising (not delivery models themselves, but monetization layers across all four)

- **Subscription** (talabat pro) — monetizes retention/frequency across all four delivery models.
- **Advertising / AdTech** — sponsored listings and ad placements monetize marketplace and quick-commerce inventory alike.

## Documents with no delivery-model content

TLB-021 (DFM buyback notice — pure capital-markets disclosure), TLB-027, TLB-028 (consensus reports — not applicable).
