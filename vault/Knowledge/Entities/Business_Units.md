# Business Units — Consolidated Entity List

Deduplicated from all 29 documents' raw facts fragments. Two distinct groupings recur across the corpus: **reportable financial segments** (geographic) and **business verticals** (product-line).

## Reportable financial segments (geographic)

- **GCC segment** — UAE, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain, Oman. The dominant segment (~85% of GMV in FY2024, per TLB-001). Consistently reported across all financial statements (TLB-003–011).
- **Non-GCC / "other segments"** — historically Jordan, Iraq, **and Egypt**, bundled together with no Egypt-specific breakout (TLB-001, 003–007, 009, 011, 015, 023, 026, 029).
- **Egypt segment** — new **standalone reportable segment** starting with the FY2025 audited financials (TLB-008) and continuing through TLB-002 (Annual Report 2025) and TLB-010 (Q1 2026 financials), after Egypt independently met IFRS 8's quantitative thresholds. Non-GCC now refers only to the residual Jordan + Iraq bucket in these later documents. See `vault/Knowledge/Entities/Countries.md` for the disclosed Egypt figures.

## Business verticals (product-line)

- **Food Vertical** — the core restaurant food-delivery marketplace.
- **Groceries and Retail (G&R) Vertical** — encompasses Local Shops (third-party marketplace) and tMarts/talabat mart (first-party dark stores); includes InstaShop post-acquisition.
- **AdTech** — advertising/sponsored-listing revenue line, reported as its own business unit across most investor-facing documents.
- **FinTech** — talabat PostPaid, talabat Pay, and related payment products, named as a distinct unit in TLB-001, 015, 018, 026.
