## The 8 operating markets

Stated explicitly and consistently across the corpus (e.g. TLB-001, TLB-002, TLB-009, TLB-012–016, TLB-019, TLB-022–026): **United Arab Emirates, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain, Oman, Jordan, Iraq, Egypt.**

Per the talabat Annual Report 2025 (TLB-002): these eight countries have a combined population of over 192 million and an addressable population of approximately 75 million.

### Egypt — reporting treatment (important, and changed over the corpus's time span)

- In most of the corpus (TLB-001, TLB-003–007, TLB-009, TLB-011, TLB-015, TLB-023, TLB-026, TLB-029), Egypt has **no standalone financial disclosure** — it is folded into the "non-GCC" segment together with Jordan and Iraq. The IPO Offering Memorandum (TLB-026) gives the only pre-2025 Egypt-specific financial figure found in the corpus: **Egypt was 6% of 2023 revenue but only 1% of 2023 Adjusted EBITDA.**
- Starting with the **FY2025 Annual Report (TLB-002)** and the **Q4/FY2025 audited financials (TLB-008)**, Egypt independently met the IFRS 8 quantitative thresholds and became its **own standalone reportable segment**, distinct from GCC and the residual Non-GCC (Jordan, Iraq) bucket. Disclosed FY2025 Egypt figures: external revenue USD 509.9m, gross profit USD 125.0m, net profit USD 44.4m — making Egypt the Group's **3rd-largest revenue country after UAE and Kuwait**.
- The **Q1 2026 financial statements (TLB-010)** carry this forward with quarterly Egypt segment data: Q1 2026 segment revenue USD 145.3m (external revenue USD 139.9m, profit before tax USD 9.4m) vs. restated Q1 2025 USD 80.3m (external USD 77.4m, profit before tax USD 0.5m) — ~81% YoY growth and a swing from near-breakeven to solidly profitable.
- The **Q4/FY2025 results presentation (TLB-019)** states explicitly: "Egypt is now our third largest market by GMV, after UAE and Kuwait, and our largest user base."
