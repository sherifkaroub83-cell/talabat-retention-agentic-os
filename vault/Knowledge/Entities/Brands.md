# Brands — Consolidated Entity List

Deduplicated from all 29 documents' raw facts fragments.

- **talabat** — the core brand/marketplace app. Appears in nearly every document in the corpus.
- **talabat pro** — subscription loyalty programme (free delivery + discounts for a monthly/annual fee). Launched UAE, March 2022; launched Egypt, February 2025. (TLB-001, 002, 009, 011, 012, 013, 014, 015, 016, 018, 019, 020, 023, 026)
- **talabat rewards / talabat Rewards** — points-based loyalty programme (distinct from talabat pro). (TLB-001, 015, 023, 026)
- **talabat mart / tMart(s)** — quick-commerce dark-store grocery format. (TLB-001, 002, 009, 011, 012, 013, 014, 015, 016, 018, 019, 020, 023, 026)
- **talabat DineOut Deals** — restaurant dine-out discovery/deals product. (TLB-001, 012, 013, 015, 016, 018, 026)
- **talabat PostPaid / talabat Postpaid** — buy-now-pay-later / deferred payment product (FinTech). (TLB-001, 012, 013, 015, 018, 023, 026)
- **talabat Pay** — closed-loop wallet product. (TLB-001, 026)
- **talabat Kitchens / Talabat Kitchen** — cloud-kitchen product. (TLB-001, 012, 013, 016, 018, 026)
- **Family Plan** — talabat pro household/family-sharing tier, introduced by TLB-018 (Q2 2025) and continuing through TLB-020 (Q1 2026).
- **"Everyday App"** — strategic positioning/investment-programme framing (not a formal sub-brand) for talabat's FY2026 USD 100m+/USD 120m investment push into daily-use habits (talabat pro, talabat mart). (TLB-011, TLB-014, TLB-020)
- **InstaShop / instashop** — acquired grocery/retail delivery brand (UAE and Egypt; also historically Bahrain, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Oman, Lebanon, Greece), acquired for USD 31.9m (subsequent event in FY2024 financials). (TLB-004, 005, 006, 007, 008, 012, 013, 014, 015, 017, 018, 020, 026)
- **Otlob** — talabat's original Egypt brand, since rebranded to "talabat" in Egypt. (TLB-001, 012, 013, 015, 023, 026)
- **Carriage** — logistics/delivery brand acquired in Kuwait, Bahrain, and UAE. (TLB-001, 015)
- **iFood.jo** and **foodonclick** — Jordan-market brands under the Group. (TLB-012, 013, 015, 026)
- **Zomato** (UAE assets) — restaurant-contract/food-delivery assets acquired by talabat in the UAE via strategic alliance (not a talabat-owned brand; named as an acquired-asset counterparty). (TLB-015, TLB-023)
- **talabat ADCB Credit Card** — co-branded credit card with Abu Dhabi Commercial Bank (ADCB), bundling a tailored talabat pro subscription. (TLB-001, 012, 013, 015, 018, 026)
- **Everyday Roastery** — brand referenced in TLB-026 (International Offering Memorandum) alongside the other product brands.
- **Guru** (internal employee learning platform) and **tcamp** (rider training platform) — internal/operational brands, not customer-facing. (TLB-001, TLB-026)
