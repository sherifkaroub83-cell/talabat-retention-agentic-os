# Products — Consolidated Entity List

Deduplicated from all 29 documents' raw facts fragments.

- **talabat pro** — subscription loyalty programme; free delivery (above a minimum basket) plus discounts for a monthly/annual fee. The corpus's central retention product. (nearly all documents)
- **talabat rewards** — points-based loyalty programme (customers who first redeem points show a >15% order-frequency increase within 30 days, per TLB-001).
- **talabat mart / tMart** — quick-commerce dark-store grocery format (<30 minute delivery).
- **InstaShop app** — separately-branded grocery/retail delivery app (UAE and Egypt primarily); includes features like a "Previously purchased" filter and substitution/replacement flow (TLB-017).
- **talabat DineOut Deals** — restaurant dine-out discovery/deals product.
- **talabat PostPaid** — buy-now-pay-later/deferred-payment FinTech product; associated with a 14% order-frequency increase post-adoption (TLB-001).
- **talabat Pay** — closed-loop payment wallet.
- **talabat Kitchens** — cloud-kitchen product/service.
- **talabat ADCB co-branded credit card** — cashback/free-delivery card partnership with Abu Dhabi Commercial Bank, bundling a tailored talabat pro subscription.
- **Family Plan** — household-sharing tier of talabat pro, introduced ~Q2 2025 (TLB-018) and continuing through Q1 2026 (TLB-020).
- **AdTech product suite** — Sponsored Listings, Keyword Ads, Deal Targeting, banner ads, premium placements, "GEMs" (per TLB-015, TLB-026).
- **Local Shops marketplace** — third-party local-retailer marketplace product (distinct from the dark-store tMart model).
- **Liquidity Provider (LP) mandate** and **Share Buyback Programme (SBB)** — capital-markets products/programmes, not customer-facing (TLB-010, 014, 020, 021).
- **talabat Partner Portal / Partner App** — merchant-facing tools for restaurant/shop partners.
