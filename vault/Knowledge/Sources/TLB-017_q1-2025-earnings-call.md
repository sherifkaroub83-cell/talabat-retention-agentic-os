# TLB-017 — talabat Q1 2025 Earnings Call Presentation

## Title
Q1 2025 Results — "The Leading On-Demand Delivery Platform in MENA"

## Citation
TLB-017 — talabat Holding plc, "Q1 2025 Results," 12 May 2025, Input_Data/04_Earnings_Call_Presentations/20250512_talabat-Q125-earnings-call-presentation.pdf

## Publication Date
12 May 2025

## Publisher
talabat Holding plc (Investor Relations)

## Executive Summary
This 19-page deck covers talabat's Q1 2025 results, the first quarter to include (partial) InstaShop financials following talabat's acquisition of 100% of InstaShop. Reported talabat-only (excl. InstaShop) figures show Q1'25 GMV of USD 2.1bn (+33% y/y constant currency), Management Revenue of USD 846mn (+38% y/y cFX), Adjusted EBITDA of USD 140mn (6.7% of GMV), and Adjusted Net Income of USD 99mn (4.8% of GMV) (p.4). A dedicated section explains the strategic rationale for the InstaShop acquisition — a grocery/retail-focused, largely non-overlapping customer base (only 1 in 3 InstaShop users use talabat G&R) present in UAE and Egypt (p.6-7). The deck also shows talabat has structurally reduced Ramadan-driven order seasonality by ~40% since 2023 through operational improvements and G&R adoption (p.9). FY2025 guidance (excl. InstaShop) is reiterated at 17-18% GMV growth, 18-20% revenue growth, 6.5-7.0% Adj. EBITDA margin, 5.0-5.5% net income margin, min. USD 400mn dividends (p.13).

## Key Financial Metrics
- Q1'25 GMV (talabat-only, excl. InstaShop): USD 2.1bn (2,084mn), +30% y/y reported / +33% y/y constant currency (p.4, p.10)
- Q1'25 Management Revenue: USD 846mn, +34% y/y reported / +38% y/y cFX (p.4, p.10)
- Q1'25 Adjusted EBITDA: USD 140mn, 6.7% of GMV (+0.2pp y/y) (p.4, p.11)
- Q1'25 reported Net Income: USD 103mn, 4.9% of GMV (p.4)
- Q1'25 Adjusted Net Income: USD 99mn, 4.8% of GMV (-0.2pp y/y), +24% y/y (p.4, p.11)
- Q1'25 Adjusted Free Cash Flow: USD 135mn, 6.5% of GMV, +39% y/y, cash conversion ratio 96% (p.11)
- InstaShop-only Q1'25: GMV USD 173mn (+10% y/y reported, +16% y/y cFX); Adj. EBITDA USD 3.4mn (2.0% of GMV, +0.3pp y/y, +25% y/y); Adj. Net Income USD 2.3mn (1.3% of GMV, +6% y/y) (p.12)
- Q1'25 reported financials bridge (talabat + InstaShop, IFRS basis): Management Revenue USD 857mn; Adj. EBITDA USD 141mn; Net Profit USD 103mn; Adj. Net Profit USD 99mn; Adj. FCF USD 136mn (p.16)
- Dividends: USD 110mn re: Q4'24 approved at 2025 AGM; company remains on track for minimum USD 400mn dividends for FY2025 (p.4)
- FY2025 guidance (excl. InstaShop) vs. Q1'25 actual: GMV growth 17-18% guided (USD 8.7-8.8bn) vs. Q1 33% (USD 2.1bn); Revenue growth 18-20% guided (USD 3.49-3.55bn) vs. Q1 38% (USD 873mn); Adj. EBITDA margin 6.5-7.0% guided (USD 565-614mn) vs. Q1 6.7% (USD 140mn); Net income margin 5.0-5.5% guided (USD 435-482mn) vs. Q1 4.9% (USD 103mn); Adj. FCF margin 6.0-6.5% guided (USD 521-570mn) vs. Q1 6.5% (USD 135mn) (p.13)

## Key Operational Metrics
- InstaShop: >8K active partners, >2M listed SKUs, ~16% y/y GMV growth (constant currency, Q1'25 vs Q1'24) (p.6)
- InstaShop customer base overlap: only 1 in 3 InstaShop users use talabat Grocery & Retail — "2 out of every 3 of instashop users are not active on talabat's Grocery & Retail," reinforcing complementary customer bases (Jan-Mar 2025 comparison) (p.6)
- InstaShop NPS of 54 (study Oct-Dec 2024, 12.6k respondents); 10 years in Grocery & Retail (p.7)
- Ramadan seasonality: year-on-year drop in daily orders reduced from 1x (2023) to 0.9x (2024) to 0.6x (2025), a ~40% reduction in seasonal impact since 2023, attributed to operational improvements and increasing G&R adoption (p.9)
- Revenue margin (% of GMV): 39.5% (Q1'24) rising to 40.6% (Q1'25) (p.10)

## Countries Mentioned
UAE, Egypt (explicitly named as InstaShop's market presence, p.6). GCC markets referenced generically re: corporate tax increases (p.11).

## Egypt Mentions
- Egypt named alongside UAE as InstaShop's "Market Presence" (p.6).
- Customer base overlap analysis (InstaShop vs. talabat G&R) is based on data covering InstaShop's UAE and Egypt operations for the period 1 Jan 2025 - 31 March 2025 (p.6, footnote 4).
- Adjusted Net Income definition references "non-cash unrealised foreign exchange loss from shareholder loan liability in Delivery Hero Egypt SAE" as an excluded component (p.17).

## Retention-Relevant Information
- InstaShop acquisition explicitly framed as a way "to scale G&R faster" with "limited customer overlap" vs. talabat's existing base — a cross-sell/win-back opportunity, since 2 of 3 InstaShop users are not active on talabat's G&R (p.6).
- InstaShop users described as "monthly habitual users, ordering large baskets," with a "previously purchased filter" and "substitution/replacement flow" as UX features supporting reorder convenience (p.7).
- Reduced Ramadan seasonality (40% improvement since 2023) attributed partly to "increasing G&R adoption," implying multi-vertical usage smooths order volatility and supports retention through demand troughs (p.9).
- "Driving customer loyalty" retained as a growth lever (AdTech solutions, CPG partnerships, new initiatives incl. DineOut deals, talabat Kitchens) (p.5).
- "Deeper category penetration" lever again lists frequency-enhancing initiatives, loyalty boosters, and FinTech (p.5).

## Important Quotations
- "To scale G&R faster over the coming years, talabat has successfully acquired 100% of instashop" (p.6)
- "Only 1 out of 3 instashop users use talabat G&R" — "2 out of every 3 of instashop users are not active on talabat's Grocery & Retail - reinforcing complementary customer bases" (p.6)
- "instashop users are monthly habitual users, ordering large baskets" (p.7)
- "We've significantly reduced the impact of seasonality over time... YoY drop reduced by ~40% since 2023" (p.9)
- "Strong GMV growth due to stronger demand across all regions and verticals, driven by customer acquisitions, higher order frequency and expanded multi-verticality" (p.10)

## Limitations
- No Egypt-specific standalone GMV, revenue, or customer-count figures; Egypt appears only in the InstaShop market-presence and customer-overlap context, not talabat-core financials.
- All headline financials described as "talabat-only," excluding InstaShop, except the appendix reported-financials bridge (p.16); comparability across quarters requires care given the phased InstaShop consolidation.
- Presentation summarizes pro forma financial statements only; not the auditor-reviewed Q1 2025 financial statements (Disclaimer, p.18).
