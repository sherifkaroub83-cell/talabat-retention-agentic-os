# TLB-012 — talabat Investor Presentation (24 February 2025)

The Leading On-Demand Delivery Platform in MENA — Investor Presentation (covering Q4 & FY 2024 results)

## Executive Summary
This 28-page investor presentation, presented by CEO Tomaso Rodriguez, CFO Khaled Alfakesh and IR's Shadi Salman, covers talabat's Q4 and full-year 2024 results and 2025 outlook. It positions talabat as the category-leading on-demand food, grocery and retail delivery platform across 8 MENA countries (UAE, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain, Egypt, Oman, Jordan, Iraq), reporting 2024 GMV of ~USD 7.4bn (+23% y/y), Management Revenue of USD 3.0bn (+32% y/y), Adjusted EBITDA of USD 497mn (6.7% of GMV, +55% y/y) and net income of USD 346mn (4.7% of GMV, +64% y/y) (p.13, p.19, p.21, p.22). It highlights the multi-vertical ecosystem (Food, tMarts, Local shops, talabat pro) as a driver of loyalty and frequency, the pending InstaShop acquisition (expected to close 2025), and the February 2025 full launch of talabat pro in Egypt (p.17). The deck sets FY2025 guidance for GMV growth (17-18%), revenue growth (18-20%), EBITDA margin (6.5-7.0%), net income margin (5.0-5.5%) and dividends (min. USD 400mn) (p.24).

## Key Financial Metrics
- 2024 Total GMV: ~USD 7.4bn, +23% y/y (p.4, p.13, p.19)
- 2024 Total Management Revenue: USD 3.0bn, +32% y/y (p.13, p.19)
- 2024 Adjusted EBITDA: USD 497mn, +55% y/y, 6.7% of GMV (+1.4ppt y/y margin expansion) (p.13, p.21)
- 2024 Net Income: USD 346mn, +64% y/y, 4.7% of GMV (+1.2ppt y/y margin expansion) (p.13, p.22)
- 2024 Adjusted Net Income: USD 393mn (+53% y/y), 5.3% of GMV (p.22)
- 2024 Adjusted Free Cash Flow: 6.2% of GMV, +48% y/y (p.21)
- Dividends: company intends to pay USD 110mn re: Q4'24, implying annualised dividend yield >4.5% (based on AED 1.47 share price, 12 Feb 2025); at least USD 400mn intended for FY'25 (p.13, p.22)
- G&R segment ~25% of total GMV in 2024 (up from ~21%, G&R y/y growth ~46-47%) (p.20)
- Non-GCC GMV growth ~42% y/y (from lower base); GCC GMV growth ~20% y/y; Non-GCC contribution to GMV ~16% (of range 12-24% across periods shown) (p.20)
- tMart business ~28% of revenue in 2024 (p.19)
- FY2024 vs. guidance: GMV growth 23% (guided 22-23%, in line, high end); Revenue growth 32% (guided 28-30%, exceeded); Adj. EBITDA margin 6.7% (guided ~6.5%, exceeded); Net income margin 4.7% (guided ~5%, in line); Adj. FCF margin 6.2% (guided 6.0-6.5%, in line); Dividends USD 110mn (guided min. USD 100mn, exceeded) (p.24)
- FY2025 guidance: GMV growth 17-18% y/y constant currency; Revenue growth 18-20%; Adj. EBITDA margin 6.5-7.0%; Net income margin 5.0-5.5%; Adj. FCF margin 6.0-6.5%; Dividends min. USD 400mn (p.24)
- 2024 GMV by category benchmark: talabat 2024 GMV ~$7.4bn vs. c.$15bn "Foodservice ordered online + Grocery delivery + Serviceable retail e-commerce" addressable category, c.$136bn "Eat-at-home + Grocery + High Potential retail e-commerce" categories, and c.$299bn overall Foodservice and Retail market; category CAGR 24-29E of 9-12% (p.6)

## Countries Mentioned
UAE, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain, Egypt, Oman, Jordan, Iraq (the 8 "talabat perimeter" MENA countries) (p.4, p.6, p.11). GCC region defined as UAE, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain, Oman; non-GCC as Jordan, Egypt, Iraq (p.11).

## Egypt Mentions
- Egypt listed as one of the 8 MENA countries in talabat's market presence / "talabat perimeter" (p.4, p.11).
- Egypt included in the non-GCC segment grouping (with Jordan, Iraq) used for GMV growth reporting (non-GCC GMV +42% y/y in 2024) (p.11, p.20).
- Egypt included among survey countries (Kuwait, UAE, Egypt, Qatar, Bahrain) where users were surveyed on restaurant variety, delivery time, rider behaviour, customer support, discounts, prices, delivery fees and loyalty programs, resulting in 80%+ ranking talabat #1 (Aug 2024 survey) (p.7).
- Company history: "Otlob (Egypt) rebranded to talabat" — noted as a milestone in talabat's journey/timeline (p.5).
- talabat pro adoption growth figure (2.1x Dec'24 vs Dec'23) explicitly "excludes Egypt and Iraq," since pro was only live in UAE, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain, Jordan, Oman as of Dec 2024 (p.17, footnote 1).
- "talabat pro now in Egypt" — key update noted; footnote clarifies "Full launch of talabat pro in Egypt took place in Feb '25" (p.17).
- New personalized-app acquisition uplift metrics (tPro +24%, Grocery +2%) explicitly "excludes Egypt and Iraq" for the tPro figure (p.16, footnote 1).
- Adjusted Net Income definition footnote references "non-cash unrealised foreign exchange loss from shareholder loan liability in Delivery Hero Egypt SAE" as a component excluded from Adjusted Net Income (p.26, APM appendix).

## Retention-Relevant Information
- talabat pro described as the company's tool for "Turbo-charging customer loyalty," featuring free delivery (subject to minimum order value) and exclusive deals; adoption growth of 2.1x (Dec'24 vs Dec'23) and +28% incremental frequency uplift for pro members vs. lookalike non-pro users (p.17).
- talabat pro launched fully in Egypt in February 2025 — directly relevant to Egypt retention strategy timing (p.17).
- Multi-vertical ecosystem framed as the core loyalty driver: customer journey progression from "Orders Food" → "Expands to Grocery & Retail" → "Signs up for talabat pro"; multi-vertical users order 13.0x/month vs. 3.8x/month for food-only users (p.9).
- Order frequency uplift of >20% specifically attributed to talabat pro subscription (July 2024 data) (p.9).
- "Multiple levers for sustained growth" strategic framework explicitly lists "Turbo-charging customer loyalty" (AdTech, CPG partnerships, new initiatives like DineOut deals and talabat Kitchens) and "Deeper category penetration" (frequency enhancing initiatives, loyalty boosters, FinTech) as growth levers (p.10, p.14).
- New personalized talabat app ("it's your app") strategy: framed around three pillars — Unlock Personalisation, Showcase Relevant Choice, Strengthen Multi-Verticality — yielding +24% tPro acquisition uplift and +2% grocery acquisition uplift (p.15, p.16).
- Partner-funded savings (~$450mn LTM) and 80%+ "ranked #1" score used as evidence of value-driven loyalty (p.7).
- Network effects flywheel diagram: more customers → more partners/orders → better delivery/fleet efficiency → more sales, reinforcing retention indirectly via service quality (p.8).
- Huge headroom in category penetration and multi-verticality: Food customer adoption 23%, Groceries 2%, Other adjacent verticals 1% of addressable population — implying large room to deepen engagement/frequency among existing customers (p.11).

## Limitations
- No Egypt-specific financial figures (GMV, revenue, active customers, etc.) are disclosed anywhere in the document — Egypt is folded into the "non-GCC" segment together with Jordan and Iraq.
- Several key operational KPIs (order frequency uplift, talabat pro adoption growth of 2.1x) explicitly exclude Egypt and Iraq from their calculation base, since talabat pro was not fully live there until Feb 2025 — meaning most of the loyalty/subscription metrics in this document predate and do not reflect Egypt.
- Financial results in this presentation exclude any contribution from InstaShop (footnote, p.5, p.10 disclaimer on p.27), which had not yet closed.
- This is an unaudited/preliminary pro forma summary (per Disclaimer, p.27), not the final audited FY2024 financial statements.
- Forward-looking FY2025 guidance is subject to change and does not reflect the InstaShop acquisition's potential impact (p.24, footnote 1).
