## Executive Summary
This is a one-page bilingual (Arabic/English) regulatory disclosure notice filed on the Dubai
Financial Market disclosing the status of Talabat Holding PLC's share buyback (treasury shares)
program. The buyback decision was taken at the General Assembly Meeting on 13 April 2026, with
the program running from 18 May 2026 to 12 April 2028, for up to 5% of issued capital. As of the
notice date, 107,900,000 shares (0.4636% of issued capital) had been bought under the program at
an average price of 1.182 AED/share, leaving approximately 1,056,512,031 shares still eligible for
purchase. The notice also discloses a specific day's purchase: on 10 July 2026, talabat bought
150,000 shares at an average price of 1.200 AED/share (range: 1.190–1.210 AED). The notice is
signed by Abdullah AlGhrawi, VP Legal, GRC & Board Secretary. (Doc ID, filename, page 1)
