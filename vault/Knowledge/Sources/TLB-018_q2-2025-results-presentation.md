Q2 2025 Results — "The Leading On-Demand Delivery Platform in MENA"

## Executive Summary
This 17-page deck covers talabat's Q2 2025 and H1 2025 results, presented by CEO Tomaso Rodriguez, CFO Khaled Alfakesh and IR's Shadi Salman. Talabat-only (excl. InstaShop) figures show Q2'25 GMV of USD (cFX) 2.5bn (+33% y/y), Management Revenue of USD (cFX) 986mn (+36% y/y), Adjusted EBITDA of USD 166mn (6.8% of GMV), and Adjusted Net Income of USD 116mn (4.8% of GMV) (p.4). The deck emphasizes accelerating Monthly Active User (MAU) growth (~25% y/y vs. ~18% y/y a year earlier), rising multi-verticality (42% GMV share, up from 36% a year prior) and talabat pro penetration (2.1x growth), and introduces a new "Family Plan" that extends talabat pro benefits to up to 4 household members, delivering >40% higher retention vs. the solo plan (p.5-6). Full-year 2025 guidance was revised upward following strong H1 performance: GMV growth raised to 27-29% (from 17-18%), revenue growth to 29-32% (from 18-20%), while EBITDA and net income margin guidance were held roughly stable (p.10).

## Key Operational Metrics
- Platform Monthly Active Users grew from 63K (Jun'24) to 77K (Jun'25); y/y MAU growth accelerated from ~18% (Jun'24 vs Jun'23) to ~25% (Jun'25 vs Jun'24) (p.5)
- Multi-verticality: 42% of GMV from multi-vertical users in Jun'25 vs. 36% in Jun'24 (+4pp... note: page shows 34%/30% and 36%/42% figures in different spots; multi-verticality GMV share rose to 42% by Jun'25) (p.5)
- talabat pro adoption penetration: 2.1x growth Jun'24 to Jun'25 (p.5)
- FinTech GMV penetration ("PostPaid" and "talabat ADCB Credit Card"): 5.6% (Jun'24) to 6.1% (Jun'25) (p.5)
- Active Riders: +36% y/y; Active Partners: +21% y/y (p.5)
- Partner-Funded Savings (H1, including incentives, B2B and bank partnerships): USD 195M (H1'24, restated) to USD 277M (H1'25) (p.5)
- Family Plan: >40% cost saving per person vs. solo plan (for a 4-member family); >60% higher retention vs. solo plan (p.6)

## Retention-Relevant Information
- New "Family Plan" launched: extends talabat pro benefits (free delivery, exclusive tMart deals, booster discounts, partnership discounts e.g. streaming/ride-hailing) to up to 4 family members under one plan, with payment sharing and parental spend controls; delivers >40% cost savings per person and >60% higher retention vs. the solo plan (p.6).
- talabat pro penetration measured as # of pro subscribers / platform active users in pro-live markets, showing 2.1x growth Jun'24 to Jun'25 (p.5).
- Multi-verticality (share of multi-vertical users) is tracked as a core strategic pillar under "Ecosystem," with GMV share rising materially y/y (p.5).
- MAU growth accelerated to ~25% y/y (Jun'25 vs Jun'24), attributed to "increased focus on our strategic pillars": Selection, Experience, Value, Ecosystem (p.5).
- "Driving customer loyalty" retained as a growth lever, including AdTech solutions, CPG partnerships, and new initiatives (DineOut deals, talabat Kitchens) (p.5... actually growth-lever slide appears as business-update framing consistent with prior quarters).
