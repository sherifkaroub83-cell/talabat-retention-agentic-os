## Executive Summary
This is a single-sheet spreadsheet ("2024 Quarterly Historicals") presenting a quarterly breakdown
of GMV and selected income-statement lines for talabat on a pro forma basis, as if the corporate
restructuring carried out at the end of September 2024 (ahead of talabat's IPO) had taken place on
1 January 2023. It explicitly excludes instashop, which was only consolidated into talabat's
financials as of 25 February 2025. The dataset is prepared on the same basis as the financials in
the International Offering Memorandum used for the IPO, and is intended to enable like-for-like
comparability of the combined company (ex-instashop) with prior-year periods. It provides
Q1'24–Q4'24 and FY'24 figures for GMV (with GCC/non-GCC and Food/G&R splits), Management
Revenue and its components (commission fees, subscription fee & other income, delivery & service
fees, advertising & listing fees), Revenue (IFRS), gross profit, Adjusted EBITDA, net income,
Adjusted Net Income, Adjusted Free Cash Flow, and cash conversion ratio, each with margins as a
percentage of GMV.

## Limitations
- Single-sheet spreadsheet with no narrative/commentary — purely tabular data, no explanatory text
  beyond the header note.
- No country-level breakdown; Egypt is not separately identifiable (folded into "non-GCC" with
  Jordan and Iraq).
- No operational metrics (no MAU, orders, riders, partners).
- Pro forma basis assumes the IPO-related restructuring occurred on 1 January 2023 and explicitly
  excludes instashop — figures are not directly comparable to as-reported IFRS consolidated

  statements).
- FY'24 GMV (USD 7,427.85mn), management revenue, and non-GCC GMV (USD 1,096.31mn) figures in this
  sheet match the FY2024 comparatives quoted in TLB-002's Financial Review section, providing a
  useful cross-check between sources.

  this is an XLSX export with only one sheet and no page markers.
