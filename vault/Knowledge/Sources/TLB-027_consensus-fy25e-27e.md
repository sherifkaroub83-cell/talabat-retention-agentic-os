## Executive Summary
This single-page, dense tabular document compiles sell-side analyst forecasts for talabat's Q4'25e and FY'25e-27e financial performance, contributed by 9 investment banks (Arqaam Capital, Bank of America, Barclays, Citi, EFG Hermes, Goldman Sachs, JPMorgan, Morgan Stanley, UBS). For each KPI and period it reports the number of contributing analysts, and the Min, Median, Max and arithmetic-average ("Cons.") of their estimates. Key metrics covered: GMV, Management Revenue, Revenue (IFRS), Gross Profit, Adjusted EBITDA, Net Income, Adjusted Net Income, Adjusted Free Cash Flow, Dividends, DPS, NOSH and payout ratio, each shown against FY'24A/Q4'24A actuals as a baseline. Median FY'25e GMV consensus was ~USD 10.0bn (vs. FY'24A USD 7.4bn), with FY'26e and FY'27e medians of ~USD 11.6bn and ~USD 13.2bn respectively — implying analysts expected GMV growth to decelerate from the low-30s% in FY'25e to high-teens/mid-teens % by FY'27e. As a secondary, non-company-guidance source, this document should be used only for external-analyst-expectation context (e.g., benchmarking a retention strategy's implied impact against market expectations), not as ground truth for talabat's actual or targeted performance.

## Limitations
- **This is a SECONDARY source — sell-side analyst estimates, not talabat management guidance or actuals.** Every forward-looking figure here is a third-party forecast, explicitly disclaimed by talabat as not representing its own views.
- No Egypt or country-level breakdown whatsoever; entirely group-consolidated figures.
- No operational KPIs (customers, orders, retention, loyalty) — financial metrics only.
- The compiled figures reflect only analysts who had updated their models as of the compilation date (6 Feb 2026) following talabat's most recent results disclosure; the panel of contributing analysts differs slightly by metric (6-9 contributors depending on line item), so "Cons." across different KPIs is not a perfectly consistent panel.
- Some secondary derived ratio rows (e.g., "revenue take rate," per-period growth % for certain metrics) contain values in the extracted flat text whose exact Min/Median/Max/Cons. column alignment could not be fully disambiguated with the same confidence as the primary KPI rows (GMV, Revenue, EBITDA, Net Income) — those primary rows were independently verified by cross-checking that "Cons." growth percentages reconcile arithmetically with the absolute Cons. values against the prior-period base. Where ambiguity existed, the more speculative ratio rows were omitted from this note in favor of the verified primary metrics.
- Superseded by TLB-028 (compiled 4 May 2026), which reflects a later, more current analyst panel and updated estimates (FY'26e-28e).
