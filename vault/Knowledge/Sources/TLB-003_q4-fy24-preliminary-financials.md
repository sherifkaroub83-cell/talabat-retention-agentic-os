# TLB-003 — Q4/FY2024 Preliminary Financial Results

## Title
Talabat Holding plc — Preliminary financial results for the twelve months ended 31 December 2024 (unaudited)

## Publication Date
12 February 2025 (signed by Chairperson Pieter-Jan Vandepitte)

## Publisher
Talabat Holding plc (DFM regulatory disclosure)

## Executive Summary
This is talabat Holding plc's first post-IPO preliminary (unaudited) financial disclosure, covering the statutory period from inception (3 September 2024) to 31 December 2024, filed per DFM disclosure requirements ahead of the completed audit (Doc ID, page 2). It reports total assets of USD 1,107M, shareholders' equity of USD 538M, revenue of USD 801M and net profit of USD 138M for the inception-to-31-Dec-2024 period (TLB-003, page 2). The document also presents unaudited pro forma financial information for Q4-2024/Q4-2023 and FY-2024/FY-2023, prepared as if the September 2024 corporate restructuring (ahead of the 10 December 2024 IPO) had been effected on 1 January 2023 (TLB-003, page 3). On this pro forma basis, FY2024 GMV reached USD 7,428M (+23% y/y), Revenue (IFRS) USD 2,872M (+32% y/y), Adjusted EBITDA USD 497M (+55% y/y), and Net Profit USD 346M (+64% y/y) (TLB-003, page 3). The pro forma data breaks GMV and Management Revenue into GCC vs. non-GCC and Food vs. Grocery & Retail sub-segments, but does not name Egypt specifically. The document is purely financial/statutory in nature — no operational KPIs (orders, active customers, riders) or country-specific narrative are included.

## Retention-Relevant Information
- "Subscription fee & Other Income" grew 44% y/y to USD 952M for FY-2024, the fastest-growing revenue line disclosed, suggesting strong subscription/loyalty momentum group-wide, though this line also includes non-subscription "other income" and is not Egypt-specific (TLB-003, page 3).
- Grocery & Retail GMV grew faster (47% y/y FY-2024) than Food GMV (16% y/y), indicating a group-wide mix shift toward quick-commerce/grocery that is relevant to basket diversification as a retention lever (TLB-003, page 3).

## Limitations
- Preliminary and unaudited — figures were subsequently subject to full audit (see TLB-004 for the audited version).
- Covers only the statutory inception-to-31-Dec-2024 period for the balance sheet/headline figures; the Q4/FY pro forma comparison table is presented on a separate, explicitly pro forma basis not directly reconcilable to the statutory figures.
- No country-level breakdown beyond GCC vs. non-GCC; Egypt is not named or quantified separately.
- No operational KPIs (orders, active customers, riders, restaurant partners) are disclosed.
- Very short document (4 pages), largely administrative/regulatory in format.
