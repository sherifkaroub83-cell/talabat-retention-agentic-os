# TLB-023 — Talabat Holding plc Announces its Intention to Float on the Dubai Financial Market (DFM)

## Title
Talabat Holding plc Announces its Intention to Float on the Dubai Financial Market (DFM)

11 November 2024 (Dubai, UAE)

## Publisher
Talabat Holding plc (media/IR contact: Teneo, Financial Communications Advisor)

## Executive Summary
This is talabat's formal "intention to float" (ITF) announcement, the richest of the four IPO press releases in this batch. It confirms a planned 15% stake sale by sole shareholder Delivery Hero MENA Holding GmbH, sets out the dividend policy (minimum USD 100 million dividend in April 2025, USD 400 million for FY2025 in two instalments, then twice-yearly dividends targeting a 90% net income payout), and gives an extensive "Investment Highlights" and "talabat's Strategy" narrative covering market leadership, the multi-sided marketplace flywheel, FinTech/loyalty plans, AdTech plans, adjacent-category expansion, and the planned InstaShop acquisition. It also discloses key combined financial highlights for FY2022, FY2023, and the nine months to 29 September 2024 (GMV, revenue, Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted Net Profit, Adjusted Free Cash Flow, cash conversion). Chairperson Pieter-Jan Vandepitte and CEO Tomaso Rodriguez both provide commentary framing the IPO as a "defining moment" for talabat since Delivery Hero's 2015 acquisition of the company.

- Revenue: AED 7,987.7m (USD 2.2bn) FY2023 vs AED 6,310.3m (USD 1.7bn) FY2022 (+26.6% YoY); AED 7,604.3m for 1 Jan–29 Sep 2024 vs AED 5,760.9m same period 2023 (+32.0%) (TLB-023, page 10, page 7)
- Adjusted EBITDA: AED 1,180.5m FY2023 vs AED 796.8m FY2022; AED 1,315.7m for 1 Jan–29 Sep 2024 vs AED 805.4m same period 2023 (TLB-023, page 10)

- Adjusted Net Profit: AED 944.0m FY2023 vs AED 563.7m FY2022; AED 995.0m for 1 Jan-29 Sep 2024 vs AED 578.6m same period 2023 (TLB-023, page 10)
- Adjusted Net Profit Margin: 4.2% FY2023 vs 3.0% FY2022; 5.0% for 1 Jan-29 Sep 2024 vs 3.5% same period 2023 (TLB-023, page 10)
- Adjusted Free Cash Flow: AED 1,105.3m (USD 0.3bn) FY2023 vs AED 616.9m (USD 0.2bn) FY2022 (+79.2% YoY); AED 1,265.3m for 1 Jan-29 Sep 2024 vs AED 752.6m same period 2023 (+68.1%) (TLB-023, page 7, page 10)
- Adjusted Free Cash Flow Margin: ~6.4% for 1 Jan-29 Sep 2024 vs 4.6% same period 2023 (TLB-023, page 7)
- Cash Conversion: 77.4% FY2022, 93.6% FY2023, 93.4% (9M 2023), 96.2% (9M 2024) (TLB-023, page 10)
- Food service TAC (2023, all talabat markets): ~AED 77bn (USD 21bn); Grocery TAC: ~AED 349bn (USD 95bn); Other adjacent categories TAC: ~AED 29bn (USD 8bn) (TLB-023, page 5-6)
- Planned dividends: minimum AED 367.25m (USD 100m) in April 2025 (Q4 2024 results); minimum AED 1,469m (USD 400m) in two instalments (October 2025, April 2026) for FY2025; thereafter twice-yearly dividends targeting 90% net income payout (TLB-023, page 2-3)
- Share capital at UAE Prospectus date: AED 931,529,625 divided into 23,288,240,625 shares, nominal value AED 0.04 each (TLB-023, page 2)

## Key Operational Metrics

- ~99% order success rate; average delivery time under 30 minutes (September 2024) (TLB-023, page 6)
- Aggregate annual customer savings of more than AED 1,567.7 million from 29 September 2023 to 29 September 2024 (TLB-023, page 6)
- GMV for cohorts acquired since 2019 increased approximately 4x over 5 years; cash conversion rate of 96% (1 Jan-29 Sep 2024) (TLB-023, page 7)
- Over 480 product, design, engineering and data technologists across Dubai and Cairo tech-hubs (TLB-023, page 7)
- Management team has over 40 years combined C-suite management experience (TLB-023, page 8)
- Addressable population across 8 MENA countries: ~71.1 million; total population: over 185 million (TLB-023, page 6)
- talabat pro: over 20% increase in order frequency among subscribers (September 2024) (TLB-023, page 8)
- AdTech products adopted per partner increased 20% from 2021 to H1 2024 (TLB-023, page 9)

## Countries Mentioned
UAE, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain, Egypt, Oman, Jordan, Iraq (talabat's 8 operating markets); also United States, Canada, Australia, South Africa, Japan, Saudi Arabia, and various EEA/UK jurisdictions (legal boilerplate) (TLB-023, page 5, 6-16)

## Egypt Mentions
- talabat "has operations in the UAE, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain, Egypt, Oman, Jordan, and Iraq." (TLB-023, page 5)
- Under "we intend to pursue targeted investments, acquisitions, and strategic partnerships": "We have pursued a strategy of making strategic alliances with suitable partners (e.g. 'Zomato' in the UAE and 'Otlob' in Egypt), and we expect to continue to do so in the future." (TLB-023, page 9) — confirms Otlob was talabat's Egyptian brand/partner alliance.
- On the planned InstaShop acquisition: "InstaShop, through its subsidiaries, primarily operates in the groceries and retail space in the UAE and Egypt, where customers can order groceries, pharmacy products, beauty items, and other personal products, with delivery times of approximately 30 minutes." (TLB-023, page 10)
- Footnote on Adjusted Net Profit definition: excludes "foreign exchange income (loss) (mainly related to non-cash unrealised foreign exchange loss from shareholder loan liability in Delivery Hero Egypt SAE)" — confirms an Egypt-specific legal entity, "Delivery Hero Egypt SAE," and that Egypt-linked FX exposure is material enough to warrant an adjustment (TLB-023, page 10, footnote 11).
- Cairo is named as one of talabat's two tech-hub cities (with Dubai), housing part of the >480 technologists (TLB-023, page 7).

## Retention-Relevant Information
- talabat pro described as part of the "FinTech and customer loyalty offerings" strategic pillar: "We also plan to continue to leverage our extensive loyalty programme and subscription service, talabat pro, with a view to increasing order frequency. talabat pro provides benefits such as free delivery and exclusive deals for a fixed monthly or yearly fee. This subscription model has proven effective in boosting customer engagement and retention, with over 20% increase in order frequency amongst talabat pro subscribers for the month of September 2024." (TLB-023, page 8)
- talabat Postpaid (FinTech): "We plan to continue investing into our 'FinTech' operations by enhancing our payment acceptance capabilities both in terms of performance and cost saving, expanding usage of talabat Postpaid into existing and new markets." (TLB-023, page 8)
- AdTech as partner-retention/growth tool: "Our AdTech products have demonstrated significant growth, with AdTech products adopted per partner increasing by 20% from 2021 to H1 2024... which significantly contributed to a reduction in their customer acquisition costs." (TLB-023, page 9)
- Machine learning for personalisation: "talabat's machine learning models allow it to introduce and cross-sell verticals, as well as initiatives such as talabat pro and talabat Rewards, at the right time for customers to benefit from." (TLB-023, page 8) — direct evidence of AI-driven personalization tied to retention initiatives (talabat pro, talabat Rewards).
- Flywheel description: "talabat's business model benefits from powerful network effects, which fuel a virtuous cycle of growth, improvements to service quality and deepening of platform loyalty – the 'talabat flywheel'." (TLB-023, page 6)
- Value proposition dimensions for retention: Selection (>56,300 Restaurants, 9,500 Local Shops, 155+ cuisines), Experience (99% order success rate, <30 min average delivery), Value (targeted/personalised promotions, AED 1,567.7m annual savings) (TLB-023, page 6)
- Adjacent-category expansion strategy explicitly tied to platform loyalty: "we plan to enter into adjacent product categories... including beauty and cosmetics, health products, flowers, and pet essentials," plus continued tMart rollout "to being the platform of choice for our customers." (TLB-023, page 9)

## Limitations
Financial figures are presented on a "combined basis" from unaudited interim combined and carve-out financial statements of Delivery Hero FZ LLC and the Extended Perimeter Group — not final audited IPO-entity financials. No standalone Egypt financial breakout is given; Egypt appears only in qualitative/strategic mentions and one footnote referencing "Delivery Hero Egypt SAE" FX exposure. The InstaShop acquisition was still pending (expected to close in 2025) at the time of this announcement. Much of the document (pages 12-16) is legal disclaimer boilerplate.
