## Executive Summary
This is talabat Holding PLC's second quarterly IFRS interim financial statement, covering the three-month period ended 30 June 2025 and the cumulative period since inception (3 September 2024) to 30 June 2025 (TLB-006, page 1). Q2 2025 revenue was USD 981.0M with net profit of USD 121.3M (TLB-006, page 5). Cumulative revenue reached USD 2,615.9M with cumulative net profit of USD 362.5M (TLB-006, page 5). The Group paid a USD 110.0M dividend during the period (in respect of Q4 2024 results) (TLB-006, page 6, note 23). Total equity grew to USD 651.3M by 30 June 2025 (TLB-006, page 6). As with the Q1 2025 statement, the Group reports on a "GCC" vs. "Other segments" basis; Egypt is named as part of the MENA footprint and as the incorporation country for one InstaShop entity and for "Delivery Hero Egypt SAE," but is not broken out financially (TLB-006, pages 8, 9, 22). This is a formal IFRS statement with notes; it contains no operational KPIs (GMV, orders, active customers) or retention/loyalty narrative. Note: the extracted text for page 4 (statement of financial position) is empty/missing in this text file — see Limitations.
