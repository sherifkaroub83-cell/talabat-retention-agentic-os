# TLB-011 — Q1 2026 Results Press Release

## Title
Talabat Holding plc — Q1 2026 Results Press Release: "talabat delivers strong Q1 2026 performance, advances strategic investments and increases guidance"

12 May 2026

## Publisher
Talabat Holding plc (Dubai, UAE)

## Executive Summary
This press release reports talabat's pro forma Q1 2026 results (this time including InstaShop for comparability) and raises full-year 2026 net income guidance (TLB-011, page 1). GMV grew 19% y/y (18% cFX) to USD 2.7 billion, "driven by an expanded customer base" and supported by Ramadan operations, favourable Eid seasonality, and the platform's positioning amid "the ongoing regional conflict" (TLB-011, pages 1–2). Revenue grew 23% to USD 1.0 billion, but Adjusted EBITDA fell 9% y/y to USD 130 million (4.8% of GMV, down from 6.3%) and net income fell 18% to USD 87 million (3.2% of GMV), reflecting the company's new 2026 "Everyday App" investment programme (TLB-011, pages 1–2). Full-year 2026 guidance for net income was raised by USD 20 million to USD 300–330 million, while GMV growth (11–14%), Revenue growth (14–17%), Adjusted EBITDA (USD 510–540M), and Free Cash Flow (USD 370–400M) guidance were reaffirmed (TLB-011, page 1). The company also announced it expects to commence its recently-approved share buyback programme (up to 5% of issued shares) shortly after the results (TLB-011, page 1). Notably, this release explicitly cites "increased incentives to retain medium and high-value customers" as a driver of the higher GMV-to-revenue conversion ratio — a directly retention-relevant strategic disclosure (TLB-011, page 2). GCC vs. non-GCC GMV splits are given but Egypt is not broken out separately in this press release (unlike the companion financial statements, TLB-010, which for the first time reports Egypt as its own segment).

## Key Financial Metrics
- Q1 2026 GMV: USD 2.685 billion (+19% y/y; +18% cFX) (TLB-011, page 4)
  - o/w GCC: USD 2,122M (+12% y/y, 79% of total, prior year 84%) (TLB-011, page 2)
  - o/w non-GCC: USD 563M (+52% y/y, 21% of total, prior year 16%) (TLB-011, page 2)
- Q1 2026 Revenue: USD 1,048M (+23% y/y); USD 1,041M cFX (+22%); GMV-to-revenue conversion ratio 39% (prior year 38%) (TLB-011, page 4)
- Revenue mix as % of GMV: Commissions 12.8% (prior year 13.4%); Delivery fees 6.1% (prior year 6.9%); Service fees 2.2%; Subscription fees 1.0% (prior year 0.5%); Own grocery & other income 15.2% (prior year 12.6%); Advertising and listing fees 3.4%; Vouchers & other discounts -1.7% (TLB-011, page 4)
- Q1 2026 Gross Profit: USD 264M (-0.1% y/y); 9.8% of GMV (prior year 11.7%) (TLB-011, page 4)
- Q1 2026 Adjusted EBITDA: USD 130M (-9% y/y, USD 13M lower); 4.8% of GMV (prior year 6.3%) (TLB-011, page 2)
- Q1 2026 Net income: USD 87M (-18% y/y); 3.2% of GMV (prior year 4.7%) (TLB-011, page 2)
- Q1 2026 Free Cash Flow: USD 104M (+7% y/y); 3.9% of GMV (prior year 4.3%); Cash Conversion Ratio 81% (prior year 68%) (TLB-011, page 2)
- FY2026 guidance (raised/reaffirmed): GMV growth 11-14% (cFX); Revenue growth 14-17% (cFX); Adjusted EBITDA USD 510-540M; Net income raised to USD 300-330M (from prior USD 280-310M per TLB-009); Free Cash Flow USD 370-400M (TLB-011, page 1)
- 2026 investment programme: USD 120 million across three areas (talabat mart, talabat pro, new retail offerings); ~USD 25 million deployed during Q1 2026 (TLB-011, page 2)

## Retention-Relevant Information
- **"increased incentives to retain medium and high-value customers"** is explicitly cited as one of two factors (alongside higher talabat mart revenue share) behind the improved GMV-to-revenue conversion ratio in Q1 2026 — direct evidence that talabat is actively running retention-focused incentive programmes at the group level (TLB-011, page 2).
- talabat pro described again as being strengthened "as a multi-vertical engagement engine, building on its core free delivery offering with more exclusive benefits such as discounts across food and grocery verticals, on-time guarantee and priority customer support, dine-out discounts and content-streaming and ride-hailing partner services" (TLB-011, page 2).
- The 2026 "Everyday App" investment programme (USD 120 million) explicitly targets: (1) scaling talabat mart, (2) strengthening talabat pro, and (3) "building out new offerings in the retail space and additional services to our customers to cover their everyday needs" (TLB-011, page 2) — note this is an increase from the ">USD 100 million" figure disclosed in TLB-009, now specified precisely at USD 120 million and framed as three areas instead of two.
- Subscription fees as % of GMV rose from 0.5% (Q1 2025) to 1.0% (Q1 2026), a doubling of the subscription revenue intensity ratio (TLB-011, page 4), consistent with the >100% revenue growth figure in TLB-010.
- CEO Toon Gyssels quote frames the strategy as building "the app that consumers rely on every day" — an explicit retention/habitual-usage framing (TLB-011, page 3).

## Limitations
- No Egypt-specific GMV, revenue, or profit figures in this press release — Egypt is only quantified as part of the aggregated "non-GCC" segment (with Jordan and Iraq). For Egypt-only figures for the same quarter, see the companion financial statements (TLB-010), which report Egypt as a standalone segment.
- Figures are described as "pro forma" comparatives prepared "as if the acquisition of instashop... was completed on 1 January 2025," which differs from the Q4 2025 release's treatment (which excluded InstaShop from Q4 2025/FY2025 headline commentary) — comparability caveats apply across releases.
- No quantification of "increased incentives to retain medium and high-value customers" (no cost, discount rate, or customer-segment size disclosed).
- No talabat pro subscriber counts or Egypt-specific adoption data.
- Active customer figure ("over seven and a half million... as of December 2025") is dated to December 2025, not the Q1 2026 reporting period itself.
