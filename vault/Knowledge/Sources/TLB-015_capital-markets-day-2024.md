The Leading On-Demand Delivery Platform in MENA — talabat Capital Markets Event (updated 7 November 2024)

## Executive Summary
This 127-page Capital Markets Day deck is the single richest strategy document in the corpus. It positions talabat as "the leading on-demand delivery platform in MENA" across eight countries (UAE, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain, Egypt, Oman, Jordan, Iraq), built on a three-sided marketplace flywheel (customers, Partners, riders) reinforced by network effects. The deck covers: category/market opportunity (vastly underpenetrated foodservice and grocery & retail categories with strong secular tailwinds); detailed customer, partner, and rider value propositions; the Grocery & Retail vertical (tMarts and Local shops models) and the pending InstaShop acquisition; FinTech products (PostPaid, co-branded credit card); loyalty programmes (talabat pro, talabat Rewards); AdTech/advertising monetisation; technology and AI-driven personalisation; management/ESG; long-term growth levers; and full financial results (GMV, revenue, gross profit, Adjusted EBITDA, net income, free cash flow) plus 2024E/2025E/medium-term financial guidance. Critically for a customer-retention study, the deck reveals that talabat pro (the core loyalty/subscription programme) and PostPaid (BNPL) were, as of this deck, **not yet live in Egypt** — talabat pro was rolled out in 6 of 8 countries, explicitly excluding Egypt and Iraq, with expansion into Egypt described as a future growth lever. Egypt is also flagged as a disproportionately low-margin market: in 2023 Egypt represented ~6% of total revenue but only ~1% of total Adjusted EBITDA.

## Key Operational Metrics
- Active customers: ~2.2M (Jan-2020) → >6.0M (Jul-2024); CAGR ~25% (TLB-015, page 37)
- Active partners: ~15k (Jan-2019) → >64k (Jul-2024), +4.4x, including growth from ~0.3k to ~9.3k Local shops (TLB-015, page 7, 16, 46)
- Active riders: ~13k (Dec-2019) → ~115k (Jul-2024), 64% CAGR 2019-24 (TLB-015, page 17, 55)
- Average order frequency: 6.5x per active customer (July 2024) (TLB-015, page 13, 48)
- Restaurants: ~14.5k (Jan-2019) → ~55.1k (Jul-2024), 30% CAGR; plus 9.3k Local shops (Jul-2024) (TLB-015, page 40)
- >155 cuisines/categories; >64k active Partners; ~99% order success rate; average delivery time <30 minutes (TLB-015, page 14, 39)
- talabat pro penetration: 7.6% of user base as of Jul-2024 (vs ~48% global leading peer benchmark); live in 6 of 8 countries (all except Egypt and Iraq); frequency uplift 22%; customer retention uplift 32%; gross profit per customer uplift 136% (TLB-015, page 25, 77, 104)
- PostPaid penetration: 2.2% (Jul-2024); GMV share from debit card transactions 50%+; frequency uplift 14%; live in UAE, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain, Oman only (TLB-015, page 25, 74, 103)
- talabat Rewards adoption rate: rising trend Dec-2022 to Jul-2024, contributing to frequency uplift for first-time redeemers (TLB-015, page 79)
- AdTech annual revenue: $84M (2021) → $144M (2022) → $194M (2023), 52% CAGR 2021-23; AdTech revenue as % of GMV: 2.1% (2021) → 2.8% (2022) → 3.2% (2023), vs ~7% global leading peer benchmark (TLB-015, page 26, 50, 105)
- Partner ROAS (return on ad spend, CPC): 4.9x average (TLB-015, page 13, 47-48)
- InstaShop: founded Dubai 2015, operating in UAE and Egypt; ~8,800 partners across 20 sub-verticals; customers order 5x/month with 73% MoM retention rate (TLB-015, page 72)
- Multi-vertical (Food + Grocery & Retail) customers: 13.0 orders/month, $221/month spend vs Food-only customers: 3.8 orders/month, $54/month spend — a 3.4x frequency / 4.1x spend multiple (TLB-015, page 18, 61)
- 129 tMart stores; 9,300+ Local shops (TLB-015, page 18-19, 64)
- NPS: talabat NPS ~20% higher than regional peers' average; >60% of users intend to continue using talabat; 2/3 of users would not switch despite a price increase (TLB-015, page 44)

## Egypt Mentions
- Egypt is one of talabat's 8 MENA countries throughout the deck's country maps and footprint slides (TLB-015, pages 7, 12, 19, 32, 64).
- Timeline: "Otlob (Egypt) rebranded to talabat" — listed as a milestone in talabat's expansion history (year not isolable from the timeline graphic, positioned among 2015-2019-era milestones) (TLB-015, page 9).
- Grocery & Retail footprint slide groups EGYPT with JORDAN under the "Local shops" model footprint (as distinct from the tMarts-model countries UAE/Kuwait/Qatar/Bahrain/Oman/Iraq); the Local shops model is associated with a 2% EBITDA margin figure on the same slide, versus an 8% EBITDA margin cited for the "best-in-class tMarts country" (TLB-015, page 19, 64) — the slide's layout makes the country-to-margin mapping somewhat ambiguous in extracted text; treat the 2%/8% figures as describing the two business models generally rather than confirmed Egypt-specific EBITDA.
- Non-GCC region (Egypt, Jordan, Iraq) addressable population 71.1M; monthly orders per capita 0.13x vs GCC 1.28x — Egypt is part of this pooled non-GCC figure, not broken out individually (TLB-015, page 12, 24, 32, 34).
- Egypt included among survey countries for the consumer funnel/NPS study: "Users surveyed on restaurant variety, delivery time, rider behaviour, customer support, discounts, prices, delivery fees and loyalty programs in Kuwait, UAE, Egypt, Qatar, and Bahrain" (TLB-015, page 39, 44).
- Partner-growth slide: "Egypt (Otlob rebranded to talabat in 2020)" is compared against "Iraq (launched in 2021)" on share of GMV from partners acquired in 2023 and current/total inventory available; Egypt's total inventory figure appears to be "#80k+" (exact pairing of the two other figures — #3.6k and #19k+ — to Egypt vs. Iraq is not fully disambiguated in the extracted text/table) (TLB-015, page 41).
- Rider survey conducted "across UAE, Kuwait, Qatar and Egypt" for earnings/safety benchmarking (TLB-015, page 56-57).
- Rider training app "tcamp" is "available in Egypt, Oman, UAE, Bahrain, Kuwait, and Oman [sic]" (TLB-015, page 58).
- InstaShop "primarily operates in the groceries and retail space in the UAE and Egypt" — founded in Dubai in 2015, ~$487M GMV FY23, positive EBITDA margin, ~8,800 partners across 20 sub-verticals, customers ordering 5x/month with a 73% MoM retention rate (TLB-015, page 72).
- talabat pro: "6/8 countries rolled out" with footnote clarifying "All countries except Egypt and Iraq" — i.e., as of this deck, talabat's core loyalty/subscription programme was **not available in Egypt** (TLB-015, page 77).
- Growth-lever slide: "Expanding loyalty programme in other countries beyond Jordan" is listed as a forward roadmap item for the non-GCC region — implying that among non-GCC countries (Egypt, Jordan, Iraq), only Jordan had the loyalty programme live at deck date, and expansion into Egypt/Iraq was still a planned initiative (TLB-015, page 34).
- talabat pro country chart again lists Egypt separately under "Planned expansion" (alongside Iraq implicitly) versus countries where it is "Live in 6 out of 8 countries" (TLB-015, page 104).
- PostPaid: "Live in" UAE, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain, Oman; "Planned expansion" footnote: "Full expansion of PostPaid offering planned in other non-GCC countries subject to regulatory approvals" — implying PostPaid, like talabat pro, was not yet available in Egypt (TLB-015, page 103).
- Growth-lever slide on non-GCC opportunity: "Egypt and Iraq are emerging countries with high growth rates. Currently only present in ~19% and ~42% of larger cities in Iraq and Egypt" — i.e., talabat's city-level footprint covered only ~42% of Egyptian cities with population >100k as of the deck date, a stated geographic-expansion opportunity (TLB-015, page 101).
- **Financial risk disclosure (most quantified Egypt data point in the document):** "talabat's exposure to foreign currency risk is primarily linked to Egypt segment – In 2023, Egypt represented ~6% of total revenue and only ~1% of total Adj. EBITDA." (TLB-015, page 116)
- Cairo is one of talabat's two "tech-hubs" (with Dubai) — mentioned in the IPO announcement companion documents (TLB-023) rather than explicitly in this deck's tech-employee slide, though this deck references ">440 tech employees (FTEs)" without a city breakdown (TLB-015, page 7, 90).

## Retention-Relevant Information
- **talabat pro (core loyalty/subscription product):** described as "our premium subscription fostering frequency and retention." Retention uplift 32% (customers retained on talabat 12 months after joining talabat pro vs. lookalike non-members); frequency uplift 22%; gross profit per customer uplift 136%. Live in 6 of 8 countries — **excluding Egypt and Iraq** — with expansion into remaining countries and deepening of the value proposition flagged as forward-looking growth levers (roadmap item #4 under "Technology" and lever "C. Turbo-charging customer loyalty" under Long-Term Value Creation). Global leading peer benchmark cited at ~48% penetration vs talabat's 7.6% — implying "significant headroom for growth from user base" (TLB-015, pages 25, 77-78, 89, 104).
- **PostPaid (BNPL FinTech):** delivers 14% frequency uplift by "simplifying payments," profitable product, smooths "pay-week cyclicality." Live only in UAE, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain, Oman; non-GCC (including Egypt) expansion is "subject to regulatory approvals" (TLB-015, pages 25, 74, 103).
- **Co-branded credit card (ADCB partnership, UAE only):** drives "higher loyalty" via unlimited free delivery, welcome bonus, cashback tiers (35% back on talabat orders for first 10 transactions, 1.25% thereafter) — framed explicitly as fostering "customer retention with attractive offers" (TLB-015, page 75).
- **talabat Rewards:** points-based programme "encouraging consistent behaviour, increasing loyalty and reducing churn"; adoption rate rising from Dec-2022 to Jul-2024, with measurable order-frequency uplift for first-time redeemers (TLB-015, page 79).
- **Multi-vertical adoption as a retention lever:** customers using both Food and Grocery & Retail verticals order 13.0 times/month and spend $221/month, vs 3.8 times/month and $54/month for Food-only customers (3.4x frequency, 4.1x spend) — "Expanding from Food into Grocery & Retail increases frequency, boosts spending and drives platform growth" (TLB-015, page 18, 61).
- **Cohort analysis:** GMV per average customer grows ~4x within 5 years of acquisition (2019 cohort); cumulative orders per cohort show a consistent, compounding increase in order frequency over time, with "COVID peak performance...maintained across our cohorts" and "more recent cohorts generate higher GMV than previous cohorts" (TLB-015, pages 8, 15, 38).
- **AI/ML-driven personalisation directly tied to retention:** "talabat's machine learning models allow it to introduce and cross-sell verticals, as well as initiatives such as talabat pro and talabat Rewards, at the right time for customers to benefit from" (echoed from TLB-023); "Predictive AI Recommendations" generate $14M+ p.a. incremental EBITDA, with "6 orders needed to create personalisation"; "Tailored recommendations," "Targeted offers," and "Cross-vertical synergies" are named as core personalisation levers "enhancing lifetime value" (TLB-015, pages 21, 84).
- **NPS / brand loyalty evidence:** talabat NPS ~20% higher than regional peers; >60% of users "intend to continue to use talabat in the future"; 2/3 of users "will not switch to other players despite price increase"; ~70% of users find talabat "more innovative vs other players" (TLB-015, page 44).
- **Customer contact rate** (proxy for service-quality-driven retention) decreased from a rebased 100 (2020) to 57 (H1 2024), alongside improving on-time delivery rate (67%→78%) and average delivery time (37min→31min) (TLB-015, page 42).
- **Forward roadmap explicitly linking AI/personalisation to retention:** "AI driven recommendations in new form factors," "Optimising timing, placement, and incentives of vertical and product cross-sells," and "Expanding our talabat pro subscriber base" (including "Launching in remaining categories" and "Deepening value proposition") are listed together as the top two of five forward technology priorities (TLB-015, page 89).
- **Non-GCC / Egypt-specific retention gap:** the loyalty-programme roadmap explicitly frames "Expanding loyalty programme in other countries beyond Jordan" as a growth lever for the non-GCC region, directly implying Egypt is a targeted future market for talabat pro (TLB-015, page 34).

## Limitations
This is an investor/strategy presentation, not audited financial statements — many figures are non-IFRS ("management reporting," "Adjusted EBITDA," "Adjusted Net Income," combined/carve-out bases) and are explicitly flagged as forward-looking/non-verified. Egypt is never broken out as a standalone P&L segment; it is folded into "non-GCC" (with Jordan and Iraq) for GMV/revenue/orders-per-capita metrics, with only two genuinely Egypt-specific quantified data points in the whole 127-page deck: (1) the ~6% revenue / ~1% Adj. EBITDA contribution in 2023, and (2) the ~42% larger-city footprint coverage. Some table-formatted slides (e.g., page 41's partner-inventory comparison between Egypt and Iraq, and page 112's revenue-mix table) lost column/row alignment in PDF-to-text extraction, so exact figure-to-label pairings for those specific tables should be treated as approximate and cross-checked against the source PDF if precision is required. The deck predates the InstaShop acquisition close (expected 2025) and predates the actual IPO (which priced 29 November 2024, after this deck's most recent update). No Q3/Q4 2024 or FY2024 actuals are included — only guidance ranges.
