# TLB-024 — Talabat Holding plc Offering Size Increased to Address Significant International and Regional Investor Demand

## Executive Summary
This short regulatory announcement discloses that talabat increased its IPO offering size mid-bookbuild, from 3,493,236,093 shares (15% of total issued share capital) to 4,657,648,125 shares (20%), in response to "significant international and regional investor demand" in the Second Tranche (Qualified Investor Offering), including sizeable anchor orders from global long-only and technology-sector investors. The additional 1,164,412,032 shares were allocated entirely to the Second Tranche. The Offer Price Range (AED 1.50–1.60) remained unchanged, implying a revised offering size of between AED 7.0bn (c. US$1.9bn) and AED 7.5bn (c. US$2.0bn) and an unchanged implied market capitalisation range of c. AED 34.9bn–37.3bn (c. US$9.5bn–10.1bn). All other IPO mechanics (subscription close dates, pricing announcement date of 29 November 2024, expected Admission around 10 December 2024) remained unchanged.

## Key Financial Metrics
- Revised Offering size: 4,657,648,125 Shares (20% of total issued share capital), up from 3,493,236,093 Shares (15%) (TLB-024, page 2)
- Revised expected offering proceeds: between AED 7.0 billion (c. US$1.9 billion) and AED 7.5 billion (c. US$2.0 billion) (TLB-024, page 2)
- Implied market capitalisation at listing (unchanged): between c. AED 34.9 billion (c. US$9.5 billion) and c. AED 37.3 billion (c. US$10.1 billion) (TLB-024, page 2)
- Additional shares allocated: 1,164,412,032, wholly to the Qualified Investor Offering (Second Tranche) (TLB-024, page 2)
