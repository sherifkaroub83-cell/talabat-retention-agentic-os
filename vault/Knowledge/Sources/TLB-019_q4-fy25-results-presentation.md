# TLB-019 — talabat Q4 & FY 2025 Results Presentation

## Title
Q4 & FY 2025 Results — "The Leading On-Demand Delivery Platform in MENA"

## Citation

## Publisher
talabat Holding plc (Investor Relations)

## Executive Summary
This 29-page deck, presented by new CEO Toon Gyssels, CFO Khaled Alfakesh and IR's Shadi Salman, covers talabat's Q4 and full-year 2025 results and sets FY2026 guidance. FY2025 (talabat-only, excl. InstaShop) GMV reached USD 9.5bn (+28% y/y cFX), Management Revenue USD 3.92bn (+33% y/y cFX), Adjusted EBITDA USD 615mn (6.5% of GMV), and net income USD 464mn (4.9% of GMV); full-year dividends reached USD 421mn, or 90% of reported net income, exceeding the minimum USD 400mn guidance (p.5). This is the single most Egypt-relevant document in the batch: it states explicitly that "**Egypt is now our third largest market by GMV, after UAE and Kuwait, and our largest user base**" (p.14). It contains a substantial, dedicated retention/loyalty analytics section — including M1 retention by customer segment, high-value customer stickiness, and quantified talabat pro retention/frequency uplift (p.9-11) — plus FY2026 guidance of 11-14% GMV growth (materially decelerating from 28%) alongside a >USD 100mn/USD 175mn investment programme to build talabat into "the everyday app," including ~USD 45mn tMart capex (p.11, p.18-19).

## Key Financial Metrics
- FY2025 GMV (talabat-only): USD 9.5bn (9,421mn), +28% y/y cFX / +27% y/y nominal reported as USD 9,421mn is the talabat-only figure and USD 10.2bn (27% y/y) is the pro-forma-including-instashop figure (p.5, p.13)
- Q4'25 GMV (talabat-only, cFX): USD 2.5bn, +21% y/y (p.5, p.13)
- FY2025 Management Revenue (talabat-only): USD 3.92bn (3,876mn), +33% y/y cFX; pro-forma incl. instashop 32% y/y, USD 4.04bn (p.5, p.13)
- Q4'25 Management Revenue (talabat-only, cFX): USD 1.0bn (1,039mn), +26% y/y (p.5, p.13)
- FY2025 Adj. EBITDA (talabat-only): USD 615mn, 6.5% of GMV, in line with guidance; pro-forma incl. instashop 6.2%/USD 625mn (p.5)
- Q4'25 Adj. EBITDA: USD 156mn, 6.3% of GMV (p.5, p.15)
- FY2025 Net income (talabat-only): USD 464mn, 4.9% of GMV (near guidance, <2% off low end); on constant-currency basis reached USD 469mn; pro-forma incl. instashop 4.6%/USD 470mn (p.5, p.16)
- Q4'25 Net income (talabat-only): USD 123mn, 5.0% of GMV; Adjusted Net Income USD 124mn (5.0% of GMV) (p.5, p.16, p.24)
- FY2025 Adj. FCF (talabat-only): USD 559mn, 5.9% of GMV (near guidance); pro-forma incl. instashop 5.6%/USD 567mn (p.5, p.15)
- FY2025 dividends: total USD 421mn (90% of reported net income), exceeding min. USD 400mn guidance — comprising interim USD 202mn (paid Oct 2025) + final USD 219mn (subject to AGM approval) (p.5, p.16)
- FY2025 vs. original & revised guidance: GMV growth 28% actual vs. 17-18% original / 27-29% revised (in line, mid-range); Revenue growth 33% actual vs. 18-20% original / 29-32% revised (exceeded); Adj. EBITDA margin 6.5% actual = 6.5% revised guidance (in line); Net income margin 4.9% actual vs. 5.0% revised (near, <2% off low end); Adj. FCF margin 5.9% actual vs. 6.0% revised (near); Dividends USD 421mn vs. min. USD 400mn (exceeded) (p.5)
- FY2026 guidance (talabat + instashop pro forma basis): GMV growth 11-14% cFX (USD 11.2-11.5bn); Revenue growth 14-17% cFX (USD 4.4-4.5bn) — note talabat moves to IFRS Revenue reporting only from 2026; Adj. EBITDA USD 510-540mn (4.4-4.8% of GMV); Net income USD 280-310mn (2.4-2.8% of GMV); Free Cash Flow USD 370-400mn (3.2-3.6% of GMV); Dividends 90% of net income = USD 252-279mn (p.18)
- Adj. EBITDA 2026 margin bridge: 6.0% (Q4'25 constant margin) → 5.3% after G&R product-mix shift (-0.2%) → 4.6% after "Everyday App"/tMart investment (-0.7%, ~USD 75mn) → after Food-leadership investment (-0.5%, ~USD 55mn), landing at guided long-term range (p.19)
- GMV by geography FY2025: GCC USD 7,702mn (+22% y/y, 81% contribution); Non-GCC USD 1,719mn (+57% y/y, 18% contribution) (p.14)

## Key Operational Metrics
- Active partners: 84k (Dec'25) vs 69k (Dec'24), +22% y/y (p.7)
- Active riders: 157k (Dec'25) vs 123k (Dec'24), +27% y/y (p.7)
- Partner-funded savings: $159mn (Q4'25) vs $138mn (Q4'24), +15% y/y; $588mn full-year 2025 (p.7)
- GMV share of multi-vertical customers: 73% (Dec'25) vs 68% (Dec'24), +5.4pp (p.7); multi-vertical customers "now exceed one-third of the base" (p.7)
- GMV share of talabat pro subscribers: 25% ("1.9x growth"), underpinning "one-quarter of the MAUs" (p.7)
- Advertising Revenue Margin: 3.5% of GMV (Q4'25) vs 3.3% (Q4'24), +0.2pp; NMR (non-commission revenue) now >20% of AdRev; tMart NMR ~7% of tMart sales in UAE (p.7)
- Platform MAUs: 6.4mn (Q4'24) → 7.6mn (Q4'25), +19% y/y, +1.2mn customers added in 2025 (p.8)
- Platform customer frequency: 6.7 (Q4'24) → 6.8 (Q4'25); Top 5% customer frequency: 36.1 (Q4'24) → 37.9 (Q4'25) (p.8)
- High-value customers (competitive markets: UAE, Kuwait, Qatar): 39% of customer base but 81% of GMV; non-high-value 52% of customers/16% of GMV; new customers 9% of customers/3% of GMV (Q4'25) (p.9)
- Q4'25 y/y M1 retention change by segment (competitive markets): high-value +6%, non-high-value -4%, new ~0% (p.9)
- M1 retention uplift from loyalty/multi-verticality: mono-vertical subscriber +16pp retention vs. mono-vertical non-subscriber; multi-vertical subscriber +20pp retention vs. mono-vertical non-subscriber (Q4 2025 average) (p.10)
- talabat pro: +26% retention uplift, +28% frequency uplift vs. non-pro customers (lookalike-adjusted) (p.11)
- tMart: ~47% YoY GMV growth (Q4'25 vs Q4'24), <1.5% TAC (Total Addressable Category) penetration per Redseer analysis (p.11)
- GMV by vertical FY2025: Food USD 6,652mn (+20% y/y); G&R USD 2,768mn (+47% y/y, 32%... G&R % shown as 29%/32% across periods) (p.14)

## Countries Mentioned
UAE, Kuwait, Qatar (explicitly named as talabat's "competitive markets," p.9). Egypt named explicitly multiple times (p.14). No full 8-country list is repeated in this deck's body text, though GCC / non-GCC segment framing persists.

## Egypt Mentions
- "**Non-GCC segment grew much faster. Egypt is now our third largest market by GMV, after UAE and Kuwait, and our largest user base**" (p.14) — the most significant, specific Egypt data point across this whole batch of documents: Egypt ranked #3 by GMV and #1 by user base among all talabat markets.
- Non-GCC GMV FY2025 reached USD 1,719mn (+57% y/y), of which Egypt is presumably the largest single-country contributor (implied but not separately broken out) (p.14).
- Adjusted Net Income / Net Income bridge references "Delivery Hero Egypt SAE" shareholder-loan-related FX items in the standard APM footnote (p.27-28).

## Retention-Relevant Information
- Dedicated retention section: "In 2025, we expanded our customer base by 1.2mn and deepened engagement across customer tiers" with platform MAU and frequency trends by cohort, including "Top 5%" power users (p.8).
- "High value customers remain sticky and continue to increase their frequency, despite competitive pressure" — in competitive markets (UAE, Kuwait, Qatar), high-value customers are 39% of the base but drive 81% of GMV, and saw +6% y/y M1 retention and +1% y/y frequency even as non-high-value customers saw -4% M1 retention (p.9).
- "Loyalty and multi-verticality as key retention drivers": M1 retention is +16pp higher for mono-vertical talabat pro subscribers vs. non-subscribers, and +20pp higher for multi-vertical pro subscribers vs. non-subscribers (p.10).
- talabat pro delivers a measured +26% retention uplift and +28% frequency uplift vs. lookalike non-pro customers (p.11).
- ">$100mn of investment in 2026" (part of a $175mn Board-approved programme) explicitly aimed at "Doubling down on building talabat as the everyday app," including "Deepening the value customers receive from our subscription proposition" (p.11).
- tMart (grocery dark stores) framed as the "fastest growing vertical" with "huge headroom to penetrate Grocery TAC" (<1.5% penetration) — an explicit growth/retention lever via multi-verticality (p.11).
- Ecosystem products like talabat pro are said to "underpin one-quarter of the MAUs, translating to higher engagement & better retention"; multi-vertical customers "now exceed one-third of the base, indicating higher lifetime value and engagement" (p.7).

## Limitations
- Despite naming Egypt as the #3 GMV market and #1 user-base market, this document still provides no standalone Egypt GMV, revenue, or customer-count figures — Egypt remains embedded in the non-GCC segment total (USD 1,719mn FY2025).
- Retention/M1-retention analytics on p.9 are explicitly scoped to "talabat's competitive markets... UAE, Kuwait, and Qatar" — these figures should not be assumed to apply to Egypt.
- Figures are talabat-only (excl. instashop) unless noted as "pro-forma including instashop"; FY2026 guidance is on a different (instashop-inclusive) base than FY2025 actuals, complicating direct comparability.
- Presentation summarizes pro forma (unaudited/un-reviewed) financial statements; disclaimer text on the final page appears to reference "Q3 2025 Financial Statements" (likely a template carry-over error) rather than Q4/FY2025 (p.28).

## Potential Business Questions this document can answer
- How does talabat rank Egypt among its markets by GMV and user base, and what does that imply about Egypt's strategic priority?
- What quantified retention/frequency uplift does talabat pro and multi-vertical adoption deliver, and how might these mechanisms translate to an Egypt-specific loyalty programme design?
- How does talabat segment and treat "high-value" vs. "non-high-value" vs. "new" customers, and what retention trends does each show?
- What does talabat's FY2026 investment programme (~USD 175mn, incl. ~USD 45mn tMart capex) imply for the pace and direction of future multi-vertical/loyalty investment that could extend to Egypt?
- How did FY2025 actual results compare to original and revised guidance, and what is the FY2026 outlook?
