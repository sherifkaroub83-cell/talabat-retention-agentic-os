## Executive Summary
This is talabat Holding PLC's third quarterly IFRS interim financial statement, covering the three-month period ended 30 September 2025 and the cumulative period since inception (3 September 2024) to 30 September 2025 (TLB-007, page 1). Q3 2025 revenue was USD 1,004.2M with net profit of USD 120.9M (TLB-007, page 5). Cumulative revenue reached USD 3,620.0M with cumulative net profit of USD 483.4M (TLB-007, page 5). Total assets stood at USD 1,590.5M and total equity at USD 575.2M as of 30 September 2025 (TLB-007, page 4). The Board declared two dividends during/around the period: a USD 110.02M interim dividend (Q4 2024 results, paid) and a further USD 202.16M interim dividend proposed 22 September 2025 (H1 2025 results, paid after period end) — the latter appears as "Dividends payable" of USD 202,159,050 on the balance sheet (TLB-007, pages 4, 22). As in prior quarters, the Group reports on a "GCC" vs. "Other segments" basis; Egypt is named as part of the MENA footprint and as the incorporation country for one InstaShop entity and for "Delivery Hero Egypt SAE," but is not broken out financially in this document (TLB-007, pages 8, 9, 25). This source was a scanned PDF with no text layer and was OCR'd via Tesseract; most pages OCR'd cleanly, but page 6 (statement of changes in equity) is substantially garbled/illegible — see Limitations.
