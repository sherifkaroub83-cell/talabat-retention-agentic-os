# TLB-001 — talabat Annual Report 2024

## Title
Integrated Annual Report 2024 — "An appetite for SUCCESS" (talabat Holding PLC combined Strategic report, Governance report and Financial statements for FY2024)

## Citation
TLB-001 — talabat Holding PLC, "Integrated Annual Report 2024", 4 April 2025 (published 3 April 2025 per financial calendar; PDF creation date 17 April 2025), Input_Data/01_Annual_Reports/20250404_Talabat-Annual-Report-2024-En.pdf

## Publication Date
Report covers FY2024 (year ended 31 December 2024); published/creation-dated 17 April 2025 (PDF metadata); the report's own financial calendar lists "Annual Report 2024" as due 3 April 2025 (TLB-001, p.138).

## Publisher
talabat Holding PLC (registered office: talabat HQ, Office B06 City Walk, Al Safa Street, Dubai, UAE) (TLB-001, p.139)

## Executive Summary
This is talabat's first Integrated Annual Report as a listed company, covering FY2024, the year of its December 2024 IPO on the Dubai Financial Market (DFM) — described as the largest global technology IPO of the year (TLB-001, p.8, p.11). talabat operates as the leading on-demand food, grocery and retail delivery marketplace in eight MENA markets (UAE, Kuwait, Qatar, Egypt, Bahrain, Oman, Jordan, Iraq), serving over 6.5 million Active Customers, 68,000+ Active Partners and 123,000+ Active Riders as of December 2024 (TLB-001, p.4). FY2024 GMV reached USD 7.4 billion (+23% YoY), Management Revenue reached USD 3.0 billion (+32% YoY), and Adjusted EBITDA rose 55% to USD 497 million (6.7% of GMV) (TLB-001, p.25). The report details talabat's three-pillar growth strategy (deeper category penetration, turbo-charging customer loyalty, deeper vendor/supplier partnerships), its loyalty ecosystem (talabat pro, talabat rewards, DineOut Deals, PostPaid, co-branded credit card), its AdTech and technology/AI capabilities, full financial statements (proforma and audited consolidated interim), governance structure post-IPO, sustainability framework, and principal risk factors. Egypt is explicitly called out multiple times as a strategically important but currency-volatile non-GCC market where talabat pro was newly launched (February 2025) and where category leadership is more than 10x the nearest competitor (TLB-001, p.5).

## Key Financial Metrics
- GMV: USD 7,428mn in 2024 vs USD 6,062mn in 2023, +23% YoY (TLB-001, p.26)
- GMV by segment: GCC USD 6,332mn (+20% YoY, 85% of GMV); non-GCC (Egypt, Jordan, Iraq) USD 1,096mn (+42% YoY, surpassed USD 1bn for first time) (TLB-001, p.26)
- GMV by vertical: Food Vertical USD 5,542mn (+16% YoY, 75% of GMV); Grocery & Retail Vertical USD 1,886mn (+47% YoY, 25% of GMV, up from 21% in 2023) (TLB-001, p.26)
- Management Revenue: USD 2,956mn in 2024 vs USD 2,242mn in 2023, +32% YoY; 40% of GMV (2023: 37%) (TLB-001, p.27)
- Revenue (IFRS): USD 2,871,862,270 in 2024 vs USD 2,175,013,998 in 2023 (proforma basis) (TLB-001, p.27, p.93)
- Revenue composition: Commission fees USD 1,062mn (+25%); Subscription fee & Other Income USD 952mn (+44%); Delivery & Service fees USD 696mn (+29%); Advertising and listing fees USD 246mn (+27%) (TLB-001, p.27)
- Gross profit: USD 915mn in 2024 (+32% YoY), 12.3% of GMV (2023: 11.4%) (TLB-001, p.25-28)
- Adjusted EBITDA: USD 497mn in 2024 (+55% YoY), 6.7% of GMV (2023: 5.3%) (TLB-001, p.25, p.28)
- Net income: USD 346mn in 2024 (+64% YoY), 4.7% of GMV (2023: 3.5%) (TLB-001, p.31)
- Adjusted Net Income: USD 393mn in 2024 (+53% YoY), 5.3% of GMV (2023: 4.2%) (TLB-001, p.25, p.31)
- Adjusted Free Cash Flow: USD 462mn in 2024 (+54% YoY), 6.2% of GMV; Cash Conversion Ratio 93% (2023: 94%) (TLB-001, p.25, p.30)
- Net cash: USD 322mn (+19% vs end-2023); cash and short-term deposits USD 419mn; zero external debt (TLB-001, p.25, p.31)
- Effective tax rate: 5.5% in 2024 (2023: 3.6%); expected to rise to ~15% from 2025 under BEPS 2.0 Pillar 2 / DMTT (TLB-001, p.29)
- Corporate income tax by country: Egypt 22.5% (flat 2023-2025e); Jordan 21% (incl. 1% national contribution tax); Iraq 15%; UAE 9%→15% (2025e); Kuwait/Bahrain 0%→15% (2025e); Qatar 10%→15% (2025e); Oman flat 15% (TLB-001, p.29)
- Dividend: Board proposed USD 110mn dividend for Q4 2024 (annualised yield >4.5%); target of at least USD 400mn total dividends for 2025, then 90% net income payout ratio target thereafter (TLB-001, p.8, p.67)
- AdTech revenue: USD 246mn in 2024, 3.3% of GMV (2023: 3.2%), +27% YoY; global benchmark target ~7% of GMV medium-term (TLB-001, p.13, p.20-21)
- Partner-funded savings: ~USD 450mn in 2024 (6.1% of GMV) vs USD 308mn in 2023 (5.1% of GMV) (TLB-001, p.15, p.21)
- Blended commission rate: 16.2% of GMV excluding tMarts GMV (2023: 15.6%) (TLB-001, p.27)
- Delivery & service fee monetisation: 9.4% of GMV (2023: 8.9%) (TLB-001, p.27)
- Delivery costs: 15.3% of GMV (2023: 14.8%) (TLB-001, p.27)
- CARC (Customer Acquisition and Retention Costs): USD 103mn or 1.4% of GMV in 2024 (2023: USD 89mn or 1.5% of GMV) (TLB-001, p.28)
- FY2024 GMV CAGR 2015-2024: 48% (TLB-001, p.7, p.9)
- Full-year consolidated interim P&L (post-restructuring entity, 3 Sept–31 Dec 2024 only): Revenue USD 801mn; Net profit USD 138mn (TLB-001, p.96, p.100)

- Active Partners: >68,000 (December 2024), including >10,000 Local Shops; up from >15,000 Partners (incl. >300 Local Shops) in January 2019 (TLB-001, p.4, p.15)

- Local Shops SKUs: over 700,000 SKUs across >10,000 Partners (TLB-001, p.17)

- Category leadership (relative food-service share vs. next closest peer, H1 2024): Egypt 10x+, Qatar 10x+, Kuwait 8x+, Jordan/UAE 4x+, Bahrain/Iraq 3x+, Oman 2x+ (TLB-001, p.5)

## Egypt Mentions
- Egypt is one of talabat's eight countries of operation (UAE, Kuwait, Qatar, Egypt, Bahrain, Oman, Jordan, Iraq) (TLB-001, p.2, p.9).
- talabat's relative food-service category share in Egypt is more than 10x its next closest peer — the highest multiple of any of the 8 countries alongside Qatar (TLB-001, p.5).
- Company timeline: talabat's "Launch of own-delivery service" coincided with "expansion to Jordan and Egypt"; later, talabat acquired Otlob (Egypt) as part of the Carriage/Otlob acquisitions; Otlob subsequently rebranded to talabat in Egypt (TLB-001, p.7).
- Chairperson's statement: "Inflationary pressures and currency fluctuations in some of our markets (for example, Egypt) also required agile pricing and cost management strategies" during 2024 (TLB-001, p.9).
- CEO's statement: talabat operates across "the GCC excluding Saudi Arabia... as well as Egypt, Jordan and Iraq" as its two key MENA segments (TLB-001, p.11).
- "At the time of writing, talabat pro was recently launched in Egypt, marking a significant expansion with the programme now available in seven of the eight countries in which we operate" (TLB-001, p.11).
- Market survey: the 80%+ "users rank talabat as #1" preference statistic was based on a survey covering "Kuwait, UAE, Egypt, Qatar and Bahrain" (August 2024) (TLB-001, p.15).
- talabat pro "has seen strong adoption across talabat's markets, including Egypt, where it was recently launched in February 2025, with a notable increase in customer retention and order frequency among subscribers" (TLB-001, p.19).
- 2024 strategy highlights: "talabat pro now in all markets except Iraq with adoption rate more than doubling during 2024 (foundational work was also carried out during the year for successful launch in Egypt in early 2025)" (TLB-001, p.20).
- Future focus (loyalty pillar): "Grow talabat pro subscription base in GCC and elsewhere (e.g. Egypt, launched February 2025), leveraging early traction..." (TLB-001, p.21).
- Future focus (category penetration pillar): "Integrate instashop (a leading online grocery e-marketplace acquired in February 2025 and operating in the UAE and Egypt) within the talabat ecosystem, potentially cross-listing partners and extending talabat pro benefits to instashop users" (TLB-001, p.21).
- Financial review: "In the non-GCC segment, which is growing at a higher pace relative to GCC countries, albeit from a much lower base, Egypt's performance is notably strong and profitable at an Adjusted EBITDA level" (TLB-001, p.26).
- Corporate income tax rate in Egypt: flat 22.5% for 2023, 2024 and 2025e (highest CIT rate among talabat's 8 markets) (TLB-001, p.29).
- Adjusted EBITDA/Adjusted Net Income normalise for "an intercompany shareholder loan to talabat's Egyptian entity in EGP currency, which was written down due to the devaluation of the currency in April 2024" (TLB-001, p.31; also glossary definition of Adjusted Net Income, p.137, and consolidated financial statements notes, p.135 referencing "unrealised foreign exchange loss from shareholder loan liability in Delivery Hero Egypt SAE").
- Risk factors: "challenges such as infrastructure limitations, electricity outages and unreliable networks – particularly in regions like Egypt – may hinder [technology adoption/scale] growth" (TLB-001, p.34, Risk A4 — Market adoption and technological evolution risks).
- SME empowerment: talabat "facilitated Partner growth sessions across several markets, including the launch of a new academy in Egypt with The German Chamber of Commerce, training over 200 SMEs by July 2024" (TLB-001, p.46).
- "In the UAE, our collaboration with the Khalifa Fund empowers Emirati entrepreneurs... Similarly, in Egypt, our partnership with the Micro, Small and Medium Enterprise Development Authority (MSMEDA) strengthens the SME ecosystem, promoting sustainable growth and resilience" (TLB-001, p.46).
- Rider training: "78,000 riders across Bahrain, Egypt, Kuwait, Oman and the UAE completed online courses through the 'tcamp' digital learning platform" during the year (TLB-001, p.49).
- Charitable partnerships: "In Egypt, we support impactful initiatives through the Egypt Food Bank, Misr El Kheir foundation and a number of other organisations working to address food insecurity and social welfare" (TLB-001, p.55).
- Corporate restructuring: the pre-IPO restructuring "consolidate[d] the Ultimate Parent Company's business in the MENA region, which includes... Bahrain, Iraq, Egypt, and Jordan" alongside the GCC countries (TLB-001, p.104).
- Legal entities: "Delivery Hero Egypt SAE" (Egypt-incorporated, import/export, grocery, central market, non-food supermarket, general trade office, commission agent, 100% owned) and "Delivery Hero Dmart Egypt LLC" (Egypt-incorporated, general trade and distribution, 100% owned) are named among the Group's material consolidated subsidiaries (TLB-001, p.105-106).
- Segment reporting note: "the Non-GCC countries (Egypt, Jordan, and Iraq) do not individually meet the 10% threshold for revenue, assets, or profits. As such, these countries do not qualify as reportable segments, but are disclosed under other segments" (TLB-001, p.117).
- Functional currencies of Group entities include "Egyptian Pound (EGP)" among others (TLB-001, p.128, financial risk management note).
- Glossary: instashop "is a leading online grocery e-marketplace operating in the UAE and Egypt", acquired by talabat effective 25 February 2025 (TLB-001, p.137).
- Glossary: MENA is defined as the "Region including countries like UAE, Saudi Arabia, Egypt and others" (TLB-001, p.137).
- Adjusted Net Income glossary definition: excludes "foreign exchange income (loss) (mainly related to non-cash unrealised foreign exchange loss from shareholder loan liability in Delivery Hero Egypt SAE)" (TLB-001, p.138).

## Retention-Relevant Information
- Strategic pillar 2 of 3, "Turbo-charging customer loyalty," is explicitly built around loyalty/subscription/FinTech: "Build an ecosystem of services around our core marketplace to deepen customer loyalty and engagement... increasing the touchpoints and daily relevance for users, increasing order frequency, lifetime value and user stickiness" (TLB-001, p.21).
- talabat pro (flagship subscription loyalty programme): launched March 2022 in UAE; by 2024 available in all countries except Iraq (and, per CEO letter, "now available in seven of the eight countries" following its Egypt launch); 2.1x increase in adoption rate in 2024; 24% uplift in conversion rates through the newly redesigned app; September 2024 subscribers showed a 28% uplift in order frequency vs. lookalike non-subscriber users (matched on frequency, basket size, tenure) (TLB-001, p.11-12, p.19-20).
- talabat rewards (non-subscription, points-based loyalty programme): earn/redeem points within 60 days; gamified raffles; in September 2024, customers who redeemed points for the first time showed a >15% increase in order frequency within 30 days (TLB-001, p.12, p.19).
- talabat DineOut Deals: launched May 2024 in UAE; no subscription required; unlimited use; enhances talabat pro value via subscriber-only extra discounts; drives foot traffic for restaurant partners (TLB-001, p.12, p.19).
- talabat PostPaid (FinTech): launched UAE 2022; allows payment deferral up to 30 days at no extra cost; drove a 14% increase in order frequency (measured Nov 2021–Jan 2024, comparing frequency 3 months after adoption vs. before); reduces order cyclicality around pay-week; profitable; PostPaid adoption grew 2.6x YoY in 2024 (TLB-001, p.13, p.19-21).
- Co-branded credit card with ADCB (UAE, since August 2023): welcome bonus and cashback paid in talabat Pay credit, plus free delivery via a tailored talabat pro subscription; "strengthened customer retention and engagement"; significant uptake during 2024 (TLB-001, p.13, p.19-21).
- App redesign (Q4 2024, centred on hyper-personalisation): talabat pro acquisition rates up 24% and grocery acquisitions up 2% in the short time since launch; ML models need ~6 orders to develop personalised recommendations; personalisation estimated to deliver USD 14mn+ incremental EBITDA p.a. (TLB-001, p.9, p.22-24).
- AI-driven logistics and targeting: "AI-driven logistics improvements have enhanced delivery times and optimised resource allocation" (CEO statement); AdTech uses "integration of AI-driven targeting and automated ad-buying processes" to optimise efficiency and lower CAC while amplifying Partner visibility (TLB-001, p.10, p.13).
- Partner retention: ~99% Partner retention among pro-participating vendors in 2024, with ~USD 450mn in Partner-funded savings cited as evidence of merchant ROI (TLB-001, p.21).
- 2024 highlights bullet list under "Turbo-charging customer loyalty": talabat pro adoption rate >2x; DineOut Deals launched; ~99% Partner retention among pro vendors; PostPaid adoption +2.6x YoY; significant co-branded card uptake (TLB-001, p.20-21).
- Future focus for loyalty pillar: grow talabat pro base in GCC and Egypt; expand PostPaid to new markets and grow UAE adoption; launch co-branded cards in other GCC/non-GCC markets (TLB-001, p.21).
- Business model section frames "Loyalty programmes" and "FinTech" as core levers of "how we operate," explicitly stating loyalty programmes "play an important role in enhancing customer retention, increasing order frequency and driving overall profitability" (TLB-001, p.18-19).
- Multi-vertical customers order with higher frequency than food-only customers, and the Grocery & Retail Vertical's growing GMV share (25% in 2024 vs 21% in 2023) is framed as a retention/frequency driver via multi-verticality (TLB-001, p.11, p.20).
- Personalisation/technology: app evolution described from "simple food-ordering platform" (2016) to "multi-vertical marketplace" (2021) to hyper-personalised "It's not just the talabat app, it's your app" (2025); proprietary personalisation and ranking algorithm cited as an incremental EBITDA driver (TLB-001, p.22-23).

## Limitations
- Egypt is never broken out as a standalone financial segment: the Non-GCC segment (Egypt, Jordan, Iraq) is disclosed only in aggregate because none of the three countries individually meets the 10% IFRS 8 reportable-segment threshold for revenue, assets or profits (TLB-001, p.117). No Egypt-only GMV, revenue, EBITDA, order count or active-customer figures appear anywhere in this document.
- The report is dated as of FY2024/early 2025 (creation date 17 April 2025) and reflects information "at the time of writing" (e.g., Egypt talabat pro launch, instashop closing) — some statements (e.g. "recently launched" for talabat pro Egypt) describe February 2025 events occurring after the FY2024 period end but before publication.
- Financial statements are presented in two forms — "proforma" (as if the pre-IPO restructuring had occurred 1 January 2023, enabling 2023 vs 2024 comparability) and "consolidated interim" (audited, covering only the entity's actual life from inception 3 September 2024 to 31 December 2024) — and the two use different bases; care is needed not to conflate the short-period audited P&L (revenue USD 801mn, net profit USD 138mn) with the full-year proforma figures (revenue USD 2,872mn, net profit USD 346mn).
- instashop's FY2024 financials are explicitly excluded from this report ("Financials shown in this combined annual report do not include any contribution from instashop") since its acquisition only closed 25 February 2025 (TLB-001, p.7).
- No unit economics or KPIs (active customers, order frequency, GMV) are broken out for Egypt or any individual non-GCC country; only qualitative statements and a few Egypt-specific ratios (CIT rate, category-share multiple) are given.
