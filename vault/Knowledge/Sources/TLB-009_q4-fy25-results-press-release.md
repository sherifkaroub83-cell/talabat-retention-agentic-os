# TLB-009 — Q4/FY2025 Results Press Release

Talabat Holding plc — Q4 2025 Results Press Release: "talabat reports resilient results for Q4 2025, announces disciplined investment cycle to drive long-term growth"

## Executive Summary
This press release reports talabat's pro forma Q4 and full-year 2025 financial and operational results and unveils a 2026 disciplined investment plan (TLB-009, page 1). Q4 2025 GMV grew 21% y/y to USD 2.5 billion, revenue grew 26% to USD 1.0 billion, Adjusted EBITDA grew 13% to USD 156 million (6.3% of GMV), and net income was USD 123 million (5.0% of GMV), down 11% y/y due to higher GCC corporate tax rates and prior-year deferred tax benefits (TLB-009, pages 1–2). For FY2025, GMV grew 28% (constant currency) to USD 9.5 billion, revenue grew 33% to USD 3.9 billion, Adjusted EBITDA reached USD 615 million (6.5% of GMV), net income reached USD 464 million (4.9% of GMV), and Adjusted Free Cash Flow reached USD 559 million (5.9% of GMV) — all landing in or near guidance (TLB-009, page 1). The Board recommended total 2025 dividends of USD 421 million (90% payout of net income), exceeding prior guidance of USD 400 million (TLB-009, page 1). For 2026, the Company guided to 11–14% GMV growth, Adjusted EBITDA of USD 510–540M, net income of USD 280–310M, and Free Cash Flow of USD 370–400M, and announced more than USD 100 million in earmarked 2026 investment across two areas: scaling the grocery vertical (talabat mart) and strengthening the talabat pro loyalty subscription programme (TLB-009, pages 1, 3). Notably, the release confirms talabat pro is "now also available in all eight markets following successful launches in Egypt and Iraq in 2025" (TLB-009, page 3) — a direct, dated Egypt-specific retention-program data point. Toon Gyssels is named as talabat's newly-appointed CEO (TLB-009, page 3).

## Retention-Relevant Information
- **talabat pro launched in Egypt in 2025** — explicitly stated: "The programme is now also available in all eight markets following successful launches in Egypt and Iraq in 2025." (TLB-009, page 3). This is a key, directly citable fact for the capstone's Egypt retention strategy analysis.
- talabat pro described as "a multi-vertical engagement engine," offering: free delivery, booster discounts on best sellers within Food, discounts on hundreds of key value items within talabat mart, on-time guarantee, priority customer support, dine-out discounts, and partner services (streaming, ride-hailing) (TLB-009, page 3).
- "a surge in talabat pro adoption" cited as a contributor to Q4 2025 GMV/order growth across markets (TLB-009, page 1).
- 2026 investment plan explicitly earmarks spend to "strengthen[] talabat pro as a multi-vertical engagement engine," with "[i]ncremental investments will focus on enhancing value for both customers and participating vendors" (TLB-009, page 3).
- GMV growth attributed partly to "customer acquisition and higher order frequency" (TLB-009, page 1) — frequency is a direct retention-relevant KPI category, though no numeric frequency figure is given.
- CEO quote frames talabat pro and talabat mart investment as central to expanding "our multi-vertical subscriber base" (TLB-009, page 3).

## Limitations
- Figures presented are pro forma and exclude InstaShop for comparability purposes (InstaShop consolidated as of 25 February 2025); 2026 guidance, by contrast, does incorporate InstaShop (TLB-009, pages 1, 5).
- No Egypt-specific GMV, revenue, or profit figures — Egypt is only quantified as part of the aggregated "non-GCC" segment (with Jordan and Iraq).
- The "six and a half million active customers" figure in the boilerplate "About talabat" section is dated December 2024, not the current reporting period (December 2025) — appears to be unrevised legacy boilerplate text and should not be cited as a Q4 2025/FY2025 active-customer count.
- No quantification of talabat pro subscriber numbers, penetration rate, or Egypt-specific adoption — only qualitative confirmation that the programme launched in Egypt during 2025.
