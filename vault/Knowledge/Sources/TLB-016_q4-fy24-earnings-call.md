# TLB-016 — talabat Q4 & FY 2024 Earnings Call Presentation

## Executive Summary
This 20-page earnings call deck, presented by CEO Tomaso Rodriguez, CFO Khaled Alfakesh and IR's Shadi Salman, covers talabat's Q4 and full-year 2024 results, the first full-year results disclosed post-IPO. It reports FY2024 GMV of ~USD 7.4bn (+23% y/y), Management Revenue of USD 3.0bn (+32% y/y), Adjusted EBITDA of USD 497mn (6.7% of GMV, +55% y/y) and net income of USD 346mn (4.7% of GMV, +64% y/y) (p.5). The deck frames talabat as the category-leading on-demand food, grocery and retail delivery platform across 8 MENA countries, and highlights a redesigned, personalized app experience and the "turbo-charging customer loyalty" strategy centered on talabat pro, which grew adoption 2.1x y/y (in live markets) and fully launched in Egypt in February 2025 (p.7-9). The company guided FY2025 GMV growth of 17-18%, revenue growth of 18-20%, Adj. EBITDA margin of 6.5-7.0%, net income margin of 5.0-5.5%, and minimum dividends of USD 400mn (p.16). All figures in this deck exclude the pending InstaShop acquisition.

## Key Financial Metrics
- FY2024 Total GMV: ~USD 7.4bn, +23% y/y (p.5, p.11)
- FY2024 Total Management Revenue: USD 3.0bn, +32% y/y (p.5, p.11)
- FY2024 Adjusted EBITDA: USD 497mn, +55% y/y, 6.7% of GMV (+1.4ppt y/y margin expansion) (p.5, p.13)
- FY2024 Net Income: USD 346mn, +64% y/y, 4.7% of GMV (+1.2ppt y/y margin expansion) (p.5, p.14)
- FY2024 Adjusted Net Income: USD 393mn, +53% y/y, 5.3% of GMV (p.14)
- FY2024 Adjusted Free Cash Flow: 6.2% of GMV (p.13, p.16)
- Dividends: intends to pay USD 110mn re: Q4'24 (implying annualised yield >4.5% on AED 1.47 share price as of 12 Feb 2025), and at least USD 400mn for FY'25; dividend payout ratio 90% (p.5, p.14)
- G&R segment ~25% of total GMV in 2024 (up from ~21%); G&R y/y GMV growth ~46-47% (p.12)
- Non-GCC GMV growth ~42% y/y (from a lower base); GCC GMV growth ~20% y/y; non-GCC contribution to GMV ~16% (p.12)
- tMart business ~28% of revenue in 2024 (p.11)
- FY2024 actual vs. guidance: GMV growth 23% (guided 22-23%, in line, high end); Revenue growth 32% (guided 28-30%, exceeded); Adj. EBITDA margin 6.7% (guided ~6.5%, exceeded); Net income margin 4.7%/Adj. NI 5.3% (guided ~5%, in line); Adj. FCF margin 6.2% (guided 6.0-6.5%, in line); Dividends USD 110mn (guided min. USD 100mn, exceeded) (p.16)
- FY2025 guidance: GMV growth 17-18% y/y (constant currency); Revenue growth 18-20%; Adj. EBITDA margin 6.5-7.0%; Net income margin 5.0-5.5%; Adj. FCF margin 6.0-6.5%; Dividends min. USD 400mn (p.16) — note guidance does not reflect potential InstaShop impact
- Adj. EBITDA margin expansion of 1.4ppt to 6.7% driven by ~1ppt Gross Profit margin expansion (improved logistics efficiency, AdTech momentum, growth in service fees, improved G&R profitability), economies of scale in G&A/IT, and improved marketing efficiency (p.13)

## Key Operational Metrics
- Category-leading on-demand food ordering, delivery, takeaway and Grocery & Retail marketplace in 8 MENA countries (p.4)
- talabat pro adoption growth 2.1x (Dec'24 vs Dec'23), only counting markets live as of Dec 2024 (UAE, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain, Jordan, Oman); excludes Egypt and Iraq (p.9)
- Incremental pro uplift (frequency) +28%, measured Sep'24 vs. lookalike non-pro users (p.9)
- New personalized app: +24% tPro acquisitions uplift, +2% grocery acquisitions uplift (Nov-Dec'24 vs. previous experience; tPro figure excludes Egypt and Iraq) (p.8)

## Countries Mentioned
UAE, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain, Egypt, Oman, Jordan, Iraq — the 8 countries within the "talabat perimeter" (p.4).

## Egypt Mentions
- Egypt listed as one of talabat's 8 MENA countries of presence / market perimeter (p.4).
- talabat pro adoption growth figure of 2.1x (Dec'24 vs Dec'23) explicitly "excludes Egypt and Iraq," since pro was live only in UAE, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain, Jordan, Oman as of Dec 2024 (p.9, footnote 1).
- "talabat pro now in Egypt" — key update; footnote clarifies "Full launch of talabat pro in Egypt took place in Feb '25" (p.9, footnote 5).
- New personalized-app metrics: tPro acquisitions uplift of +24% comparison "excludes Egypt and Iraq" (p.8, footnote 1); the +2% grocery acquisitions uplift figure does include Egypt (footnote 2 does not exclude it).
- Adjusted Net Income definition references "non-cash unrealised foreign exchange loss from shareholder loan liability in Delivery Hero Egypt SAE" as a component excluded from Adjusted Net Income (p.18, p.14).

## Retention-Relevant Information
- "Turbo-charging customer loyalty" is one of five levers for sustained growth and profitability, encompassing AdTech solutions, CPG partnerships, and new initiatives (DineOut deals, talabat Kitchens) (p.6).
- "Deeper category penetration" lever includes frequency-enhancing initiatives, loyalty boosters, and FinTech (p.6).
- Company narrative arc: "Delivering food to your doorstep" (2016) → "Delivering food and a lot more" (2021) → "It's not just talabat app, it's your app" (2025), i.e. a shift toward personalization (p.7).
- New personalized app drove +24% talabat pro acquisitions uplift and +2% grocery acquisitions uplift vs. the previous app experience (Nov-Dec'24) (p.8).
- talabat pro described as "our premium subscription programme, featuring FREE delivery (subject to minimum order values) and exclusive deals across a wide selection of vendors"; adoption grew 2.1x y/y with +28% incremental frequency uplift vs. lookalike non-subscribers (p.9).
- talabat pro fully launched in Egypt in February 2025, with "plans to accelerate adoption & strengthen value proposition. More to come in 2025" (p.9).
- Fully multi-vertical ecosystem cited among Key Company Highlights as "driving loyalty, platform spend and expansion" (p.4).

## Limitations
- No Egypt-specific financial figures (GMV, revenue, active customers) are disclosed; Egypt is folded into the "non-GCC" segment with Jordan and Iraq.
- Key loyalty/subscription metrics (talabat pro adoption 2.1x, tPro acquisition uplift +24%) explicitly exclude Egypt and Iraq, since pro was not fully live there until Feb 2025 — meaning nearly all quantified pro/loyalty data in this document predates and excludes Egypt.
- Results and FY2025 guidance in this presentation exclude any contribution from the pending InstaShop acquisition (p.16, footnote 1).
- This is a summary of unaudited preliminary pro forma financial statements, not the final audited FY2024 statements (Disclaimer, p.19).
