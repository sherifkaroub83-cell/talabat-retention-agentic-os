## Executive Summary
This single-page tabular document is the successor to TLB-027, compiling sell-side analyst forecasts for talabat's Q1'26e and FY'26e-28e performance from 10 contributing banks (Arqaam Capital, Bank of America, Barclays, Citi, EFG Hermes, ING, Jefferies, JPMorgan, Morgan Stanley, UBS — ING and Jefferies added vs. the prior report; Goldman Sachs no longer listed). It reports Min/Median/Max/Cons.(average) for GMV, Revenue (IFRS), Gross Profit, Adjusted EBITDA, Net Income, and Free Cash Flow, plus dividends/DPS/payout ratio, against an FY'25A baseline. Consensus median FY'26e GMV is ~USD 11.4bn, rising to ~USD 12.8bn (FY'27e median) and ~USD 14.2bn (FY'28e median), implying a further deceleration of growth to low-double-digit percentages. Notably, consensus Adjusted EBITDA margin is expected to compress sharply from 6.2% (FY'25A) to a ~4.5-4.8% range through FY26e-28e — consistent with talabat's own disclosed FY2026 investment-cycle guidance in TLB-019/TLB-020. As a secondary source, use only for external market-expectation benchmarking, not as company guidance or actuals.

## Limitations
- **This is a SECONDARY source — sell-side analyst estimates, not talabat management guidance or actuals.**
- No Egypt or country-level breakdown; entirely group-consolidated figures.
- No operational KPIs (customers, orders, retention, loyalty) — financial metrics only.
- Panel of contributing analysts differs by line item (3-10 contributors depending on metric, e.g. only 3 analysts had submitted Q1'26e Free Cash Flow estimates) — treat low-count consensus figures (e.g., Q1'26e FCF) with more caution than high-count ones (e.g., GMV, #10).
- Comparatives noted as including "reclassifications, including 12 and 3 full months of instashop, respectively" for FY'25A/Q1'25A — meaning the FY'25A baseline here is not identical to the talabat-only FY'25A figures reported in TLB-019 (which excluded instashop); this affects like-for-like comparison against TLB-027's FY'25e forecasts.
- Some secondary derived-ratio rows (e.g., revenue take rate) were not transcribed with full confidence in exact column alignment from the flat-text extraction; primary KPI rows (GMV, Revenue, EBITDA, Net Income, FCF, Dividends) were independently verified via arithmetic cross-checks against the growth percentages shown.
