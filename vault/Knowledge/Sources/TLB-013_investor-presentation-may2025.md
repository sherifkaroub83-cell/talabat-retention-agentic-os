## Executive Summary
This 35-page investor presentation covers talabat's Q1 2025 results alongside a recap of FY2024 performance and detailed disclosure of the newly completed InstaShop acquisition. Q1 2025 GMV reached USD 2.1bn (+33% y/y constant currency, excluding InstaShop), Management Revenue USD 846mn (+38% y/y), Adjusted EBITDA USD 140mn (6.7% of GMV), and Net Income USD 103mn (4.9% of GMV) (p.14, p.18, p.19). talabat completed the 100% acquisition of InstaShop to accelerate Grocery & Retail scaling, disclosing InstaShop's UAE/Egypt market presence, ~16% y/y GMV growth, >8K active partners, >2M listed SKUs, and NPS of 54, with "limited customer overlap" versus talabat's own G&R base (p.15, p.16). The deck also details reduced Ramadan/Eid seasonality impact on orders (a 40% reduction in seasonal drop since 2023) and reiterates FY2025 guidance (GMV growth 17-18%, revenue growth 18-20%, EBITDA margin 6.5-7.0%) alongside a full FY2024 results recap (GMV ~USD 7.4bn, +23% y/y; EBITDA USD 497mn, 6.7% of GMV).

## Key Operational Metrics
- >68k Active Partners; >6.5mn Active Customers; >123k Active Riders; 4.9x Partner ROAS; 6.7x average order frequency; <30 min average delivery time (all as of/for December 2024) (p.32)
- 58,000+ selected restaurant partners; 10,000+ Local shops partners (≥1 order Dec 2024); >130 tMart stores (inclusive of leased-not-yet-operational) (p.6)
- Order frequency: 13.0/month multi-vertical users vs 3.8/month food-only users (July 2024); order frequency uplift post-talabat-pro subscription >28% (p.6)
- ~$450mn total FY2024 partner-funded savings; 80%+ of surveyed users rank talabat #1; ~99% seamless order rate (p.5)
- InstaShop: ~16% y/y GMV growth (Q1'25 vs Q1'24, constant currency); >8K active partners; >2M listed SKUs; NPS of 54 (Oct-Dec 2024 study, 12.6k respondents); 10 years in Grocery & Retail (p.15, p.16)
- InstaShop/talabat customer overlap: only 1 out of 3 InstaShop users use talabat G&R — i.e., 2 out of 3 InstaShop users are not active on talabat's G&R, reinforcing complementary customer bases (period 1 Jan-31 Mar 2025) (p.15)
- Ramadan seasonality: orders daily-average drop reduced from 1x (2023) to 0.9x (2024) to 0.6x (2025), a ~40% reduction in y/y seasonality impact since 2023 (p.17)
- talabat pro adoption growth 2.1x (Dec'24 vs Dec'23, excludes Egypt and Iraq); incremental pro uplift +28% (p.8)
- New personalized app: +24% tPro acquisitions uplift, +2% grocery acquisitions uplift (Nov-Dec'24 vs previous experience) (p.12)

## Retention-Relevant Information
- talabat pro remains the flagship loyalty/subscription lever: 2.1x adoption growth Dec'24 vs Dec'23 and +28% incremental frequency uplift for pro subscribers vs. lookalike non-subscribers (Sep'24 measurement) (p.8).
- talabat pro fully launched in Egypt in Feb 2025 — the most direct, dated retention-strategy data point for Egypt in this document (p.8).
- Multi-vertical customer journey (Food → Grocery & Retail → talabat pro) drives frequency: multi-vertical users order 13.0x/month vs. 3.8x/month for food-only users (p.6).
- InstaShop acquisition explicitly framed as complementary-customer-base expansion strategy for G&R, not cannibalization — only 1-in-3 InstaShop users already use talabat G&R (p.15). InstaShop's own retention indicator: NPS of 54 and "monthly habitual users, ordering large baskets" (p.16).
- Reduced Ramadan/Eid seasonality (40% y/y drop reduction since 2023) attributed partly to "increasing G&R adoption" — implying multi-vertical adoption smooths order volatility and supports retention through seasonal troughs (p.17).
- "Driving customer loyalty" retained as a named strategic lever (AdTech, CPG partnerships, DineOut deals, talabat Kitchens), alongside "Deeper category penetration" (frequency enhancing initiatives, loyalty boosters, FinTech) (p.4).
- New personalized talabat app strategy (Unlock Personalisation, Showcase Relevant Choice, Strengthen Multi-Verticality) continues to be cited, with +24% tPro acquisition uplift and +2% grocery acquisition uplift (p.12).

## Limitations
- No Egypt-specific financial figures (GMV, revenue, active customers) are disclosed; Egypt remains folded into the non-GCC segment with Jordan and Iraq.
- talabat pro KPIs (2.1x adoption growth, +24% acquisition uplift) explicitly exclude Egypt and Iraq, since pro only fully launched in Egypt in Feb 2025 — this document's own Q1 2025 window barely captures Egypt pro performance and none is separately reported.
- Q1 2025 headline financials (GMV USD 2.1bn, EBITDA USD 140mn, etc.) explicitly exclude InstaShop contribution except where separately disclosed (InstaShop-only section, p.20, and reported/IFRS bridge, p.21).
- Presentation states it is a summary of pro forma financial statements, not the auditor-reviewed or audited financial statements (Disclaimer, p.34).
- InstaShop financial disclosures (GMV growth, active partners, SKUs) are presented at a InstaShop-total level, not broken out by UAE vs. Egypt.
