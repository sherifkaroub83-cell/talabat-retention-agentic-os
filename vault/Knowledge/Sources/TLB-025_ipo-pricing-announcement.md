# TLB-025 — Talabat Holding plc Prices its IPO at the Top of the Range, Raising $2 Billion

## Executive Summary
This announcement confirms the successful pricing of talabat's IPO at the top of the previously announced range: AED 1.60 per share, implying a market capitalisation at listing of c. AED 37.3 billion (c. US$10.1 billion). The offering raised gross proceeds of c. AED 7.5 billion (c. US$2.0 billion) via 4,657,648,125 shares (20% of total issued share capital), achieving a "double-digit oversubscription level" driven by anchor orders from global long-only and technology-sector investors. talabat states this was "the largest global technology IPO in 2024 to date, the largest IPO in the GCC this year to date and the first ever technology sector IPO on DFM." Admission to trading on DFM was expected around 10 December 2024 under ticker "TALABAT" and ISIN "AEE01569T248." CEO Tomaso Rodriguez frames the milestone as validating 20 years of growth and reiterates the forward strategy: enhancing product offering, increasing market penetration, investing in loyalty programmes, scaling advertising, and exploring adjacent categories.

## Key Financial Metrics

- Final Offering size: 4,657,648,125 Shares (20% of total issued share capital) (TLB-025, page 2)
