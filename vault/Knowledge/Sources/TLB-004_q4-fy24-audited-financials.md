# TLB-004 — Talabat Holding PLC Consolidated Interim Financial Statements, FY2024 (Audited)

## Executive Summary
This is talabat Holding PLC's first audited annual/interim financial statement, covering the period from inception (3 September 2024, the date the Company was incorporated in ADGM ahead of its DFM IPO) through 31 December 2024 (TLB-004, page 1). For the full period since inception, the Group reported revenue of USD 801.3 million and net profit of USD 137.9 million (TLB-004, pages 3, 10). On a proforma/full-year basis the Directors' Report states GMV reached USD 7.4 billion (+23% YoY), revenue USD 3.0 billion (+32%), Adjusted EBITDA nearly USD 500 million (+55%, 6.7% of GMV), and net income USD 346 million (+64%, 4.7% of GMV) (TLB-004, page 3). Total assets at 31 December 2024 were USD 1,106.9 million against total equity of USD 538.4 million (TLB-004, page 9). The Group's principal activities are an online platform for food, grocery and retail ordering, delivery services, and ad-tech solutions to partners (TLB-004, page 3). Segment reporting (Note 3.16 and IFRS 8) treats the GCC (UAE, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain, Oman) as a single reportable segment (>75% of revenue and assets), while Egypt, Jordan and Iraq — grouped as "Non-GCC" — do not individually meet the 10% reportable-segment threshold and are therefore not broken out (TLB-004, page 37). Egypt appears by name as part of the Group's MENA footprint and as the incorporation country of two subsidiaries (Delivery Hero Egypt SAE and Delivery Hero Dmart Egypt LLC), both acquired via common-control restructuring on 30 September 2024 ahead of the IPO (TLB-004, pages 13, 16, 63). A subsequent event discloses the 25 February 2025 acquisition of InstaShop Ltd from Delivery Hero SE for total consideration of USD 31,928,889 (TLB-004, page 64) — confirming the corpus's flagged InstaShop valuation of ~USD 32 million (not USD 360 million).

- Subsidiary table lists "Delivery Hero Egypt SAE," incorporated in Egypt, principal activities "Import and export, grocery, central market, non-food supermarket, general trade office, commission agent," 100% effective holding (TLB-004, page 16)
- Subsidiary table also lists "Delivery Hero Dmart Egypt LLC," incorporated in Egypt, principal activities "General trade and distribution," 100% effective holding (TLB-004, page 16)
- Segment note: "In contrast, the Non-GCC countries (Egypt, Jordan, and Iraq) do not individually meet the 10% threshold for revenue, assets, or profits. As such, these countries do not qualify as reportable segments, but are disclosed under other segments." (TLB-004, page 37)
- Note 33 (Acquisition of subsidiaries under common control) lists "Delivery Hero Egypt SAE" among the entities over which Delivery Hero FZ LLC obtained control on 30 September 2024 as part of the pre-IPO restructuring (TLB-004, page 63)
- Currency note: the Egyptian Pound (EGP) is listed among the functional currencies of Group entities exposed to foreign-exchange risk (TLB-004, page 61, "Jordanian Dinar (JOD), Kuwaiti Dinar (KWD), Egyptian Pound (EGP), Omani Rial (OMR), Bahraini...")

## Retention-Relevant Information
- Subscription fees are disclosed as a distinct revenue line: USD 9,728,074 for the period since inception through 31 December 2024 — the smallest of the five itemized revenue lines (Commission, Delivery, Advertising and listing, Service, Subscription), indicating subscription (talabat pro) monetization is explicitly tracked but is a minor share of total revenue at the Group level (TLB-004, page 49).
- Revenue-recognition policy note: "For subscription programs offered to orderers and restaurants, the Group acts as principal. Revenue from subscription fees is recognised on a straight-line basis over the period of the subscription." (TLB-004, page 24)
- Note 6 (Intangible assets and goodwill) impairment-testing methodology discloses that local management teams use cohort models for revenue planning, and that "the key inputs of the cohort models include the customer retention/reorder rate, customer activity rate, average order size, and commission rates" — confirming that reorder/retention rate is a formally modeled driver of Talabat UAE and Talabat Kuwait's discounted-cash-flow valuations (goodwill impairment testing), though no retention-rate figures are disclosed (TLB-004, page 43).
- No Egypt-specific retention, loyalty, or subscription commentary is present; this is a statutory financial statement with no MD&A-style narrative by country.

## Limitations
- No Egypt-only financial breakout — Egypt is folded into "Non-GCC" together with Jordan and Iraq, with no country-level revenue, profit, or asset figures disclosed for FY2024 (unlike TLB-008/FY2025, where Egypt later becomes its own reportable segment).
- No operational KPIs (orders, active customers, riders, restaurant partner counts) — purely a statutory IFRS financial statement; GMV appears only as a headline growth figure in the Directors' Report, not as a reconciled or defined metric within the audited notes.
- Covers a 120-day "period since inception" (3 Sept–31 Dec 2024), not a clean calendar year; some Directors' Report highlights (GMV, revenue, EBITDA growth rates) are presented on a "proforma" full-year basis that is not fully reconciled to the audited income statement figures within this document.
- No narrative discussion of competitive dynamics, market risk (beyond standard FX/interest-rate financial-instrument risk), or retention/loyalty strategy.
- Comparative prior-year (2023) columns in the primary statements are blank/not populated in the extracted text (pre-inception company had no 2023 results).
