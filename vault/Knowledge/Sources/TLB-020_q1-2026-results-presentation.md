# TLB-020 — talabat Q1 2026 Results Presentation

## Title
Q1 2026 Results — "The Leading On-Demand Delivery Platform in MENA"

## Citation
TLB-020 — talabat Holding plc, "Q1 2026 Results," 12 May 2026, Input_Data/04_Earnings_Call_Presentations/20260512_talabat-Q1-2026-Results-Presentation.pdf

12 May 2026

## Publisher
talabat Holding plc (Investor Relations)

## Executive Summary
This 22-page deck is the most recent quarterly results presentation in the corpus, presented by CEO Toon Gyssels, CFO Khaled Alfakesh and IR's Shadi Salman. It reports Q1 2026 results (talabat + instashop, including fully-loaded instashop in comparatives): GMV USD (cFX) 2.7bn (+18% y/y), Revenue USD (cFX) 1.0bn (+22% y/y), Adjusted EBITDA USD 130mn (4.8% of GMV, -9% y/y), and Net Income USD 87mn (3.2% of GMV, -18% y/y) (p.4, p.10-11). The deck documents a deliberate margin step-down as talabat executes a USD 175mn Board-approved 2026 investment programme to "strengthen Food leadership" and "build the Everyday App" (tMart dark stores, talabat pro, new verticals) (p.12, p.16). It also discloses operational resilience measures taken during "regional conflict" that began 28 Feb 2026, including rider/picker network expansion and item-availability recovery (p.7), and provides an extensive Q&A on investment rationale, a new Liquidity Provider mandate, and a share buyback programme of up to 5% of issued shares over 24 months (p.16-18). FY2026 guidance was reiterated at 11-14% GMV growth with net income guidance revised upward to USD 300-330mn (p.14). Egypt is referenced specifically regarding FX depreciation exposure incorporated into FY2026 guidance (p.14).

## Key Financial Metrics
- Q1'26 GMV (talabat + instashop, cFX): USD 2.7bn, +18% y/y (nominal +19% y/y); talabat-only-equivalent reported as 2,685mn vs. 2,257mn pro forma Q1'25 (p.4, p.10)
- Q1'26 Revenue (cFX): USD 1.0bn, +22% y/y (nominal +23% y/y); reported 1,048mn vs. 853mn pro forma Q1'25, take rate rose from 38% to 39% of GMV (p.4, p.10)
- Q1'26 Adjusted EBITDA: USD 130mn, 4.8% of GMV, -9% y/y (down from 6.3% margin in Q1'25 pro forma) (p.4, p.11)
- Q1'26 Net Income: USD 87mn, 3.2% of GMV, -18% y/y (down from 4.7% margin, USD 106mn in Q1'25 pro forma) (p.4, p.11)
- Q1'26 Free Cash Flow: USD 104mn, 3.9% of GMV, +7% y/y (p.11)
- Net cash position: exceeding USD 693mn at end of Q1 2026 (p.16)
- FY2026 guidance (12 May 2026, revised): GMV growth 11-14% cFX (USD 11.2-11.5bn, unchanged); Revenue growth 14-17% cFX (USD 4.4-4.5bn, unchanged); Adj. EBITDA 4.4-4.8% of GMV (USD 510-540mn, unchanged); Net income 2.6-2.9% of GMV (USD 300-330mn, revised UP from USD 280-310mn); Free Cash Flow 3.2-3.6% of GMV (USD 370-400mn, unchanged); Dividends 90% of net income = USD 270-297mn (revised up in line with net income) (p.14)
- Adj. EBITDA margin bridge Q1'26: 6.0% (Q4'25 baseline) → 4.8% (Q1'26 actual) via Food-leadership investment (-0.2%) and Everyday App investment (-0.4% opex + -0.5% capex-related... total investment impact ~-1.2pp per bridge) (p.12)
- 2026 investment programme: USD 175mn Board-approved total, split into ~USD 55mn (0.5pp of GMV) Food-leadership investment and ~USD 120mn Everyday App investment (~USD 75mn opex/0.7pp of GMV + ~USD 45mn capex) (p.12, p.16)
- Dividends: USD 219mn paid in respect of H2 2025; on track to pay 90% of 2026 net income per policy (p.4)
- Share buybacks: programme to repurchase up to 5% of issued shares over 24 months expected to commence "in the coming days" (as of 12 May 2026); Liquidity Provider appointed 25 March 2026 with USD 15mn allocated (p.4, p.17)

## Key Operational Metrics
- Active partners: 93k (Mar'26) vs 79k (Mar'25), +18% y/y (p.8)
- Active riders/rider network: 168k (Mar'26) vs 134k (Mar'25), +25% y/y; additional riders during Q1'26 crisis response: >8K additional riders, >1.7K additional pickers vs. Q4'25 (p.7, p.8)
- Partner-funded savings: $188mn (Q1'26) vs $141mn (Q1'25), +33% y/y; reached "all-time high of 7% of the GMV" (p.7, p.8)
- GMV share of multi-vertical customers: 76% (Mar'26) vs 72% (Mar'25), +4pp y/y (p.8)
- GMV share of talabat pro subscribers: 49% (Q1'26) vs 32% (Q1'25) — "tPro subscribers represent around half of talabat platform GMV" (p.8)
- Advertising Revenue Margin: 3.4% (Mar'26) vs 3.3% (Mar'25), +0.1pp (p.8)
- Item availability recovered to pre-crisis levels within days; ~3 months coverage for critical SKUs within 48hrs; rider availability recovered within hours; modest (single-digit) restaurant price increases (excl. Iraq and Egypt) (p.7)

## Countries Mentioned
UAE (referenced re: fuel price increases, "excl. UAE," p.7). Iraq and Egypt referenced as excluded from a restaurant-pricing metric (p.7). Egypt referenced specifically re: FX depreciation (p.14). No full country list given in body text.

## Egypt Mentions
- "Modest increase in restaurant prices (single-digits only)" — footnoted "(2) Excluding Iraq and Egypt," implying Egypt (and Iraq) saw a different/untracked restaurant pricing pattern during the Q1'26 conflict-response period (p.7).
- "**Egypt FX exposure: YtD date [year-to-date] depreciation incorporated in guidance. GMV inflation-hedge dynamics provide partial offset for further depreciation.**" — listed as one of four explicit risks/mitigants supporting full-year FY2026 guidance (p.14).
- No Egypt-specific GMV, revenue, or customer figures disclosed.

## Retention-Relevant Information
- talabat pro GMV share reached "around half" (49%) of talabat platform GMV in Q1'26, up sharply from 32% a year earlier — described as "reflecting strong engagement and retention" (p.8).
- Multi-vertical GMV share continued climbing to 76% (from 72% y/y), "underscoring deepening multi-vertical engagement" (p.8).
- Investment programme Q&A explicitly frames Food-leadership investment as retention-focused: "On the customer side, we focus on retaining medium- and high-value customers through our subscription programme and targeted incentives" (p.16).
- Everyday App investment programme explicitly aims at "enhancing the talabat pro loyalty programme to grow subscribers, strengthen coverage of key GMV partners, and expand exclusive talabat mart deals" (p.16).
- Q&A: "improving frequency, basket size, and retention position talabat to scale its Everyday App across our markets" is cited as the rationale for continued G&R/ecosystem investment despite macro uncertainty (p.16).
- Partner-funded savings reaching an "all-time high of 7% of the GMV" in Q1'26, framed under the "Affordability"/"Experience" pillars supporting customer retention amid conflict disruption (p.7).

## Important Quotations

## Limitations
- No Egypt-specific quantitative financial or operational data — only two qualitative Egypt references (FX exposure and exclusion from a pricing metric).
- Adj. EBITDA and Net Income both declined y/y in Q1'26 due to a deliberate, disclosed investment cycle; margin trends in this quarter should not be read as underlying business deterioration without accounting for the ~USD 175mn 2026 investment programme (p.12, p.16).
- Results reflect a "regional conflict" that began 28 Feb 2026 and a ceasefire "in mid-April" — an unusual, non-recurring operating environment that may distort quarter-over-quarter comparability (p.7, p.18).
- Financial comparatives are pro forma for instashop (consolidated in reported figures as of 25 February 2025), complicating exact y/y comparison methodology (p.4).
