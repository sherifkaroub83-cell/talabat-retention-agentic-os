## Executive Summary
This is talabat Holding PLC's Q1 2026 IFRS interim financial statement, covering the three-month period ended 31 March 2026 with restated Q1 2025 comparatives (TLB-010, page 1). **This is the first quarterly financial statement in which Egypt is broken out as its own separate reportable segment**, distinct from "GCC" and a residual "Non-GCC" (Jordan/Iraq) category — the note explicitly states "As Egypt operations have met the quantitative thresholds for reportable segments, they are presented as a separate reportable segment" (TLB-010, page 19). This gives, for the first time in the assigned corpus, explicit Egypt-only revenue and profit figures: Q1 2026 Egypt segment revenue of USD 145.3M (external revenue USD 139.9M) and profit before income taxes of USD 9.4M, versus restated Q1 2025 Egypt segment revenue of USD 80.3M (external revenue USD 77.4M) and profit before tax of USD 0.5M (TLB-010, page 20) — implying roughly 81% y/y Egypt segment revenue growth. Group-wide, Q1 2026 revenue was USD 1,047.7M (net profit USD 86.6M), down from Q1 2025's comparative growth trajectory partly due to planned 2026 margin investment (TLB-010, page 5). Total assets reached USD 1,734.2M and total equity USD 774.8M as of 31 March 2026 (TLB-010, page 4). The Company also disclosed a new treasury-share/liquidity-provider arrangement (Al Ramz Capital LLC) and a subsequent-event share buyback authorization of up to 5% of issued shares approved 13 April 2026 (TLB-010, pages 14, 22). The statement also references "recent geopolitical developments" and resilience in the face of "the ongoing regional conflict" affecting operations (TLB-010, page 8).

- Subscription fees revenue Q1 2026: USD 25,772,018, more than doubling from USD 12,327,925 in Q1 2025 — a >100% y/y increase, the largest of any revenue line's growth rate disclosed (TLB-010, page 16). This is consistent with talabat pro's 2025 Egypt/Iraq launches (per TLB-009) driving incremental subscription revenue.
- No further retention/loyalty narrative — this is a statutory IFRS financial statement without MD&A commentary. See TLB-011 (companion press release) for the operational/strategic narrative on the same quarter, including explicit reference to "increased incentives to retain medium and high-value customers."
- The new Egypt segment disclosure (profit before tax swinging from a near-breakeven USD 0.5M in Q1 2025 to USD 9.4M in Q1 2026, on revenue growth of ~81%) is a materially important, directly citable data point establishing Egypt's growing standalone financial significance to the group — highly relevant context for the capstone's retention strategy given Egypt's now-quantified scale and profitability trajectory.

## Limitations
- Egypt is disclosed as a standalone segment for the first time in this document; prior quarters in the corpus (TLB-005, 006, 007) do not have comparable Egypt-only figures, so historical trend analysis is limited to this single quarter-over-quarter comparison (Q1 2026 vs. restated Q1 2025).
- No operational KPIs (GMV, orders, active customers, riders) are disclosed in this statement — see the companion press release (TLB-011) for GMV and qualitative operational commentary for the same quarter.
- No explanation is given in this note as to why Egypt specifically crossed the "quantitative thresholds" for separate segment reporting (i.e., no percentage-of-group-revenue or asset threshold disclosed).
- No narrative on retention/loyalty strategy, talabat pro adoption in Egypt, or competitive dynamics — purely a statutory financial statement.
