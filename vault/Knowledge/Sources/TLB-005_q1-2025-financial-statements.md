# TLB-005 — Q1 2025 Condensed Consolidated Interim Financial Statements

Talabat Holding PLC — Condensed consolidated interim financial statements for the period since inception on 3 September 2024 till 31 March 2025

## Executive Summary
This is talabat Holding PLC's first quarterly IFRS interim financial statement following its December 2024 IPO, covering the three-month period ended 31 March 2025 and the cumulative period since inception (3 September 2024) to 31 March 2025 (TLB-005, page 1). Q1 2025 revenue was USD 833.6M with net profit of USD 103.3M (TLB-005, page 5). Cumulative (inception-to-date) revenue was USD 1,634.9M and net profit USD 241.2M (TLB-005, page 5). Total assets at 31 March 2025 stood at USD 1,304.8M against total equity of USD 629.4M (TLB-005, page 4). The document details the 25 February 2025 acquisition of InstaShop Ltd (100%) from Delivery Hero SE, a common-control transaction valued at USD 31.9M consideration (TLB-005, pages 8, 25). The Group reports on a two-segment basis: "GCC" (Kuwait, UAE, Oman, Qatar, Bahrain) and "Other segments" (TLB-005, page 22) — Egypt is not broken out but is named as part of the wider MENA footprint and as the incorporation country of one InstaShop subsidiary and of "Delivery Hero Egypt SAE" (acquired 30 September 2024) (TLB-005, pages 8, 9, 25). This is a formal IFRS financial statement with notes; it contains no operational KPIs (GMV, orders, active customers) or retention/loyalty narrative.

- No operational KPIs (GMV, orders, active customers, riders) — purely an IFRS statutory financial statement with notes.
- No narrative discussion of strategy, competition, or retention/loyalty initiatives.
- Segment definition names only "GCC" explicitly; the "Other segments" label is generic and not itemized by country in this document.
