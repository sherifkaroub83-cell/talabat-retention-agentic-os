## Executive Summary
This regulatory announcement sets the IPO offer price range (AED 1.50–1.60 per share) and opens the subscription period for talabat's initial public offering on the Dubai Financial Market (DFM). It confirms 3,493,236,093 shares (15% of total issued share capital) would be offered, all sold by sole shareholder Delivery Hero MENA Holding GmbH. Implied market capitalisation at listing was set between c. AED 34.93bn (c. US$9.51bn) and c. AED 37.26bn (c. US$10.15bn). Three cornerstone investors (UAE Strategic Investment Fund 5, Abu Dhabi Pension Fund, Emirates International Investment Company) committed a combined c. AED 918 million (US$250 million). The subscription period ran 19–27 November 2024 for UAE Retail Investors and to 28 November 2024 for Professional Investors, with final pricing expected 29 November 2024 and Admission expected around 10 December 2024. CEO Tomaso Rodriguez's commentary reiterates talabat's growth strategy pillars: product offering, market penetration, FinTech and loyalty programmes, advertising, and adjacent categories.
