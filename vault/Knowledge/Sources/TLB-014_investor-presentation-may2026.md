## Executive Summary
This 25-page presentation is the most recent investor deck in the corpus, covering Q1 2026 results (including full-year InstaShop consolidation), a recap of FY2025 guidance performance, and a new Board-approved USD 175mn "Everyday App" investment programme for 2026. Q1 2026 GMV reached USD 2.7bn (+18% y/y constant currency, including InstaShop), Revenue USD 1.0bn (+22% y/y), Adjusted EBITDA USD 130mn (4.8% of GMV, down y/y as the company steps up strategic investment), and Net Income USD 87mn (3.2% of GMV) (p.11, p.15, p.16). FY2026 guidance was revised upward for net income (to USD 300-330mn), reflecting a deliberate margin step-down from 6.0% (Q4'25) to a guided mid-range of ~4.6% Adj. EBITDA margin, to fund strengthening Food leadership and building talabat mart/talabat pro/new verticals under the "Everyday App" strategy (p.6, p.9, p.19). Egypt is explicitly flagged as a named FX/macro risk (currency depreciation) in the FY2026 guidance risk section, with GMV inflation-hedge dynamics cited as a partial offset (p.9). The deck also discloses a new share buyback programme (up to 5% of shares over 24 months) and a Liquidity Provider mandate appointed 25 March 2026 (p.19, p.20).

## Retention-Relevant Information
- talabat pro GMV share (talabat-only) rose to 49% of platform GMV in Mar'26 (Q1'26) from 32% in Mar'25 — described as "reflecting strong engagement and retention" (p.14).
- GMV share of multi-vertical customers grew +4pp y/y, "underscoring deepening multi-vertical engagement" (p.14).
- Partner-funded savings grew 25% y/y to $188mn in Q1'26, "sustaining a consistent quarter on quarter growth" — framed under the "Value" pillar of the customer value proposition (p.14).
- FY2026 Everyday App investment programme (~USD 120mn) explicitly targets "enhancing the talabat pro loyalty programme to grow subscribers, strengthen coverage of key GMV partners, and expand exclusive talabat mart deals" (p.19, Q1 answer).
- Food-leadership investment (~USD 55mn) explicitly states: "On the customer side, we focus on retaining medium- and high-value customers through our subscription programme and targeted incentives" — a direct, named retention strategy statement (p.19, Q4 answer).
- Strategic rationale for Everyday App: "As customer behaviour becomes increasingly multi-vertical, improving frequency, basket size, and retention position talabat to scale its Everyday App across our markets" (p.19, Q2 answer).
- Item availability and rider availability described as recovering quickly from conflict-related disruption, framed as supporting "Experience," "Selection," and "Affordability" — the three customer value pillars tied to retention (p.13).
- Adtech monetization (dark-store adtech margins reaching 7% of GMV in UAE) cited as an indirect retention/monetization lever expected to offset margin investment over time (p.19).

## Limitations
- No Egypt-specific GMV, revenue, active-customer, or talabat-pro-subscriber figures are disclosed; Egypt remains folded into the non-GCC segment.
- The Egypt FX risk callout (p.9) is qualitative only — no quantified currency depreciation percentage or Egypt-specific revenue impact is given.
- Restaurant pricing and some rider/picker metrics explicitly exclude Egypt and Iraq (p.13), so the reported figures do not represent Egypt's actual pricing/logistics dynamics.
- Financial comparatives are pro forma for InstaShop (fully-loaded), and reporting frameworks changed in 2026 (Management vs. IFRS P&L harmonization, IFRS revenue reporting basis from 2026), complicating direct comparison with the two earlier decks (TLB-012, TLB-013).
- The "regional conflict" referenced as a Q1'26 tailwind/risk is not further specified geographically within this document.
- Document is a summary of unaudited financial statements, not the auditor-reviewed statements (Disclaimer, p.24).
