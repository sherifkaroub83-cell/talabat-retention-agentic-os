# SWOT Analysis — 2026 Investment Programme

## Decision conclusion

This evidence-based SWOT validates the existing `DEC-009` recommendation. It does not change the
investment ranking: `OPT-002` and `OPT-003` remain the near-term priority; `OPT-001` and `OPT-005`
continue at pace behind their existing gates; and `OPT-004` remains the longer-horizon capability
build. No new assumption, forecast, financial value, or geography transfer is introduced.

## Recommendation rerun

The approved 11-criterion comparison in `DEC-009` was re-read against all twelve SWOT elements:

- **OPT-002 remains near-term/pilot:** SWOT-O2 supports the opportunity; its geographic-evidence gap
  remains, so reversibility and new country-level measurement remain mandatory.
- **OPT-003 remains near-term:** SWOT-T1 is the plan's most direct quantified competitive-pressure
  evidence, while SWOT-T3 preserves market-specific legal controls.
- **OPT-001 remains continue-at-pace:** SWOT-O1 confirms headroom, but SWOT-W3 and `FIN-04` prevent
  acceleration without margin proof.
- **OPT-005 remains continue-at-pace:** SWOT-S2 confirms an evidenced capability; attribution and
  market-transfer measurement remain necessary.
- **OPT-004 remains longer-horizon:** SWOT-S3/W2/O3 confirm strategic potential and a local proof
  point, but not Group-wide transfer readiness.

**Result: unchanged.** The SWOT validates the existing recommendation; it does not provide evidence
for a different ranking.
