# AI Opportunities

## Thesis
talabat has already told investors, in its own words, where it intends to take AI next — new-form-factor recommendations, tighter timing/placement/incentive optimisation of cross-sells, and deeper personalisation and smart targeting inside its "rapidly growing adtech business" — and these stated intentions, not a capstone-invented wish list, are the correct starting inventory for an Egypt AI-retention opportunity set. This note catalogues what talabat has evidenced it *wants to build*, distinct from [[Future AI Opportunities]], which is the capstone's own recommendation-space synthesis built on top of the corpus's data gaps.

## Synthesis
Three roadmap statements anchor this note, all traceable to specific disclosures rather than inferred. First, [[Recommendation Systems]] records the Capital Markets Day roadmap language naming "AI driven recommendations in new form factors" and "optimising timing, placement, and incentives of vertical and product cross-sells" as forward-looking items (TLB-015, page 89) — sitting alongside the already-shipped evolution from cuisine-level to item-level ranking (e.g., the "Meal for One" example) documented in the FY2025 Annual Report (TLB-002, page 15). This is a company that has both shipped a granularity upgrade and named what it wants to ship next, giving the capstone a credible, cited technology trajectory to extend into Egypt rather than propose from a blank page.

Second, [[AI]] and [[Personalization]] both cite the FY2025 Annual Report's 2026 strategy language that AI will "continue driving personalisation and smart targeting... particularly within our rapidly growing adtech business" (TLB-002, page 10). Read together with [[Advertising]]'s account of the GEMs product — an AI-supported tool explicitly designed to help Partners with "low/declining customer acquisition or retention rates" win back customers (TLB-026, page 138) — this establishes that talabat's adtech arm already operationalises AI-driven win-back logic on the Partner side. The stated 2026 ambition to deepen personalisation "particularly" in adtech is therefore not a new capability being proposed here; it is talabat's own next stated step on a mechanism it has already built and monetised.

Third, [[Personalization]] and [[Talabat Pro]] connect the same ranking model to loyalty-offer timing: machine learning decides "the right time" to surface a talabat pro or talabat Rewards offer to a given customer (TLB-023, page 8), and [[Growth Strategy]]'s 2026 "Everyday App" investment programme (~USD 100-120mn, TLB-019/TLB-020) explicitly funds deepening the loyalty programme as one of its named investment areas. The cross-sell optimisation roadmap item sits naturally inside this same funded programme — talabat has not just stated an AI ambition, it has attached committed capital to the broader initiative it belongs to, even though no line item breaks out AI spend specifically or by country.

None of these three roadmap items — new form-factor recommendations, cross-sell timing/placement/incentive optimisation, and adtech-embedded personalisation — carries an Egypt-specific commitment, deployment date, or budget in the corpus. They are Group-level statements of direction. The opportunity for the capstone is to argue that Egypt, as the newest standalone and fastest-growing segment (see [[Egypt]]), is a logical proving ground for these already-stated ambitions — but that argument belongs to [[Future AI Opportunities]], not to this note, which stays limited to cataloguing what talabat itself has said it intends to do.

- [[Growth Strategy]] — the 2026 Everyday App investment programme that funds loyalty-programme deepening as one of its named pillars.

## Egypt-Specific Considerations
All three roadmap statements are Group-level; none names Egypt, a deployment timeline for Egypt, or an Egypt budget allocation. [[Egypt]] records that talabat pro itself only launched in Egypt in February 2025, and [[AI]]'s Open Questions note that no document discloses whether Egypt runs on the same AI/ML models as the GCC or a less mature version. Any claim that these roadmap items will reach Egypt, or on what timeline, is an extrapolation, not a disclosed fact — this note limits itself to what talabat has said it wants to build in general, not to when or whether it lands in Egypt.

## Open Questions
- Whether any of the three named roadmap items ("new form factors," cross-sell timing/incentive optimisation, deepened adtech personalisation) have shipped since the Capital Markets Day and Annual Report where they were stated, in any market.
- Whether the cuisine-to-item-level recommendation upgrade (TLB-002) has reached Egypt specifically, or is being rolled out market-by-market — not disclosed.
- What "new form factors" concretely means (voice, chat-based ordering, in-app widgets, etc.) — the corpus does not elaborate.
- Whether any AI roadmap budget is allocated at the country level at all, given the 2026 Everyday App investment figure is Group-wide and unbroken-out.

## Strategic Implications
- The retention business plan can position an Egypt AI-personalisation initiative as executing talabat's own stated roadmap rather than inventing a new capability — a materially stronger pitch than a from-scratch proposal.
- The GEMs Partner-side win-back precedent is evidence that talabat already has organisational and technical familiarity with AI-driven retention/win-back logic; an Egypt customer-side analogue is a natural, low-invention extension.
- Because the 2026 Everyday App programme already funds loyalty-programme deepening, an Egypt AI-personalisation ask can be framed as a request for allocation within an existing, board-endorsed capital envelope rather than a new budget line.
- The complete absence of Egypt-specific roadmap detail is itself the opening this note deliberately leaves for [[Future AI Opportunities]] to build the capstone's own recommendation on.
