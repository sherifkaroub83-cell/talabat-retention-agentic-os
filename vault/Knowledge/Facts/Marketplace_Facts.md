## TLB-001 — Annual Report 2024

- talabat operates a three-sided marketplace (customers, Partners, riders) generating network effects described as the "talabat flywheel" (TLB-001, page 4, page 15).
- Over 68,000 Active Partners (incl. >10,000 Local Shops) and over 58,700 Restaurants as of December 2024 (TLB-001, page 4, page 17).
- Local Shops offer over 700,000 SKUs across >10,000 Partners (TLB-001, page 17).
- Category leader across all 8 countries of operation by total value of orders (relative category share ranges 2x-10x+ vs. next closest peer) (TLB-001, page 5).
- talabat penetration is ~20-25% in foodservice but only ~1-2% in grocery/other retail categories — significant headroom flagged (TLB-001, page 21).
