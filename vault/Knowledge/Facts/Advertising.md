## TLB-001 — Annual Report 2024

- AdTech revenue: USD 246mn in 2024, 3.3% of GMV (2023: 3.2% of GMV), +27% YoY (TLB-001, page 13, page 21).
- Global benchmark target for AdTech revenue is ~7% of GMV in the medium term (talabat currently well below this) (TLB-001, page 21).
- AdTech tools include Sponsored Listings, Keyword Ads and Deal Targeting; most popular cost-per-click products yielded an average return of nearly 5x on ad spend (TLB-001, page 13).
- Partner ROAS for CPC ads: 4.9x as of July 2024; September 2024 average CPC ROAS was 6.2x (TLB-001, page 15, page 19).
- AdTech uses "integration of AI-driven targeting and automated ad-buying processes" to lower customer acquisition costs and amplify Partner visibility (TLB-001, page 13).
- Future focus: grow AdTech revenue toward ~7% of GMV benchmark; expand self-service AdTech; enhance algorithmic/real-time ML targeting; new ad formats; CPG-focused campaign reporting tools (TLB-001, page 21).

## TLB-015 — Capital Markets Day 2024

- Annual AdTech revenue: $84M (2021) → $144M (2022) → $194M (2023), 52% CAGR 2021-23 (TLB-015, page 26, 50, 105).
- AdTech revenue as % of GMV: 2.1% (2021) → 2.8% (2022) → 3.2% (2023); global leading peer benchmark ~7% penetration (TLB-015, page 26, 86, 105).
- Partner ROAS (CPC only): 4.9x average, as of July 2024 (TLB-015, page 13, 47, 48, 86).
- AdTech product-level ROAS: Premium positioning 4.9x (27% adoption); Display ads 4.0x (1.1% adoption); Keyword ads 4.8x (2.2% adoption); Deal targeting 13.8x (22% adoption); GEMs 3.6x (25% adoption) (TLB-015, page 49).
- Number of AdTech products per partner: 1.17 (2021) → 1.20 (2022) → 1.29 (2023) → 1.40 (H1 2024), +20% (TLB-015, page 49).
- CPG advertising investment ratio for talabat: 8% (leading vs. typical CPG investment of 2%), a 4x differential (TLB-015, page 108).
- Global retail media ad spending CAGR 2019-25: 22% (TLB-015, page 107).
