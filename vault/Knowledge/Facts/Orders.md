- talabat reached "1 million orders per day" as a historical milestone and "100 million lifetime orders" (company timeline) (TLB-001, page 7).
- Average delivery time held at less than 30 minutes across key markets despite increased fleet utilisation in 2024 (TLB-001, page 12, page 15).
