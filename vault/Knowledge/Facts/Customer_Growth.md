## TLB-001 — Annual Report 2024

- Active Customers grew from ~2.2 million (Jan 2020) to >6.5 million (Dec 2024) (TLB-001, page 15).
- 2024 GMV growth was driven by a 25% expansion in monthly active users plus an 8% uplift in order frequency (TLB-001, page 26).
- Multi-vertical customers (ordering both food and other categories) order with higher frequency than food-only users, and their numbers increased in 2024 (TLB-001, page 21).
- Customer Acquisition Costs (CAC): USD 44mn in 2024 (2023: USD 40mn) (TLB-001, page 28); combined with talabat-funded vouchering (USD 59mn) into total CARC of USD 103mn or 1.4% of GMV (2023: USD 89mn or 1.5% of GMV) (TLB-001, page 28).
