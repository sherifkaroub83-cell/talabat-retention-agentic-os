## TLB-001 — Annual Report 2024

- talabat pro is talabat's subscription loyalty programme, launched March 2022 in the UAE (TLB-001, page 19).
- By end-2024, available in all countries of operation except Iraq; per the CEO's statement, "now available in seven of the eight countries" following its Egypt launch (TLB-001, page 11-12, page 19).
- talabat pro launched in Egypt in February 2025 ("recently launched," "at the time of writing") (TLB-001, page 11, page 19).
- 2.1x increase in talabat pro adoption rate in 2024 vs. prior year (TLB-001, page 12, page 21).
- 24% uplift in talabat pro conversion rates attributed to the newly redesigned talabat app (Q4 2024 redesign) (TLB-001, page 12, page 23).
- talabat pro subscribers showed a 28% uplift in order frequency in September 2024, measured vs. lookalike users matched on frequency, basket size and tenure who did not join pro (TLB-001, page 18).
- talabat pro offers free delivery (subject to minimum basket size) and exclusive discounts for a fixed monthly or annual fee (TLB-001, page 18).
- Programme benefits expanded in 2024 to include exclusive lifestyle benefits, double rewards points (UAE) and DineOut Deals integration (TLB-001, page 11).
- ~99% Partner retention achieved among pro-participating vendors in 2024 (TLB-001, page 21).
- Future focus: grow talabat pro subscription base in GCC and Egypt, leveraging early Egypt traction (TLB-001, page 21).

## TLB-002 — Annual Report 2025

- talabat pro subscriber adoption more than doubled in 2025 (3.2x user growth); subscribers now
  account for nearly 50% of talabat's total GMV. (TLB-002, pages 4, 10, 13)
- Expanded to all eight operating markets in 2025, including strategic launches in Egypt (February
  2025) and Iraq (September 2025). (TLB-002, page 14)
- Benefits: free delivery, booster discounts on Food best-sellers, discounts on hundreds of key
  value items within talabat mart, on-time guarantee, priority customer support, dine-out discounts,
  partner services (streaming, ride-hailing). (TLB-002, page 13)
- 2026 focus: strengthening talabat pro "as a multi-vertical engagement engine," with incremental
  investment in programmed benefits, free delivery co-funding, campaign offers, and partner
  services. (TLB-002, page 14)
- Subscription revenue (at-a-glance): USD 74mn (2025). (TLB-002, page 11)
- Subscription fees & Other Income combined line (pro forma FY2025): USD 1,397mn (+47% y/y). (TLB-002, page 20)
- Audited subscription fees (since-inception period): USD 83,790,496. (TLB-002, page 105)

## TLB-015 — Capital Markets Day 2024

- talabat pro penetration: 7.6% of user base (Jul-2024) vs ~48% global leading peer benchmark (US player, Dec-2023) (TLB-015, page 25, 104).
- Live in 6 of 8 countries — all except Egypt and Iraq (TLB-015, page 77).
- Retention uplift: 32% (12-month retention of talabat pro joiners vs lookalike non-members, cohort joined July 2023) (TLB-015, page 78, 104).
- Frequency uplift: 22% (July 2024 measurement) (TLB-015, page 25, 78, 104).
- Gross profit per customer uplift: 136% (30 days post- vs pre-subscription, July 2024 joiners) (TLB-015, page 78, 104).
- Partner-funded incentives for talabat pro users grew from $1.2M (Jan-23) to $4.2M (Jul-24) (TLB-015, page 104).
- talabat pro user CAGR (Jul-22 to Jul-24, UAE/first country of entry): 129% (TLB-015, page 25).
- talabat pro provides unlimited free delivery on select Food partners, tMart, and select Local shops (above a minimum order value), members-only discounts, tMart exclusive deals, 2x rewards points (UAE only), and Exclusive DineOut Deals (UAE only) (TLB-015, page 77).
- Total LTM partner-funded savings via talabat pro: $32M (TLB-015, page 77).
- Roadmap: "Launching in remaining categories" and "Deepening value proposition" listed as talabat pro's forward priorities (TLB-015, page 89).

## TLB-026 — International Offering Memorandum

- talabat pro launched March 2022 in the UAE; a subscription service offering free delivery (minimum basket) and exclusive deals for a fixed monthly or annual fee (TLB-026, page 133)
- As of the date of this Offering Memorandum (19 Nov 2024), talabat pro is available in all talabat markets **except Egypt and Iraq** (TLB-026, page 133)
- Only 8.3% of customers were subscribed to talabat pro as of 29 September 2024, described as leaving "substantial room for growth" (TLB-026, page 30, page 122)
- talabat pro subscriber CAGR in the UAE was 130% (September 2022–September 2024) (TLB-026, page 133)
- >20% uplift in order frequency among talabat pro subscribers for the month of July 2024 (TLB-026, page 30, page 122, page 133)
- Retention metric definition: percentage of talabat pro customers (joined July 2023) retained on talabat 12 months later, relative to "lookalike" customers with the same frequency, average food value and tenure who did not join talabat pro (TLB-026, page 133)
- Total annual customer savings from talabat pro (Oct 2023–Sept 2024) ≈ AED 135.2 million (EUR ~34 million), funded by Partners and third parties including banks (TLB-026, page 133)
- talabat pro key features: free delivery (min. basket), exclusive deals (food, tMart, Dineout Deals in UAE only), lifestyle benefits via talabat Rewards catalogue, 2x rewards points (UAE only) (TLB-026, page 134)
- Partners contribute a fixed charge per talabat pro order to help fund the free-delivery benefit; the programme is managed to remain profitable (TLB-026, page 134)
