## TLB-001 — Annual Report 2024

- FY2024 GMV: USD 7,428mn (USD 7.4bn), up 23% YoY from USD 6,062mn (USD 6.1bn) in 2023 (TLB-001, page 6, page 26).
- GMV CAGR 2015-2024: 48% (TLB-001, page 7).
- GMV by segment 2024: GCC USD 6,332mn (+20% YoY, 85% of total GMV); non-GCC USD 1,096mn (+42% YoY, surpassed USD 1bn for the first time) (TLB-001, page 26).
- GMV by segment 2023: GCC USD 5,289mn (87% of total GMV); non-GCC USD 773mn (TLB-001, page 26).
- GMV by vertical 2024: Food Vertical USD 5,542mn (+16% YoY, 75% share, 2023: 79%); Grocery & Retail Vertical USD 1,886mn (+47% YoY, 25% share, 2023: 21%) (TLB-001, page 26).
- GMV growth in 2024 driven by 25% expansion in monthly active users and an 8% uplift in order frequency (TLB-001, page 26).
- GMV excludes subscription fees (e.g. talabat pro) and rider tips (TLB-001, page 27).

## TLB-004 — Q4/FY2024 Audited Financials

- Directors' Report: Group achieved a 23% increase in Gross Merchandise Value (GMV) compared to the preceding year, reaching a record high of USD 7.4 billion (proforma basis) (TLB-004, page 3).
- Adjusted EBITDA increased by 55%, attaining nearly USD 500 million, equivalent to 6.7% of GMV (TLB-004, page 3).
- Net income rose by 64% to USD 346 million, representing 4.7% of GMV (proforma) (TLB-004, page 3).
- Adjusted (like-for-like) net income USD 393 million, or 5.3% of GMV, +53% (TLB-004, page 3).
- GMV for delivery-only stores is defined as the total value paid by customers (including VAT, delivery fees, service fees, less subsidies such as vouchers and discounts) (TLB-004, page 24).
