# TLB-013 raw facts & entities

## Facts by topic

### Revenue
- Q1 2025 Management Revenue: USD 846mn, +38% y/y constant currency (+34% y/y reported currency), talabat-only excl. InstaShop (TLB-013, page 14, page 18)
- Revenue grew faster than GMV in Q1'25 on higher contribution of tMart business and higher subscription fees (TLB-013, page 18)
- Reported (IFRS bridge) Q1 2025 Management Revenue incl. 1 month InstaShop: USD 857mn (constant currency USD 884mn) (TLB-013, page 21)
- FY2024 recap: Total Mgmt Revenue USD 3.0bn, +32% y/y (TLB-013, page 23)
- FY2025 revenue growth guidance (excl. InstaShop): 18-20% y/y constant currency, USD 3.49-3.55bn (TLB-013, page 3)

### GMV
- Q1 2025 GMV: USD 2.1bn / USD 2,084mn, +33% y/y constant currency (+30% y/y reported), talabat-only excl. InstaShop (TLB-013, page 14, page 18)
- Q1 2024 GMV (comparator): USD 1,603mn (TLB-013, page 18)
- InstaShop-only Q1 2025 GMV: USD 173mn, +10% y/y reported / +16% y/y constant currency (TLB-013, page 20)
- Reported (IFRS bridge) Q1 2025 GMV context not separately broken out beyond revenue/EBITDA/NI bridge (TLB-013, page 21)
- FY2024 recap: Total GMV ~USD 7.4bn, +23% y/y (TLB-013, page 23)
- G&R segment ~25% of total GMV in 2024; Non-GCC GMV +42% y/y, GCC GMV +20% y/y in 2024 (TLB-013, page 25)
- FY2025 GMV growth guidance (excl. InstaShop): 17-18% y/y constant currency, USD 8.7-8.8bn (TLB-013, page 3)

### Orders
- Orders daily average during Ramadan showed a 0.6x drop in 2025 vs 0.9x drop in 2024 and 1x drop in 2023 (baseline) — a ~40% reduction in y/y seasonality impact since 2023 (TLB-013, page 17)
- Average order frequency 6.7x (Dec 2024, total orders / active customers) (TLB-013, page 32)
- Frequency per month: 13.0 for multi-vertical users vs 3.8 for food-only users (as of July 2024) (TLB-013, page 6)

### Monthly Active Customers
- >6.5mn Active Customers as of December 2024 (TLB-013, page 32)

### Talabat Pro
- talabat pro adoption growth 2.1x (Dec'24 vs Dec'23); only markets where pro live as of Dec 2024 (UAE, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain, Jordan, Oman); excludes Egypt and Iraq (TLB-013, page 8)
- Incremental pro uplift +28% (frequency, measured Sep'24 vs lookalike non-pro users) (TLB-013, page 8)
- "talabat pro now in Egypt" — full launch of talabat pro in Egypt took place in Feb 2025 (TLB-013, page 8)
- Order frequency uplift post-talabat-pro subscription >28% (TLB-013, page 6)
- New app drove +24% tPro acquisitions uplift (Nov-Dec'24 vs previous experience; excludes Egypt and Iraq) (TLB-013, page 12)
- Revenue grew faster than GMV in Q1'25 partly due to higher subscription (pro) fees (TLB-013, page 18)

### Advertising
- AdTech solutions listed under "Driving customer loyalty" / supply-partnership growth lever (TLB-013, page 4)
- Reported financials Q1'25: contribution of tMart and AdTech to revenue growth (implied via revenue-outpacing-GMV commentary) (TLB-013, page 18)

### Marketplace
- 58,000+ selected restaurant partners; 10,000+ Local shops partners with ≥1 order in Dec 2024 (TLB-013, page 6)
- >68k Active Partners as of December 2024 (TLB-013, page 32)

### Q-Commerce
- >130 tMart stores (inclusive of leased-but-not-yet-operational) (TLB-013, page 6)
- talabat acquired 100% of InstaShop to scale Grocery & Retail faster over coming years (TLB-013, page 15)
- InstaShop: >8K active partners; >2M listed SKUs; ~16% y/y GMV growth (Q1'25 constant currency); 10 years in Grocery & Retail; NPS of 54 (Oct-Dec 2024 study, 12.6k respondents) (TLB-013, page 15, page 16)
- InstaShop-only Q1 2025: GMV USD 173mn (+10% y/y reported, +16% constant currency); Adj. EBITDA USD 3.4mn (2.0% of GMV, +0.3pp y/y); GMV growth driven by order volumes and improved frequency (TLB-013, page 20)
- Only 1 out of 3 InstaShop users use talabat G&R — reinforcing complementary, largely non-overlapping customer bases (period 1 Jan-31 Mar 2025) (TLB-013, page 15)
- InstaShop market presence: UAE and Egypt (TLB-013, page 15)

### Customer Growth
- Q1'25 GMV growth driven by "stronger demand across all regions and verticals, driven by customer acquisitions, higher order frequency and expanded multi-verticality" (TLB-013, page 18)
- Customer adoption headroom (2024 data, carried from prior deck): Food 23%, Groceries 2%, Other adjacent verticals 1% of addressable population (TLB-013, page 7)
- New app drove +2% grocery acquisitions uplift (Nov-Dec'24 vs previous experience) (TLB-013, page 12)

### Retention
- "Driving customer loyalty" is a named strategic growth lever (AdTech, CPG partnerships, DineOut deals, talabat Kitchens) (TLB-013, page 4)
- Multi-vertical ecosystem framed as platform for growth via customer journey: Food → Grocery & Retail → talabat pro sign-up (TLB-013, page 6)
- 80%+ of surveyed users (Kuwait, UAE, Egypt, Qatar, Bahrain) rank talabat #1 (Aug 2024 survey) (TLB-013, page 5)
- Reduced Ramadan seasonality attributed partly to "increasing G&R adoption," implying multi-vertical usage smooths demand and supports customer retention through seasonal troughs (TLB-013, page 17)
- InstaShop users described as "monthly habitual users, ordering large baskets," an indicator of high retention/engagement among the newly acquired customer base (TLB-013, page 16)

### Subscription
- talabat pro is a monthly or annual subscription with exclusive benefits, incl. free delivery on food and groceries (TLB-013, page 6)
- Reported revenue growth in Q1'25 partly attributed to higher subscription fees (TLB-013, page 18)

### Logistics
- InstaShop "backed by strong tech and operations with control over the end-to-end experience from pickers, cars, bikes and walkers" (TLB-013, page 16)
- >123k Active Riders, <30 min average delivery time (Dec 2024 figures) (TLB-013, page 32)

### AI
- Not covered in this document beyond the carried-forward general tailwind reference to "Advance in technology improving UX (AI, Machine learning and Data analytics)" (TLB-013, page 31)

### Competition
- talabat described as "Clear leader in all our MENA countries of presence" (TLB-013, page 29)
- InstaShop framed as "One of the largest premium hyper-local on-demand players, with a superb customer experience" (TLB-013, page 15)
