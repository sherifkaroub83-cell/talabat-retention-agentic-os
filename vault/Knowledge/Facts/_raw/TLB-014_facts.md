# TLB-014 raw facts & entities

## Facts by topic

### Revenue
- Q1 2026 Revenue (talabat + instashop): USD (cFX) 1.0bn / USD 1,048mn, +22% y/y constant currency (+23% y/y nominal) (TLB-014, page 9, page 11, page 15)
- Q1 2025 pro forma Revenue comparator: USD 853mn (TLB-014, page 15)
- Revenue growth outpaced GMV in Q1'26, driven by higher talabat mart share (grocery dark stores) and adtech margins, offsetting lower commission rates, higher talabat pro adoption and increased customer incentives (TLB-014, page 15)
- From 2026 onwards, talabat aligns external and internal reporting and will only report IFRS revenue (TLB-014, page 9, footnote 2)
- FY2025 IFRS Revenue for talabat + 12 months instashop: USD 3,870mn (TLB-014, page 9)
- FY2025 actual (pro-forma): Revenue USD 3.92bn, +33% y/y (TLB-014, page 3)
- FY2026e Revenue growth guidance: 14-17% y/y constant currency, USD 4.4-4.5bn (TLB-014, page 9)

### GMV
- Q1 2026 GMV (talabat + instashop): USD (cFX) 2.7bn / USD 2,422mn, +18% y/y constant currency (+19% y/y nominal) (TLB-014, page 9, page 11, page 15)
- Q1 2025 pro forma GMV comparator: USD 1,929mn / USD 2,257mn (talabat + instashop) (TLB-014, page 15)
- Q1'26 GMV growth boosted by favourable Eid seasonality and conflict tailwinds (increased eat-at-home consumption); estimated 2-3pp contribution from Ramadan/Eid calendar shift (TLB-014, page 7, page 12, page 15, page 21)
- FY2025 actual (pro-forma): GMV USD 9.5bn, +28% y/y (TLB-014, page 3)
- FY2025 Q4 standalone GMV: USD 2,657mn, +20% y/y (TLB-014, page 3)
- FY2026e GMV growth guidance: 11-14% y/y constant currency, USD 11.2-11.5bn (TLB-014, page 9)
- 2025 GMV by vertical: Food $6.7bn (25% customer adoption), Groceries $3.5bn (3% adoption), Other adjacent verticals $0.2bn (<1% adoption) (TLB-014, page 5)

### Monthly Active Customers
- Addressable population: 75mn across talabat's 8 countries (up from 71mn in the May 2025 deck) (TLB-014, page 4, page 5)
- GMV share of multi-vertical customers grew 4pp y/y (76% Mar'26 vs 72% Mar'25) (TLB-014, page 14)

### Talabat Pro
- talabat pro GMV share (talabat-only) rose to 49% of platform GMV in Mar'26 from 32% in Mar'25 (TLB-014, page 14)
- talabat pro GMV share metric noted "+0.1pp y/y growth" for a related sub-metric context on the same slide (TLB-014, page 14)
- Everyday App investment programme (~USD 120mn) includes "enhancing the talabat pro loyalty programme to grow subscribers, strengthen coverage of key GMV partners, and expand exclusive talabat mart deals" (TLB-014, page 19)
- Food-leadership investment (~USD 55mn) targets "retaining medium- and high-value customers through our subscription programme and targeted incentives" (TLB-014, page 19)
- talabat pro GMV share reported on a talabat-only basis (excluding instashop), calculated as GMV generated by Pro subscribers divided by GMV generated by talabat platform active users (TLB-014, page 14, footnote 5)

### Advertising
- Advertising revenue margin increased to 3.4% of GMV (Q1'26) vs 3.3% (Q1'25), "demonstrating monetisation resilience amid GMV growth" (TLB-014, page 14)
- Adtech margins for talabat mart (grocery dark stores) in the UAE reached 7% of GMV in Q4'25, double the company average (TLB-014, page 19)
- Revenue growth in Q1'26 partly driven by higher adtech margins (TLB-014, page 15)

### Marketplace
- Active partner base grew 18% y/y (Mar'25 to Mar'26), with accelerated local shop onboarding deepening G&R footprint (TLB-014, page 14)
- #1 category (Food) positions across 8 countries / 75mn addressable population, with relative category share vs. next-largest platform ranging 1x+ to 10x+ (TLB-014, page 4)

### Q-Commerce
- Everyday App investment includes scaling talabat mart dark stores; ~USD 45mn capex/lease investment planned for 2026 to support grocery integrated vertical (tMart), plus ~USD 75mn opex, totaling ~$120mn (TLB-014, page 6, page 8, page 19)
- Grocery TAC c.$104bn in 2025, with only 3% customer adoption and $3.5bn 2025 GMV (TLB-014, page 5)
- G&R opportunity described as a "USD 150bn+ TAM with low single-digit online penetration" (TLB-014, page 19)
- Product-mix shift to G&R contributes a (0.5%)-(0.7%) drag on Adj. EBITDA margin in the 2026 bridge, reflecting lower commission/take-rate on grocery vs food (TLB-014, page 6, page 8)

### Customer Growth
- Q1'26 GMV growth driven by "robust order volumes supported by customer acquisition," boosted by Eid seasonality and conflict-driven eat-at-home tailwinds (TLB-014, page 15)
- Active riders expanded to 168k (Mar'26) from 134k (Mar'25), +33% y/y; >8K additional riders and >1.7K additional pickers vs Q4'25 (TLB-014, page 13, page 14)
- Partner-funded savings grew 25% y/y to $188mn (Q1'26) from $141mn (Q1'25) (TLB-014, page 14)

### Retention
- "tPro subscribers represent around half of talabat platform GMV, reflecting strong engagement and retention" (TLB-014, page 14)
- "On the customer side, we focus on retaining medium- and high-value customers through our subscription programme and targeted incentives." (TLB-014, page 19)
- "As customer behaviour becomes increasingly multi-vertical, improving frequency, basket size, and retention position talabat to scale its Everyday App across our markets." (TLB-014, page 19)
- GMV share of multi-vertical customers grew 4pp y/y, "underscoring deepening multi-vertical engagement" (TLB-014, page 14)

### Subscription
- talabat pro GMV share (talabat-only) is the primary "Loyalty" KPI tracked on the "core pillars" slide, rising from 32% (Mar'25) to 49% (Mar'26) (TLB-014, page 14)
- Everyday App investment explicitly funds talabat pro loyalty programme growth (subscriber growth, partner coverage, exclusive tMart deals) (TLB-014, page 19)

### Logistics
- Rider network expanded to 168k, "reinforcing delivery quality and strengthening the customer experience" (TLB-014, page 14)
- Rider availability recovered within hours after conflict-related disruption; >8K additional riders and >1.7K additional pickers vs Q4'25 (excluding Iraq and Egypt for some pricing metrics) (TLB-014, page 13)
- ~3 months coverage for critical SKUs within 48hrs; alternative sourcing via "Green Corridors" during supply disruption (TLB-014, page 13)
- Fuel costs described as a "modest share of delivery costs, with various levers to recover cost increases" — listed as a FY2026 guidance risk/mitigant (TLB-014, page 9)

### Competition
- "Competitive intensity: Meaningful dry powder remains to defend Food leadership, if required" — listed as FY2026 guidance risk #4 (TLB-014, page 9)
- Food relative category share vs. next-largest platform ranges from 1x+ (Iraq, at IPO) to 10x+ (Kuwait, Jordan at YE'25) across the 8 countries, including Egypt at 1x+ (IPO) rising to 4x+ (YE'25) (TLB-014, page 4)
- Investment rationale: "platforms focused exclusively on food delivery face growing competitive pressure, making multi-vertical expansion strategically essential" (TLB-014, page 19)
