# TLB-023 raw facts & entities

## Facts by topic

### Revenue
- Revenue AED 7,987.7m (USD 2.2bn) FY2023 vs AED 6,310.3m (USD 1.7bn) FY2022, +26.6% YoY (TLB-023, page 10).
- Revenue AED 7,604.3m for 1 Jan-29 Sep 2024 vs AED 5,760.9m for the same period 2023, +32.0% (TLB-023, page 7).

### GMV
- GMV AED 22,263.8m (USD 6.1bn) FY2023 vs AED 18,991.8m (USD 5.2bn) FY2022 vs AED 14,540m-equivalent (USD 3.9bn) FY2021, 24% CAGR 2021-2023 (TLB-023, page 7, page 10).
- GMV AED 19,772.5m (USD 5.4bn) for 1 Jan-29 Sep 2024 vs AED 16,303.4m same period 2023, +21.3% (TLB-023, page 7).
- Food service TAC 2023 across talabat markets: ~AED 77bn (USD 21bn); Grocery TAC: ~AED 349bn (USD 95bn); Other adjacent categories TAC: ~AED 29bn (USD 8bn) (TLB-023, page 5-6).

### Orders
- ~99% order success rate; average delivery time under 30 minutes, September 2024 (TLB-023, page 6).

### Monthly Active Customers
- More than 6 million active customers, September 2024 (TLB-023, page 5).
- Over six million monthly active customers across eight regional markets (per CEO quote) (TLB-023, page 3).

### Talabat Pro
- talabat pro subscribers saw "over 20% increase in order frequency" for September 2024 (TLB-023, page 8).
- talabat pro provides free delivery and exclusive deals for a fixed monthly or yearly fee (TLB-023, page 8).

### Advertising
- AdTech products adopted per partner increased 20% from 2021 to H1 2024 (TLB-023, page 9).
- AdTech products reduce Partners' customer acquisition costs and offer sophisticated targeting tools (TLB-023, page 9).
- Future AdTech innovation areas: automation, targeted advertisements, algorithmic efficiency (TLB-023, page 9).

### Marketplace
- talabat is "the leading on-demand online food ordering, delivery, takeaway and groceries and convenience retail marketplace in the MENA region" (TLB-023, page 5).
- Business model driven by "powerful network effects" across a three-sided marketplace (customers, Partners, riders) — the "talabat flywheel" (TLB-023, page 6).
- Category share in GCC ranges 3x-10x next largest online peer; in non-GCC countries, 2x-10x (TLB-023, page 5-6).

### Customer Growth
- Addressable population across talabat's 8 markets: ~71.1 million (of >185 million total population) (TLB-023, page 6).
- GMV for customer cohorts acquired since 2019 increased ~4x over 5 years (TLB-023, page 7).

### Retention
- "This subscription model has proven effective in boosting customer engagement and retention, with over 20% increase in order frequency amongst talabat pro subscribers for the month of September 2024." (TLB-023, page 8)
- Aggregate annual customer savings of more than AED 1,567.7 million (29 Sep 2023–29 Sep 2024), funded by Partners, B2B, and bank partnerships, cited as a value-driver for retention (TLB-023, page 6).

### Subscription
- talabat pro is a monthly/annual paid subscription offering free delivery and exclusive deals (TLB-023, page 8).

### Logistics
- Over 480 product, design, engineering, and data technologists across Dubai and Cairo tech-hubs support the logistics/service technology stack (TLB-023, page 7).
- talabat's "pioneering and scalable logistics and service technology stack" automates and personalises order placement, processing, fulfilment, delivery and support (TLB-023, page 5).

### AI
- "talabat's machine learning models allow it to introduce and cross-sell verticals, as well as initiatives such as talabat pro and talabat Rewards, at the right time for customers to benefit from." (TLB-023, page 8)
- Company leverages Delivery Hero's technology/expertise for "industry-leading innovation capabilities and tailored solutions for customers, Partners, and riders" (TLB-023, page 7).

### Competition
- Strategic alliances cited: "Zomato" in the UAE and "Otlob" in Egypt (TLB-023, page 9).
- talabat's relative category share is 3x-10x next largest online peer in GCC and 2x-10x in non-GCC countries, per OC&C analysis (TLB-023, page 5-6).
