# TLB-020 raw facts & entities

## Facts by topic

### Revenue
- Q1'26 Revenue (talabat + instashop, cFX): USD 1.0bn, +22% y/y (nominal +23% y/y); reported 1,048mn vs. 853mn pro forma Q1'25; take rate 38% → 39% of GMV (TLB-020, page 4, page 10)
- FY2026 Revenue growth guidance: 14-17% cFX (USD 4.4-4.5bn), unchanged from prior guidance (TLB-020, page 14)

### GMV
- Q1'26 GMV (talabat + instashop, cFX): USD 2.7bn, +18% y/y (nominal +19% y/y); reported 2,685mn vs. 2,257mn pro forma Q1'25 (TLB-020, page 4, page 10)
- GMV growth boosted by "favourable Eid seasonality as well as conflict tailwinds (increased eat-at-home consumption)" (TLB-020, page 10)
- Q1'26 GMV growth of 18% y/y included an estimated 2-3pp temporary boost from the Ramadan/Eid calendar shift (TLB-020, page 18)
- FY2026 GMV growth guidance: 11-14% cFX (USD 11.2-11.5bn), unchanged (TLB-020, page 14)

### Orders
- Not covered with specific order-count figures.

### Monthly Active Customers
- Not covered with specific customer-count figures in this document (operational metrics focus on partners/riders/savings instead).

### Talabat Pro
- GMV share of talabat pro subscribers: 49% (Q1'26) vs 32% (Q1'25) — "tPro subscribers represent around half of talabat platform GMV, reflecting strong engagement and retention" (TLB-020, page 8)
- 2026 investment programme includes "enhancing the talabat pro loyalty programme to grow subscribers, strengthen coverage of key GMV partners, and expand exclusive talabat mart deals" (TLB-020, page 16)
- Investment focus "on the customer side, we focus on retaining medium- and high-value customers through our subscription programme and targeted incentives" (TLB-020, page 16)

### Advertising
- Advertising Revenue Margin: 3.4% (Mar'26) vs 3.3% (Mar'25), +0.1pp; "demonstrating monetisation resilience amid GMV growth" (TLB-020, page 8)

### Marketplace
- Active partners: 93k (Mar'26) vs 79k (Mar'25), +18% y/y, "accelerated local shop onboarding" (TLB-020, page 8)

### Q-Commerce
- Everyday App investment: ~USD 120mn total (~USD 75mn opex/0.7pp of GMV + ~USD 45mn capex) for "scaling talabat mart dark stores, talabat pro loyalty programme and new verticals" (TLB-020, page 12, page 16)
- tMart adtech margins in UAE reached 7% of GMV in Q4'25, "double the company average" (TLB-020, page 16)
- On-demand grocery TAM described as "USD 150bn+ TAM with low single-digit online penetration" (TLB-020, page 16)

### Customer Growth
- GMV share of multi-vertical customers: 76% (Mar'26) vs 72% (Mar'25), +4pp y/y (TLB-020, page 8)
- "As customer behaviour becomes increasingly multi-vertical, improving frequency, basket size, and retention position talabat to scale its Everyday App across our markets" (TLB-020, page 16)

### Retention
- "tPro subscribers represent around half of talabat platform GMV, reflecting strong engagement and retention" (TLB-020, page 8)
- "On the customer side, we focus on retaining medium- and high-value customers through our subscription programme and targeted incentives" (TLB-020, page 16)
- Everyday App investment framed as improving "frequency, basket size, and retention" (TLB-020, page 16)
- Partner-funded savings reached "all-time high of 7% of the GMV" in Q1'26 (TLB-020, page 7)

### Subscription
- talabat pro GMV penetration reached ~49% of platform GMV by Q1'26 (TLB-020, page 8)

### Logistics
- Active riders: 168k (Mar'26) vs 134k (Mar'25), +25% y/y (TLB-020, page 8)
- Rider/picker network expansion during conflict period: >8K additional riders, >1.7K additional pickers vs. Q4'25 (TLB-020, page 7)
- "Rider availability recovered within hours" following conflict disruption (TLB-020, page 7)

### Competition
- "Competitive intensity: Meaningful dry powder remains to defend Food leadership, if required" — listed as a risk/mitigant for FY2026 guidance (TLB-020, page 14)
- Food-leadership investment (~USD 55mn) explicitly framed as defending against "both new entrants and incumbents," investing in CVP rather than matching competitor discounts/vouchers (TLB-020, page 16)
