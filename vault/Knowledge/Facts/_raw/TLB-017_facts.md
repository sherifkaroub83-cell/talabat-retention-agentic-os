## Facts by topic

### Revenue
- Q1'25 Management Revenue (talabat-only, excl. instashop): USD 846mn, +34% y/y reported / +38% y/y constant currency (TLB-017, page 4, page 10)
- Q1'25 revenue margin: 39.5% of GMV (Q1'24) rising to 40.6% (Q1'25) (TLB-017, page 10)
- Reported financials bridge (talabat + instashop, IFRS): Management Revenue USD 857mn (TLB-017, page 16)
- FY2025 revenue growth guidance: 18-20% y/y cFX (USD 3.49-3.55bn); Q1'25 actual 38% y/y (USD 873mn cFX) (TLB-017, page 13)

### GMV
- Q1'25 GMV (talabat-only): USD 2.1bn (2,084mn), +30% y/y reported / +33% y/y cFX (TLB-017, page 4, page 10)
- InstaShop-only Q1'25 GMV: USD 173mn, +10% y/y reported / +16% y/y cFX (TLB-017, page 12)
- FY2025 GMV growth guidance: 17-18% y/y cFX (USD 8.7-8.8bn); Q1'25 actual 33% y/y (USD 2.1bn) (TLB-017, page 13)

### Orders
- Ramadan-driven daily order seasonality: y/y drop reduced from 1x (2023) to 0.9x (2024) to 0.6x (2025), a ~40% reduction since 2023 (TLB-017, page 9)

### Marketplace
- InstaShop: >8K active partners, >2M listed SKUs (TLB-017, page 6)

### Q-Commerce
- talabat acquired 100% of InstaShop "to scale G&R faster over the coming years" (TLB-017, page 6)
- InstaShop GMV growth ~16% y/y cFX (Q1'25 vs Q1'24), driven by order volumes and improved frequency (TLB-017, page 6, page 12)
- InstaShop Adj. EBITDA margin improved to 2.0% of GMV (+0.3pp y/y), with further improvement expected from revenue and cost synergies post-acquisition (TLB-017, page 12)

### Customer Growth
- InstaShop customer base overlap: only 1 in 3 InstaShop users use talabat G&R — "2 out of every 3 of instashop users are not active on talabat's Grocery & Retail," reinforcing complementary customer bases (data for 1 Jan - 31 Mar 2025) (TLB-017, page 6)
- Strong GMV growth "due to stronger demand across all regions and verticals, driven by customer acquisitions, higher order frequency and expanded multi-verticality" (TLB-017, page 10)

### Retention
- InstaShop users described as "monthly habitual users, ordering large baskets" (TLB-017, page 7)
- InstaShop NPS of 54 (study Oct-Dec 2024, 12.6k respondents) (TLB-017, page 7)
- "Previously purchased filter" and "Substitution/Replacement Flow" cited as UX features supporting convenience/reorder behavior (TLB-017, page 7)
- Reduced Ramadan seasonality (~40% improvement since 2023) attributed partly to "increasing G&R adoption" (TLB-017, page 9)
- "Driving customer loyalty" retained as growth lever (AdTech solutions, CPG partnerships, new initiatives) (TLB-017, page 5)
