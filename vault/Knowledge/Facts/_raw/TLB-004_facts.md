# TLB-004 raw facts & entities

## Facts by topic

### Revenue
- Revenue for the period since inception (3 Sep–31 Dec 2024): USD 801,268,830 (TLB-004, page 10).
- Revenue by type: Commission fees USD 290,787,842; Delivery fees USD 160,717,059; Advertising and listing fees USD 66,169,147; Service fees USD 30,262,859; Subscription fees USD 9,728,074; Other direct income USD 265,013,936; less Vouchers USD (14,297,092) and Other revenue reduction USD (7,112,995); Total USD 801,268,830 (TLB-004, page 49).
- Revenue recognised at a point in time: USD 725,371,609; over time: USD 75,897,221 (TLB-004, page 49).
- Directors' Report (proforma, full-year basis vs FY2023): Revenue increased by 32%, surpassing the upper limit of guidance, to reach USD 3.0 billion (TLB-004, page 3).
- Cost of sales: USD (546,241,012); Gross profit: USD 255,027,818 (TLB-004, page 10).
- GCC segment constitutes more than 75% of the Group's total revenue and assets (TLB-004, page 37).
- Non-GCC countries (Egypt, Jordan, Iraq) do not individually meet the 10% threshold for revenue, assets, or profits and are disclosed only under "other segments" (TLB-004, page 37).

### GMV
- Directors' Report: Group achieved a 23% increase in Gross Merchandise Value (GMV) compared to the preceding year, reaching a record high of USD 7.4 billion (proforma basis) (TLB-004, page 3).
- Adjusted EBITDA increased by 55%, attaining nearly USD 500 million, equivalent to 6.7% of GMV (TLB-004, page 3).
- Net income rose by 64% to USD 346 million, representing 4.7% of GMV (proforma) (TLB-004, page 3).
- Adjusted (like-for-like) net income USD 393 million, or 5.3% of GMV, +53% (TLB-004, page 3).
- GMV for delivery-only stores is defined as the total value paid by customers (including VAT, delivery fees, service fees, less subsidies such as vouchers and discounts) (TLB-004, page 24).

### Orders
- Revenue from commission fees is recognised at a point in time when the order has been placed (TLB-004, page 24).
- Revenue from delivery fees is recognised at a point in time when the order is delivered (TLB-004, page 24).
- Revenue from delivery-only stores sales is recognised at a point in time when the order is delivered (TLB-004, page 25).

### Talabat Pro
- Not named explicitly by brand in this document; see Subscription topic below.

### Advertising
- Advertising and listing fees revenue: USD 66,169,147 for the period since inception (TLB-004, page 49).
- "For advertising services to restaurants and other businesses (customer of the service), the Group entities also act as principal. The control over the advertising services passes to the customer over time. Revenue for advertising services is recognised based on the time elapsed relative to the contract term at the reporting date." (TLB-004, page 25).
- Group's principal activities include "ad-tech solutions to partners" (TLB-004, page 3).

### Marketplace
- "The Group generates revenue mainly from online marketplace services, separately charged delivery fees, orders placed in the Group's delivery-only stores and advertising services, as well as subscription fees, service fees and, in certain cases, separately charged payment fees." (TLB-004, page 24).
- For online marketplace services in which the Group arranges for restaurants to sell food to orderers, the Group acts as an agent, earning commission fees (TLB-004, page 24).

### Q-Commerce
- The Group's judgment on principal-vs-agent classification for delivery services references "logistics services not provided by restaurant or quick commerce partners" (TLB-004, page 38).

### Customer Growth
- Cohort models used for revenue planning "analyse the past order behaviour of (local) end customers and apply statistical methods to forecast the future behaviour of existing end customers. Future revenue from new end customers is derived from the planned marketing expenses and the development of estimated acquisition costs per new end customer." (TLB-004, page 43).

### Retention
- "The key inputs of the cohort models include the customer retention/reorder rate, customer activity rate, average order size, and commission rates." Used in Talabat UAE and Talabat Kuwait goodwill impairment-testing (discounted cash flow) models (TLB-004, page 43).

### Subscription
- Subscription fees revenue: USD 9,728,074 for the period since inception through 31 December 2024 (TLB-004, page 49).
- "For subscription programs offered to orderers and restaurants, the Group acts as principal. Revenue from subscription fees is recognised on a straight-line basis over the period of the subscription." (TLB-004, page 24).

### Logistics
- "The Group also offers delivery services in which the ordered meals or other products are collected at a restaurant or store and delivered to the orderers. The Group acts as principal for delivery services." (TLB-004, page 24).
- Delivery fees revenue: USD 160,717,059 for the period since inception (TLB-004, page 49).
- Judgement note: "the Group has concluded that it acts as a principal for organizing and providing delivery services through its platforms, as it is primarily responsible for carrying out the delivery and controls the delivery service before it is transferred to the orderer." (TLB-004, page 38).
