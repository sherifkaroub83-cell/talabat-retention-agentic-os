### Revenue
- Q1 2026 Revenue: USD 1,048M (+23% y/y); USD 1,041M cFX (+22%); conversion ratio 39% of GMV (prior year 38%) (TLB-011, page 4).
- Revenue mix as % of GMV Q1 2026: Commissions 12.8%; Delivery fees 6.1%; Service fees 2.2%; Subscription fees 1.0%; Own grocery & other income 15.2%; Advertising and listing fees 3.4%; Vouchers & other discounts -1.7% (TLB-011, page 4).
