# TLB-008 raw facts & entities

## Facts by topic

### Revenue
- Revenue for the full period since inception (3 Sep 2024–31 Dec 2025): USD 4,651,861,456 (TLB-008, page 10).
- Revenue by type: Commission fees USD 1,631,764,688; Delivery fees USD 839,271,891; Advertising and listing fees USD 397,341,762; Service fees USD 239,606,826; Subscription fees USD 83,790,496; Other direct income USD 1,604,260,347; less Vouchers USD (104,841,997) and Other revenue reduction USD (39,332,557); Total USD 4,651,861,456 (TLB-008, page 52).
- Revenue recognised at a point in time: USD 4,170,729,198; over time: USD 481,132,258 (TLB-008, page 52).
- Directors' Report (calendar year 2025 vs 2024): Revenue grew by 31% to USD 3.9 billion, in line with the guided range (TLB-008, page 3).
- Cost of sales: USD (3,234,120,644); Gross profit: USD 1,417,740,812 (TLB-008, page 10).
- Segment revenue (external, since inception): GCC USD 3,795,385,553; Egypt USD 509,936,177; Non-GCC (Jordan, Iraq) USD 346,539,726; Total USD 4,651,861,456 (TLB-008, page 61).
- Revenue by country (material countries, since inception): United Arab Emirates USD 1,680,452,563; Kuwait USD 1,175,785,453; Egypt USD 509,936,177; Qatar USD 462,169,888; Others USD 823,517,375; Total USD 4,651,861,456 (TLB-008, page 61).
- GCC segment constitutes more than 75% of the Group's total revenue and assets, far exceeding the 10% IFRS 8.13 threshold (TLB-008, page 39).

### GMV
- Directors' Report: Group delivered a 27% year-on-year increase in GMV, reaching a record USD 9.4 billion, in line with guidance range (calendar year 2025) (TLB-008, page 3).
- Adjusted EBITDA increased by 24% to nearly USD 615 million, representing 6.5% of GMV (TLB-008, page 3).
- Net income rose by 34% to USD 464 million, equivalent to 4.9% of GMV; like-for-like net income USD 451 million, +15%, 4.8% of GMV (TLB-008, page 3).

### Orders
- Revenue from commission fees is recognised at a point in time when the order has been placed (policy unchanged from TLB-004) (TLB-008, page 22 area, Note 3.3).
- Revenue from delivery fees and delivery-only store sales is recognised at a point in time when the order is delivered (TLB-008, Note 3.3).

### Talabat Pro
- Not named explicitly by brand in this document; see Subscription topic below.

### Advertising
- Advertising and listing fees revenue: USD 397,341,762 for the period since inception (TLB-008, page 52).
- Group's principal activities include "ad-tech solutions to partners" (TLB-008, page 3).

### Marketplace
- Reportable segments (GCC and Egypt) "generate revenue mainly from online marketplace services, separately charged delivery fees, orders placed in the Group's delivery-only stores and advertising services, as well as subscription fees, service fees and, in certain cases, separately charged payment fees." (TLB-008, page 60).

### Q-Commerce
- Judgement note on principal-vs-agent classification references "logistics services not provided by restaurant or quick commerce partners" (TLB-008, page 40).

### Customer Growth
- Cohort models for revenue planning "analyse the past order behaviour of (local) end customers and apply statistical methods to forecast the future behaviour of existing end customers. Future revenue from new end customers is derived from the planned marketing expenses and the development of estimated acquisition costs per new end customer." (TLB-008, page 45).

### Retention
- "The key inputs of the cohort models include the customer retention/reorder rate, customer activity rate, average order size, and commission rates." Used in Talabat UAE and Talabat Kuwait goodwill impairment-testing (DCF) models (TLB-008, page 45).
- Egypt meeting IFRS 8 quantitative thresholds to become its own reportable segment (rather than remaining bundled in "Non-GCC") signals materially increased scale and analytical/strategic visibility for Egypt operations at the Group (CODM) level (TLB-008, page 39).

### Subscription
- Subscription fees revenue: USD 83,790,496 for the period since inception through 31 December 2025, up from USD 9,728,074 disclosed in TLB-004 (FY2024, 120-day period) — periods are of different length (~16 months vs. 120 days) so not a like-for-like growth rate (TLB-008, page 52).
- "For subscription programs offered to orderers and restaurants, the Group acts as principal. Revenue from subscription fees is recognised on a straight-line basis over the period of the subscription." (policy reiterated from TLB-004) (TLB-008, Note 3.3).

### Logistics
- Delivery fees revenue: USD 839,271,891 for the period since inception (TLB-008, page 52).
- Segment delivery cost: GCC USD (1,523,248,572); Egypt USD (113,128,547); Non-GCC USD (167,545,960) (TLB-008, page 61).
