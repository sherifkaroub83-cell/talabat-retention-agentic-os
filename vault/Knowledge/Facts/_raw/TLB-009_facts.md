# TLB-009 raw facts & entities

## Facts by topic

### Revenue
- Q4 2025 Management Revenue: USD 1,039M (+26% y/y), conversion ratio 42% of GMV (TLB-009, page 2).
- FY2025 Management Revenue: USD 3,876M reported (+31% y/y); USD 3,917M cFX (+33% y/y) (TLB-009, page 4).
- FY2025 IFRS Revenue: USD 3,756 million (TLB-009, page 5, footnote 16).
- Q4 2025 revenue components: Commission fees USD 329M (+13%); Subscription fee & Other Income USD 402M (+46%); Delivery & Service Fees USD 221M (+16%); Advertising and listing fees USD 87M (+30%) (TLB-009, page 4).
- FY2025 revenue components: Commission fees USD 1,297M (+22%); Subscription fee & Other Income USD 1,397M (+47%); Delivery & Service Fees USD 859M (+24%); Advertising and listing fees USD 323M (+32%) (TLB-009, page 4).

### GMV
- Q4 2025 GMV: USD 2,476M (+21% y/y); GCC USD 1,974M (+15%, 80% of total); non-GCC USD 501M (+57%, 20% of total) (TLB-009, page 4).
- Q4 2025 GMV split by vertical: Food USD 1,687M (+12%, 68% of total); G&R USD 788M (+45%, 32% of total) (TLB-009, page 4).
- FY2025 GMV: USD 9,421M reported (+27% y/y); USD 9,512M cFX (+28% y/y) (TLB-009, page 4).

### Talabat Pro
- "The programme is now also available in all eight markets following successful launches in Egypt and Iraq in 2025." (TLB-009, page 3)
- talabat pro benefits: free delivery, booster discounts on Food best sellers, discounts on talabat mart key value items, on-time guarantee, priority customer support, dine-out discounts, and partner services (streaming, ride-hailing) (TLB-009, page 3).
- "a surge in talabat pro adoption" cited as a Q4 2025 GMV growth driver (TLB-009, page 1).
- 2026 investment plan allocates part of >USD 100 million to "strengthening talabat pro as a multi-vertical engagement engine," focused on "enhancing value for both customers and participating vendors" (TLB-009, page 3).

### Advertising
- Q4 2025 Advertising and listing fees: USD 87M (+30% y/y); FY2025: USD 323M (+32% y/y) (TLB-009, page 4).

### Q-Commerce
- talabat mart described as the "grocery integrated vertical"; G&R (Grocery & Retail) GMV grew faster than Food GMV in both Q4 2025 (+45% vs +12%) and FY2025 (TLB-009, pages 2, 4).
- 2026 investment plan (>USD 100 million) includes "improving affordability to accelerate customer adoption, increasing store density to reinforce the speed-led value proposition and build capacity for future growth, and expanding supply chain infrastructure to enhance assortment and availability" for talabat mart (TLB-009, page 2).

### Customer Growth
- Q4 2025 GMV growth reflected "robust order volume growth (customer acquisition and higher order frequency) across all markets" (TLB-009, page 1).
- Company boilerplate: "serving over six and a half million active customers as of December 2024" (dated figure, not FY2025) (TLB-009, page 6).

### Retention
- talabat pro framed as a "multi-vertical engagement engine" central to the 2026 investment strategy (TLB-009, page 3).
- CEO quote: aim to "expand our multi-vertical subscriber base by enhancing the value proposition of our talabat pro loyalty subscription programme" (TLB-009, page 3).

### Subscription
- "Subscription fee & Other Income" line grew 46% y/y in Q4 2025 to USD 402M and 47% y/y in FY2025 to USD 1,397M — the fastest-growing revenue component disclosed (TLB-009, page 4). Note: this line combines subscription fees with other income and is not a pure subscription-only figure.
