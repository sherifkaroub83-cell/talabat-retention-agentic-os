# TLB-012 raw facts & entities

## Facts by topic

### Revenue
- 2024 Total Management Revenue: USD 3.0bn, +32% y/y (TLB-012, page 13)
- Revenue growth exceeded GMV growth mainly due to further upside from AdTech revenues, and contribution of tMart business, which represented ~28% of revenue in 2024 (TLB-012, page 19)
- FY2024 revenue growth guidance was 28-30% (reported currency); actual 32%, exceeded (TLB-012, page 24)
- FY2025 revenue growth guidance: 18-20% y/y (constant currency) (TLB-012, page 24)

### GMV
- 2024 Total GMV: ~USD 7.4bn, +23% y/y (TLB-012, page 4, page 13)
- GCC GMV growth ~20% y/y in 2024; non-GCC GMV growth ~42% y/y in 2024, growing from a lower base (TLB-012, page 20)
- Grocery & Retail (G&R) segment contributed ~25% of total GMV in 2024 (TLB-012, page 20)
- FY2024 GMV growth guidance was 22-23%; actual 23%, in line at high end of range (TLB-012, page 24)
- FY2025 GMV growth guidance: 17-18% y/y (constant currency) (TLB-012, page 24)
- talabat 2024 GMV of ~$7.4bn compares to c.$15bn addressable Foodservice+Grocery+Retail e-commerce category, c.$136bn "Eat-at-home + Grocery + High Potential retail e-commerce," and c.$299bn overall Foodservice and Retail market (TLB-012, page 6)

### Orders
- Average order frequency of 6.7x, calculated as total orders for December 2024 divided by December 2024 Active Customers (TLB-012, page 8)
- 100M lifetime orders milestone and 1M orders/day milestone reached historically (company timeline) (TLB-012, page 5)
- Average delivery time <30 minutes (December 2024) (TLB-012, page 7, page 8)

### Monthly Active Customers
- >6.5mn Active Customers as of December 2024 (TLB-012, page 8)
- Frequency per month for food-only users: 3.8; frequency per month for multi-vertical users: 13.0 (as of July 2024) (TLB-012, page 9)

### Talabat Pro
- talabat pro adoption growth of 2.1x (Dec'24 vs Dec'23), only considering markets where pro was live as of Dec 2024 (UAE, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain, Jordan, Oman); excludes Egypt and Iraq (TLB-012, page 17)
- Incremental pro uplift of +28% (frequency, measured Sep'24, comparing pro joiners to lookalike non-pro users) (TLB-012, page 17)
- "talabat pro now in Egypt" — full launch of talabat pro in Egypt took place in Feb 2025 (TLB-012, page 17)
- Order frequency uplift post-subscription to talabat pro >20% (for the month of July 2024) (TLB-012, page 9)
- New personalized app drove +24% tPro acquisitions uplift (Nov-Dec'24 vs previous experience; comparison excludes Egypt and Iraq) (TLB-012, page 16)
- talabat pro featured FREE delivery (subject to minimum order value) and exclusive deals across a wide selection of vendors (TLB-012, page 17)
- talabat launched PostPaid and talabat pro as part of its historical company milestones (TLB-012, page 5)

### Advertising
- AdTech revenues cited as a driver of revenue growth exceeding GMV growth in 2024 (TLB-012, page 19)
- AdTech solutions listed as a lever under "Turbo-charging customer loyalty" / "Deeper supply partnerships" growth strategy (TLB-012, page 10, page 14)
- Partner ROAS (Return on Ads Spend for CPC) of 4.9x (TLB-012, page 8)

### Marketplace
- Category-leading on-demand online food ordering, delivery, takeaway and Grocery & Retail marketplace across 8 MENA countries (TLB-012, page 4)
- >68k Active Partners; 55,000+ selected restaurant partners; 9,300+ Local shops partners with ≥1 order in July 2024 (TLB-012, page 7, page 8, page 9)

### Q-Commerce
- 129 strategically located tMart stores (inclusive of leased-but-not-yet-operational) as of the presentation date (TLB-012, page 9)
- tMart business grew significantly, representing ~28% of revenue in 2024 (TLB-012, page 19)
- Launch of Grocery & Retail delivery listed as a historical company milestone (TLB-012, page 5)

### Customer Growth
- Demand growth (acquisition & frequency) listed under "Deeper category penetration" growth lever (TLB-012, page 10, page 14)
- Customer adoption headroom: Food 23%, Groceries 2%, Other adjacent verticals 1% of total addressable population (TLB-012, page 11)
- New app experience drove +2% grocery acquisitions uplift (Nov-Dec'24 vs previous experience) (TLB-012, page 16)

### Retention
- "Turbo-charging customer loyalty" identified as one of the core strategic growth levers (AdTech, CPG partnerships, new initiatives like DineOut deals, talabat Kitchens) (TLB-012, page 10, page 14)
- "Fully multi-vertical ecosystem driving loyalty, platform spend and expansion" cited as a Key Company Highlight (TLB-012, page 4)
- Customer value proposition refined across Quality, Convenience, and Value dimensions, with rewards programs, targeted offers and subscription named as key loyalty levers (TLB-012, page 7)
- 80%+ of surveyed users (Kuwait, UAE, Egypt, Qatar, Bahrain) rank talabat as #1 platform (Aug 2024 survey) (TLB-012, page 7)

### Subscription
- talabat pro is talabat's premium subscription programme with monthly or annual options, free delivery and exclusive benefits (TLB-012, page 9, page 17)

### Logistics
- >123k Active Riders as of December 2024 (TLB-012, page 8)
- Average delivery time <30 minutes for December 2024 (TLB-012, page 7, page 8)
- Improved logistics efficiency cited as a driver of Adjusted EBITDA margin expansion in 2024 (TLB-012, page 21)
- Launch of own-delivery service listed as a historical company milestone (TLB-012, page 5)

### AI
- "Advance in technology improving UX (AI, Machine learning and Data analytics)" listed as a secular tailwind supporting category growth (TLB-012, page 6)

### Competition
- talabat competes with all available offline and online ordering, takeaway and delivery channels, including phone/direct orders (TLB-012, page 4, footnote 1)
- talabat described as "Clear leader in all our MENA countries of presence" (TLB-012, page 4)
