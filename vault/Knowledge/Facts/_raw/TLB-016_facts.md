# TLB-016 raw facts & entities

## Facts by topic

### Revenue
- FY2024 Total Management Revenue: USD 3.0bn, +32% y/y (TLB-016, page 5)
- Revenue growth exceeded GMV growth mainly due to further upside from AdTech revenues, and contribution of tMart business, ~28% of revenue in 2024 (TLB-016, page 11)
- FY2024 revenue growth guidance was 28-30%; actual 32%, exceeded (TLB-016, page 16)
- FY2025 revenue growth guidance: 18-20% y/y constant currency (TLB-016, page 16)

### GMV
- FY2024 Total GMV: ~USD 7.4bn, +23% y/y (TLB-016, page 4, page 5, page 11)
- GCC GMV growth ~20% y/y in 2024; non-GCC GMV growth ~42% y/y in 2024, from a lower base; non-GCC contribution ~16% (TLB-016, page 12)
- Grocery & Retail (G&R) segment ~25% of total GMV in 2024, up from ~21%; G&R y/y growth ~46-47% (TLB-016, page 12)
- FY2024 GMV growth guidance was 22-23%; actual 23%, in line at high end of range (TLB-016, page 16)
- FY2025 GMV growth guidance: 17-18% y/y constant currency (TLB-016, page 16)

### Orders
- Not covered in detail beyond order-frequency-driven GMV growth commentary (TLB-016, page 11)

### Monthly Active Customers
- Strong GMV growth attributed to increased number of active customers and higher order frequency across all regions (TLB-016, page 11)

### Talabat Pro
- talabat pro adoption growth 2.1x (Dec'24 vs Dec'23), only counting markets live as of Dec 2024 (UAE, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain, Jordan, Oman); excludes Egypt and Iraq (TLB-016, page 9)
- Incremental pro uplift (frequency) +28%, measured Sep'24 vs. lookalike non-pro users (TLB-016, page 9)
- talabat pro featured FREE delivery (subject to minimum order values, e.g. UAE AED 30 MOV for pro restaurants/tMart, AED 50 for grocery stores) and exclusive deals (DineOut Deals only in UAE) (TLB-016, page 9)
- "talabat pro now in Egypt" — full launch of talabat pro in Egypt took place in Feb 2025 (TLB-016, page 9)
- New personalized app drove +24% tPro acquisitions uplift (Nov-Dec'24 vs previous experience; excludes Egypt and Iraq) and +2% grocery acquisitions uplift (TLB-016, page 8)

### Advertising
- AdTech solutions cited as a driver of revenue growth exceeding GMV growth in 2024 (TLB-016, page 11)
- AdTech solutions listed under "Deeper supply partnerships" growth lever (TLB-016, page 6)
- "AdTech or advertising" defined as non-commission based revenues (NCR) (TLB-016, page 18)

### Marketplace
- Category-leading on-demand food ordering, delivery, takeaway and Grocery & Retail marketplace in 8 MENA countries (TLB-016, page 4)

### Q-Commerce
- tMart business grew significantly, representing ~28% of revenue in 2024 (TLB-016, page 11)

### Customer Growth
- "Demand growth (Acquisition & frequency)" listed under "Deeper category penetration" growth lever (TLB-016, page 6)
- Grocery acquisitions uplift +2% (new app vs previous experience, Nov-Dec'24) (TLB-016, page 8)

### Retention
- "Turbo-charging customer loyalty" is one of five levers for sustained growth and profitability (AdTech solutions, CPG partnerships, new initiatives — DineOut deals, talabat Kitchens) (TLB-016, page 6)
- "Fully multi-vertical ecosystem driving loyalty, platform spend and expansion" cited as Key Company Highlight (TLB-016, page 4)
- Company narrative arc: "Delivering food to your doorstep" (2016) → "Delivering food and a lot more" (2021) → "It's not just talabat app, it's your app" (2025) (TLB-016, page 7)
- New personalized app strategy pillars: "Unlock Personalisation," "Showcase Relevant Choice," "Strengthen Multi-Verticality" (TLB-016, page 8)

### Subscription
- talabat pro described as "our premium subscription programme, featuring FREE delivery and exclusive deals across a wide selection of vendors" (TLB-016, page 9)
- Plans to accelerate talabat pro adoption & strengthen value proposition, "more to come in 2025" (TLB-016, page 9)
