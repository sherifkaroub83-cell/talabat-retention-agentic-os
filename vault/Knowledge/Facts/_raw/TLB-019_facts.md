# TLB-019 raw facts & entities

## Facts by topic

### Revenue
- FY2025 Management Revenue (talabat-only): USD 3.92bn (3,876mn), +33% y/y cFX; pro-forma incl. instashop USD 4.04bn, +32% y/y (TLB-019, page 5, page 13)
- Q4'25 Management Revenue (talabat-only, cFX): USD 1.0bn (1,039mn), +26% y/y (TLB-019, page 5, page 13)
- Revenue by fee type (% of GMV, FY'25): Commission Fees 14.2-16.2%; Delivery & Service Fees 12.8-14.8%; Advertising & Listing Fees 8.9-9.4%; Subscription Fees & Other Income 3.3-3.5% (TLB-019, page 13)
- FY2025 revenue growth 33% actual vs. 18-20% original guidance / 29-32% revised guidance — exceeded (TLB-019, page 5)
- FY2026 Revenue growth guidance: 14-17% cFX (USD 4.4-4.5bn); talabat to report IFRS Revenue only from 2026 onward; FY2025 IFRS Revenue for talabat+12m instashop = USD 3,870mn (TLB-019, page 18)

### GMV
- FY2025 GMV (talabat-only): USD 9.5bn (9,421mn), +28% y/y cFX; pro-forma incl. instashop USD 10.2bn, +27% y/y (TLB-019, page 5, page 13)
- Q4'25 GMV (talabat-only, cFX): USD 2.5bn, +21% y/y (TLB-019, page 5, page 13)
- GMV by geography FY2025: GCC USD 7,702mn (+22% y/y, 81% contribution); Non-GCC USD 1,719mn (+57% y/y, 18% contribution) (TLB-019, page 14)
- GMV by vertical FY2025: Food USD 6,652mn (+20% y/y); G&R USD 2,768mn (+47% y/y, ~32% of GMV in Q4'25) (TLB-019, page 14)
- FY2025 GMV growth 28% actual vs. 17-18% original guidance / 27-29% revised guidance — in line, mid-range (TLB-019, page 5)
- FY2026 GMV growth guidance: 11-14% cFX (USD 11.2-11.5bn), reflecting a larger base, more competitive environment, macro headwinds, and instashop dilution (instashop = 7% of pro-forma FY'25 GMV, grew 12% in FY'25 cFX) (TLB-019, page 18)

### Orders
- Not covered with specific order-count figures; frequency covered under Monthly Active Customers.

### Monthly Active Customers
- Platform MAUs: 6.4mn (Q4'24) → 7.6mn (Q4'25), +19% y/y; customer base grew by 1.2mn in 2025 (TLB-019, page 8)
- Platform customer frequency: 6.7 (Q4'24) → 6.8 (Q4'25); Top 5% customer frequency: 36.1 (Q4'24) → 37.9 (Q4'25) (TLB-019, page 8)
- Customer & GMV mix (competitive markets UAE/Kuwait/Qatar, Q4'25): high-value customers 39% of base / 81% of GMV; non-high-value 52%/16%; new customers 9%/3% (TLB-019, page 9)
- Q4'25 y/y M1 retention change (competitive markets): high-value +6%, non-high-value -4%, new ~0% (TLB-019, page 9)
- Q4'25 y/y frequency change (competitive markets): high-value +1%, non-high-value ~0%, new -3% (TLB-019, page 9)

### Talabat Pro
- GMV share of talabat pro subscribers: 25% ("1.9x growth"), underpinning "one-quarter of the MAUs" (TLB-019, page 7)
- talabat pro delivers +26% retention uplift and +28% frequency uplift vs. lookalike non-pro customers (TLB-019, page 11)
- M1 retention +16pp higher for mono-vertical talabat pro subscribers vs. mono-vertical non-subscribers; +20pp higher for multi-vertical pro subscribers vs. mono-vertical non-subscribers (Q4 2025 average) (TLB-019, page 10)
- 2026 investment plan explicitly includes "Deepening the value customers receive from our subscription proposition" (TLB-019, page 11)

### Advertising
- Advertising Revenue Margin: 3.5% of GMV (Q4'25) vs 3.3% (Q4'24), +0.2pp; NMR now >20% of AdRev; tMart NMR ~7% of tMart sales in UAE (TLB-019, page 7)

### Marketplace
- Active partners: 84k (Dec'25) vs 69k (Dec'24), +22% y/y, with even faster growth on local shops (TLB-019, page 7)

### Q-Commerce
- tMart: ~47% YoY GMV growth (Q4'25 vs Q4'24); <1.5% TAC (Total Addressable Category) penetration per Redseer analysis (updated Jan 2026) (TLB-019, page 11)
- 2026 investment: ~USD 45mn capex on tMarts, part of >USD 100mn total ecosystem investment (TLB-019, page 11)
- G&R vertical GMV FY2025: USD 2,768mn, +47% y/y, now 32% of total GMV in Q4'25 (TLB-019, page 14)

### Customer Growth
- Customer base grew by 1.2mn (+19% y/y) in 2025, reflecting continued momentum (TLB-019, page 8)
- GMV share of multi-vertical customers: 73% (Dec'25) vs 68% (Dec'24), +5.4pp; "multi-vertical customers now exceed one-third of the base, indicating higher lifetime value and engagement" (TLB-019, page 7)

### Retention
- "In 2025, we expanded our customer base by 1.2mn and deepened engagement across customer tiers" (TLB-019, page 8)
- "High value customers remain sticky and continue to increase their frequency, despite competitive pressure" (TLB-019, page 9)
- "Loyalty and multi-verticality as key retention drivers" — M1 retention +16pp (mono-vertical subscriber) / +20pp (multi-vertical subscriber) vs. mono-vertical non-subscriber (TLB-019, page 10)
- talabat pro: +26% retention uplift, +28% frequency uplift vs. non-pro (TLB-019, page 11)
- "Ecosystem products like pro underpin one-quarter of the MAUs, translating to higher engagement & better retention" (TLB-019, page 7)
- >USD 100mn ecosystem investment in 2026 explicitly framed as "Doubling down on building talabat as the everyday app" (TLB-019, page 11)

### Subscription
- talabat pro GMV share 25% of platform GMV, "1.9x growth" (TLB-019, page 7)

### Logistics
- Active riders: 157k (Dec'25) vs 123k (Dec'24), +27% y/y; "rider base expansion continues to support a strong customer experience, even in highly competitive markets" (TLB-019, page 7)
- Partner-funded savings: $159mn (Q4'25) vs $138mn (Q4'24), +15% y/y; $588mn full-year 2025 (TLB-019, page 7)

### AI
- Not covered in this document.

### Competition
- Rider base growth noted "even in highly competitive markets like UAE... with 5 key players" (TLB-019, page 7)
- Non-high-value customer M1 retention declined -4% y/y in competitive markets (UAE, Kuwait, Qatar), attributed to "competitive pressure" (TLB-019, page 9)
- FY2026 guidance drivers cite "more competitive environment (new entrants and incumbents)" as a headwind (TLB-019, page 18)
