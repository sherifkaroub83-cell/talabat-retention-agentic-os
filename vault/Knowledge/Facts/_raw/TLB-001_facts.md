# TLB-001 raw facts & entities

## Facts by topic

### Revenue
- Management Revenue reached USD 2,956mn in 2024, up 32% YoY from USD 2,242mn in 2023, representing 40% of GMV (2023: 37%) (TLB-001, page 27).
- IFRS Revenue (proforma basis) was USD 2,871,862,270 in 2024 vs USD 2,175,013,998 in 2023 (TLB-001, page 27).
- Revenue composition 2024: Commission fees USD 1,062mn (+25% YoY); Subscription fee & Other Income USD 952mn (+44% YoY); Delivery & Service fees USD 696mn (+29% YoY); Advertising and listing fees USD 246mn (+27% YoY) (TLB-001, page 27).
- Revenue by region (Management Revenue basis, 2024): GCC USD 2,469.7mn; Non-GCC USD 486.3mn (TLB-001, page 94).
- Revenue by region (Management Revenue basis, 2023): GCC USD 1,934.1mn; Non-GCC USD 307.5mn (TLB-001, page 94).
- Audited consolidated interim revenue (entity's actual life, 3 Sept–31 Dec 2024 only): USD 801,268,830 (TLB-001, page 101).
- Revenue recognised at a point in time: USD 725,371,609; over time: USD 75,897,221 (period 3 Sept–31 Dec 2024) (TLB-001, page 125).

### GMV
- FY2024 GMV: USD 7,428mn (USD 7.4bn), up 23% YoY from USD 6,062mn (USD 6.1bn) in 2023 (TLB-001, page 6, page 26).
- GMV CAGR 2015-2024: 48% (TLB-001, page 7).
- GMV by segment 2024: GCC USD 6,332mn (+20% YoY, 85% of total GMV); non-GCC USD 1,096mn (+42% YoY, surpassed USD 1bn for the first time) (TLB-001, page 26).
- GMV by segment 2023: GCC USD 5,289mn (87% of total GMV); non-GCC USD 773mn (TLB-001, page 26).
- GMV by vertical 2024: Food Vertical USD 5,542mn (+16% YoY, 75% share, 2023: 79%); Grocery & Retail Vertical USD 1,886mn (+47% YoY, 25% share, 2023: 21%) (TLB-001, page 26).
- GMV growth in 2024 driven by 25% expansion in monthly active users and an 8% uplift in order frequency (TLB-001, page 26).
- GMV excludes subscription fees (e.g. talabat pro) and rider tips (TLB-001, page 27).

### Orders
- Average order frequency per customer: 6.7x in December 2024 vs 6.2x in December 2023 (calculated as total December orders / December Active Customers) (TLB-001, page 15).
- talabat pro subscribers showed a 28% uplift in order frequency in September 2024 vs. lookalike non-subscribers (TLB-001, page 18).
- talabat rewards: customers who redeemed points for the first time in September 2024 showed a >15% increase in order frequency within 30 days (TLB-001, page 19).
- PostPaid users showed a 14% increase in order frequency (measured Nov 2021–Jan 2024, comparing 3 months post-adoption vs. pre-adoption) (TLB-001, page 18).
- talabat reached "1 million orders per day" as a historical milestone and "100 million lifetime orders" (company timeline) (TLB-001, page 7).
- Average delivery time held at less than 30 minutes across key markets despite increased fleet utilisation in 2024 (TLB-001, page 12, page 15).

### Monthly Active Customers
- Active Customers: >6.5 million as of December 2024 (up from ~2.2 million in January 2020) (TLB-001, page 4, page 15).
- Active Customers defined as individuals who placed at least one successful order in the specified full calendar month (TLB-001, page 136).
- 2024 GMV growth driven in part by a 25% expansion in monthly active users (TLB-001, page 26).
- ~40% of active customers engaged with healthier food choices via filters/search/bookmarks per a 2024 talabat analysis (TLB-001, page 56).

### Talabat Pro
- talabat pro is talabat's subscription loyalty programme, launched March 2022 in the UAE (TLB-001, page 19).
- By end-2024, available in all countries of operation except Iraq; per the CEO's statement, "now available in seven of the eight countries" following its Egypt launch (TLB-001, page 11-12, page 19).
- talabat pro launched in Egypt in February 2025 ("recently launched," "at the time of writing") (TLB-001, page 11, page 19).
- 2.1x increase in talabat pro adoption rate in 2024 vs. prior year (TLB-001, page 12, page 21).
- 24% uplift in talabat pro conversion rates attributed to the newly redesigned talabat app (Q4 2024 redesign) (TLB-001, page 12, page 23).
- talabat pro subscribers showed a 28% uplift in order frequency in September 2024, measured vs. lookalike users matched on frequency, basket size and tenure who did not join pro (TLB-001, page 18).
- talabat pro offers free delivery (subject to minimum basket size) and exclusive discounts for a fixed monthly or annual fee (TLB-001, page 18).
- Programme benefits expanded in 2024 to include exclusive lifestyle benefits, double rewards points (UAE) and DineOut Deals integration (TLB-001, page 11).
- ~99% Partner retention achieved among pro-participating vendors in 2024 (TLB-001, page 21).
- Future focus: grow talabat pro subscription base in GCC and Egypt, leveraging early Egypt traction (TLB-001, page 21).

### Advertising
- AdTech revenue: USD 246mn in 2024, 3.3% of GMV (2023: 3.2% of GMV), +27% YoY (TLB-001, page 13, page 21).
- Global benchmark target for AdTech revenue is ~7% of GMV in the medium term (talabat currently well below this) (TLB-001, page 21).
- AdTech tools include Sponsored Listings, Keyword Ads and Deal Targeting; most popular cost-per-click products yielded an average return of nearly 5x on ad spend (TLB-001, page 13).
- Partner ROAS for CPC ads: 4.9x as of July 2024; September 2024 average CPC ROAS was 6.2x (TLB-001, page 15, page 19).
- AdTech uses "integration of AI-driven targeting and automated ad-buying processes" to lower customer acquisition costs and amplify Partner visibility (TLB-001, page 13).
- Future focus: grow AdTech revenue toward ~7% of GMV benchmark; expand self-service AdTech; enhance algorithmic/real-time ML targeting; new ad formats; CPG-focused campaign reporting tools (TLB-001, page 21).

### Marketplace
- talabat operates a three-sided marketplace (customers, Partners, riders) generating network effects described as the "talabat flywheel" (TLB-001, page 4, page 15).
- Over 68,000 Active Partners (incl. >10,000 Local Shops) and over 58,700 Restaurants as of December 2024 (TLB-001, page 4, page 17).
- Local Shops offer over 700,000 SKUs across >10,000 Partners (TLB-001, page 17).
- Category leader across all 8 countries of operation by total value of orders (relative category share ranges 2x-10x+ vs. next closest peer) (TLB-001, page 5).
- talabat penetration is ~20-25% in foodservice but only ~1-2% in grocery/other retail categories — significant headroom flagged (TLB-001, page 21).

### Q-Commerce
- Groceries and Retail Vertical (incl. dark-store sub-vertical "tMarts") grew GMV 47% YoY in 2024, reaching 25% of total GMV (2023: 21%) (TLB-001, page 11, page 20, page 26).
- tMarts: almost 130 locations across MENA as of end-2024, addressing demand for fast grocery delivery under 30 minutes (TLB-001, page 17).
- tMart GMV as share of total Management Revenue: 28% in 2024 (2023: 26%) (TLB-001, page 27).
- Senior Management includes an "SVP of Q-Commerce" role (organisation chart) (TLB-001, page 78).
- tMart average shrinkage rate: less than 0.4%, "significantly lower compared to traditional retailers" (TLB-001, page 58).
- Senior VP Grocery & Retail (Wassim Makarem) "at the forefront of driving talabat's regional quick commerce initiatives" (TLB-001, page 80).

### Customer Growth
- Active Customers grew from ~2.2 million (Jan 2020) to >6.5 million (Dec 2024) (TLB-001, page 15).
- 2024 GMV growth was driven by a 25% expansion in monthly active users plus an 8% uplift in order frequency (TLB-001, page 26).
- Multi-vertical customers (ordering both food and other categories) order with higher frequency than food-only users, and their numbers increased in 2024 (TLB-001, page 21).
- Customer Acquisition Costs (CAC): USD 44mn in 2024 (2023: USD 40mn) (TLB-001, page 28); combined with talabat-funded vouchering (USD 59mn) into total CARC of USD 103mn or 1.4% of GMV (2023: USD 89mn or 1.5% of GMV) (TLB-001, page 28).

### Retention
- Strategic pillar "Turbo-charging customer loyalty" explicitly targets increasing "order frequency, lifetime value and user stickiness" (TLB-001, page 21).
- Loyalty programmes are described as playing "an important role in enhancing customer retention, increasing order frequency and driving overall profitability" (TLB-001, page 18).
- PostPaid described as "accretive to order frequency and customer retention" (TLB-001, page 9).
- Co-branded credit card with ADCB "strengthened customer retention and engagement" via cashback and free delivery (TLB-001, page 9, page 13).
- ~99% Partner retention among pro-participating vendors in 2024 (TLB-001, page 21).
- Employee retention: 94% of high-performers retained, 13% attrition rate in first nine months of 2024 (TLB-001, page 24-25, page 43).
- Personalisation via app redesign estimated to deliver USD 14mn+ incremental EBITDA p.a., attributed partly to higher retention/order frequency (TLB-001, page 23).

### Subscription
- talabat pro is the primary subscription product (see Talabat Pro section above).
- Subscription fee & Other Income revenue line: USD 952mn in 2024 (+44% YoY) vs USD 663mn in 2023 (TLB-001, page 27).
- Standalone subscription fees (IFRS revenue line): USD 30,111,394 in 2024 vs USD 15,637,302 in 2023 (proforma) (TLB-001, page 93); USD 9,728,074 for the audited period 3 Sept–31 Dec 2024 (TLB-001, page 125).
- Co-branded credit card includes a "tailored version of the talabat pro loyalty subscription programme" providing free delivery (TLB-001, page 13).

### Logistics
- talabat relies substantially on third-party logistics providers and freelance riders (~90% of riders operate via 3PL or freelance model) — flagged as an operational risk (limited control over labour compliance/service quality) (TLB-001, page 34, page 47).
- Rider technology: dispatch algorithms, real-time GPS tracking, optimised payment systems and a rider staffing algorithm (all licensed from Delivery Hero) (TLB-001, page 16, page 23-24).
- Average delivery time: <30 minutes across key markets (TLB-001, page 15).
- Rider Safety Score: 95% in 2024 (telematics-enabled regions) (TLB-001, page 16, page 47).
- Riders: >123,000 Active Riders as of December 2024 (TLB-001, page 4).
- EV fleet: 80 EVs in the UAE as of end-2024; 12 EV pilot tests completed in 2024 (TLB-001, page 60).

### AI
- Market overview cites "Advance in technology improving UX (AI, Machine-learning and Data analytics)" as a growth driver for the region (TLB-001, page 13).
- CEO statement: "AI-driven logistics improvements have enhanced delivery times and optimised resource allocation, ensuring both cost-efficiency and superior service levels" (TLB-001, page 10).
- AdTech uses "integration of AI-driven targeting and automated ad-buying processes" (TLB-001, page 13).
- ML-based personalisation: "It takes approximately six orders for our machine learning models to develop personalised recommendations" (TLB-001, page 22-23).
- Machine-learning models are used to cross-sell verticals and surface loyalty programmes (talabat pro, talabat rewards) at the right moment for each customer (TLB-001, page 22-23).
- Proprietary personalisation and ranking algorithm estimated to generate USD 14mn+ incremental EBITDA p.a. (TLB-001, page 23).
- Employee training curriculum includes "artificial intelligence (AI), data analytics" among key skill areas (TLB-001, page 43).
- Advertising campaign optimisation uses "advanced analytics and machine learning" (TLB-001, page 19).
- Glossary defines AI as "technologies that simulate human intelligence" (TLB-001, page 136).

### Competition
- Risk factor A1 ("Competitive market pressures"): talabat faces competition from offline restaurants/shops offering delivery, restaurants/retailers building their own online ordering capability, technology giants and well-funded entrants, and consolidation creating larger, better-resourced competitors; also cites "Integrated e-commerce companies, quick commerce providers and 'SuperApps'" as intensifying competition (TLB-001, page 32).
- Risk factor A3 ("Antitrust scrutiny"): ongoing investigations in Kuwait, notices in the UAE, and inquiries in Iraq related to talabat's strong market position (TLB-001, page 34).
- Regulatory risk examples: government-imposed commission-rate caps in Qatar; licensing disputes in Oman (TLB-001, page 32).
- talabat is described as "the only multi-country leading platform player in its region" with "the widest geographic reach amongst our peers in the MENA region" (TLB-001, page 5).
- Relative category share vs. next closest peer (food service, H1 2024): Egypt 10x+, Qatar 10x+, Kuwait 8x+, Jordan 4x+, UAE 4x+, Bahrain 3x+, Iraq/Oman 2x+ (TLB-001, page 5).
