### Revenue

- Revenue by stream FY2023: Commission fees AED 3,120.7m; Delivery fees AED 1,741.9m; Advertising & listing fees AED 698.6m; Service fees AED 231.6m; Subscription fees AED 57.4m; Other direct income AED 2,407.6m; less vouchers AED (180.6)m; less other discounts AED (89.5)m (TLB-026, page 42)
- Total management reporting revenue (before deducting vouchers/discounts): AED 6,466.5m FY2022 (34.1% of GMV); AED 8,232.5m FY2023 (37.0% of GMV, +27% growth); AED 5,937.1m (9M2023) vs AED 7,830.0m (9M2024, 39.6% of GMV, +32% growth) (TLB-026, page 42)
- Geographic revenue split (IFRS, audited, FY2023): GCC AED 6,944,733,659; Non-GCC AED 1,043,005,249; Total AED 7,987,738,908 — Egypt is not broken out within Non-GCC (TLB-026, page 264)

- Revenue growth (9M2024 vs 9M2023) was primarily driven by GMV growth across all markets (GCC GMV +18.3%, Non-GCC GMV +42.7%), a 32.6% increase in non-commission revenue, a 29.6% increase in delivery/service fees, and a 42.9% increase in tMart revenue (TLB-026, page 96)
- Revenue growth (FY2023 vs FY2022) was driven by GMV growth (GCC +15.7%, Non-GCC +29.1%), 34.6% growth in non-commission revenue, 27.3% growth in delivery/service fees, and 45.5% growth in tMart revenue (TLB-026, page 98)

### Orders
- Average order frequency per customer: 6.5x (September 2024) vs 6.0x (September 2023); calculated as total September orders divided by September active customers (TLB-026, page 26, page 118)
- Food-only users: 3.8 orders/month at AED 194 (USD 53) monthly spend (September 2024). Multi-vertical users (Food + G&R): 12.8 orders/month at AED 814 (USD 222) monthly spend (September 2024) (TLB-026, page 91, page 122)
- Order success rate ~99% and average delivery time <30 minutes (September 2024) (TLB-026, page 27)
- AdTech-generated orders: 9 million (2021); 18 million (2022); 33 million (2023); CAGR 2021–2023 = 86% (TLB-026, page 30)

### Monthly Active Customers
- More than 6 million active customers (September 2024), up from ~2.2 million (January 2020), CAGR ~25% (TLB-026, page 24, page 91)
- Active customer definition: individuals who placed at least one successful order through the platform within the month of September 2024 (TLB-026, page 20)
- 28% of customers were multi-vertical customers (ordered in both Food and G&R Verticals) for December 2023, a 38% CAGR from December 2019 (TLB-026, page 128)
- G&R Vertical penetration of total active customers: 39% (as of 29 September 2024) (TLB-026, page 129)

### Talabat Pro
- talabat pro launched March 2022 in the UAE; a subscription service offering free delivery (minimum basket) and exclusive deals for a fixed monthly or annual fee (TLB-026, page 133)
- As of the date of this Offering Memorandum (19 Nov 2024), talabat pro is available in all talabat markets **except Egypt and Iraq** (TLB-026, page 133)
- Only 8.3% of customers were subscribed to talabat pro as of 29 September 2024, described as leaving "substantial room for growth" (TLB-026, page 30, page 122)
- talabat pro subscriber CAGR in the UAE was 130% (September 2022–September 2024) (TLB-026, page 133)
- >20% uplift in order frequency among talabat pro subscribers for the month of July 2024 (TLB-026, page 30, page 122, page 133)
- Retention metric definition: percentage of talabat pro customers (joined July 2023) retained on talabat 12 months later, relative to "lookalike" customers with the same frequency, average food value and tenure who did not join talabat pro (TLB-026, page 133)
- Total annual customer savings from talabat pro (Oct 2023–Sept 2024) ≈ AED 135.2 million (EUR ~34 million), funded by Partners and third parties including banks (TLB-026, page 133)
- talabat pro key features: free delivery (min. basket), exclusive deals (food, tMart, Dineout Deals in UAE only), lifestyle benefits via talabat Rewards catalogue, 2x rewards points (UAE only) (TLB-026, page 134)
- Partners contribute a fixed charge per talabat pro order to help fund the free-delivery benefit; the programme is managed to remain profitable (TLB-026, page 134)

### Marketplace
- talabat is the leading on-demand food ordering, delivery, takeaway and groceries/convenience-retail marketplace in MENA, operating in 8 countries with population >185 million and addressable population ~71.1 million (TLB-026, page 24, page 117)
- 56,300+ Restaurants, 9,500+ Local Shops, 155+ cuisines/non-food categories platform-wide; UAE (largest market) has 20,000+ Restaurants and 4,000+ Local Shops (TLB-026, page 27, page 118)
- Restaurant GMV mix (Sept 2024): 17,600+ regional favourite restaurants = 42% of GMV; 34,700 boutique restaurants = 16% of GMV; 4,000 global leading restaurants = 15% of GMV (TLB-026, page 126)

### Customer Growth
- Active customers grew from ~2.2 million (Jan 2020) to 6 million+ (Sept 2024), CAGR ~25% (TLB-026, page 91)
- Active Partners increased ~4.5x since January 2019 (CAGR ~30%), from 15,000 (incl. 300 Local Shops) to 65,000+ (incl. 9,500+ Local Shops) by September 2024 (TLB-026, page 118)
- Active riders increased at a CAGR of ~80% since January 2019, from 4,000+ to 119,000+ by September 2024 (TLB-026, page 118)
- Cohort analysis: GMV per cohort increases in subsequent years; projected FY2024 spend multiples: 3.5x since 2019, 3.1x since 2020, 2.9x since 2021, 2.7x since 2022, 2.0x since 2023 (TLB-026, page 120)
- 80% of customers in Kuwait, UAE, Egypt, Qatar and Bahrain ranked talabat as their #1 preferred platform (as of 29 September 2024) (TLB-026, page 27, page 119)

### Retention
- talabat pro drives measured 12-month customer retention uplift relative to lookalike non-subscribers (cohort joined July 2023) (TLB-026, page 133)
- talabat Rewards "designed to increase customer spend and retention on our platform"; 18% adoption rate and >15% order-frequency uplift among first-time redeemers (as of 29 September 2024) (TLB-026, page 134)
- InstaShop (being acquired) had a 73% month-on-month retention rate and averaged 5 orders/month per customer in 2023 (TLB-026, page 31)
- Automated marketing engine suggests "next best action or reactivation flows for those customers that might have reduced their ordering frequency" (TLB-026, page 137)
- Delivery/fulfilment failures (traffic, address issues, building access, product unavailability) are explicitly flagged as adversely affecting "reputation, customer retention, and overall business performance" (TLB-026, page 61)
- Risk factor: "If we fail to retain our existing customers and Partners or to attract new customers and Partners, our marketing spend may increase and our business as well as our results may be negatively affected" (TLB-026, page 57)
- GEMs ad product is "recommended for Restaurant with low/declining customer acquisition or retention rates" (TLB-026, page 138)

### Subscription
- talabat pro: monthly or annual subscription plan; subscribers can cancel anytime (no refunds, benefits continue until renewal date); annual plan offered at a discount to encourage long-term commitment (TLB-026, page 134)
- Subscription fee revenue: AED 16.2m (2021... FY2022 shown as AED 16,228,161); AED 57,427,993 (FY2023); AED 39,757,998 (9M2023) vs AED 74,857,740 (9M2024) (TLB-026, page 42)
- Subscription revenue recognised on a straight-line basis over the subscription period (accounting policy note) (TLB-026, page 220 area)

### Logistics
- ~95% of riders sourced through third-party logistics providers (TLB-026, page 138)
- Rider average tenure grew from 33 weeks (Jan 2022) to 64 weeks (Sept 2024), a 1.9x increase; UAE rider satisfaction rating ~4.2/5.0 (29 Sept 2024) (TLB-026, page 138)
- Area covered by riders grew 15% and average delivery time improved 17% from 2020 to H1 2024 (TLB-026, page 132)
- In Bahrain, Qatar, Oman, Iraq and Egypt, the top seven third-party logistics providers represent ~50% of the rider fleet; in Jordan, the single largest provider represents ~20% of the rider fleet (TLB-026, page 51)
- Rider safety score ~94% (September 2024); rider training: 1,200+ riders trained in a physical academy (UAE), 78,000+ riders trained on "tcamp" online app across Egypt, UAE, Bahrain, Kuwait and Oman (trailing 12 months) (TLB-026, page 144, page 138)

### AI
- Machine learning models used to introduce/cross-sell verticals and time initiatives like talabat pro and talabat Rewards to individual customers (TLB-026, page 121)
- Rider staffing and order dispatch algorithms (licensed from Delivery Hero) translate demand forecasts into optimised rider shifts and seek to optimise rider pay and delivery experience (TLB-026, page 121, page 131-132)
- Personalised recommendations require at least 6 orders per customer to activate; ~13 terabytes of data generated daily (as of 29 Sept 2024) (TLB-026, page 131)
- Ad placement algorithms and audience segmentation tools underpin CPC ads and banner/promotional ad products; ad-personalisation estimated to have driven ~USD 14 million incremental Adjusted EBITDA since January 2023 (TLB-026, page 132-133)
- Fraud detection system uses machine learning tools (payments risk-factor context) (TLB-026, page 67)

### Competition
- Stated main food delivery/takeaway competitors across talabat's 8 markets: Deliveroo, Careem, noon, Jahez and Snoonu; talabat also competes with phone orders, takeaway, and Partners' own direct online ordering channels (TLB-026, page 146)
- Competition also arises from "Tech Giants," aggressive new entrants, integrated e-commerce/quick-commerce players, and "SuperApps" (TLB-026, page 45)
- Market characterised by consolidation, which could produce larger, better-resourced competitors and pressure commission rates/margins (TLB-026, page 45)
- Competition-law exposure: Kuwait Competition Protection Authority investigations/litigation re: Partner dealings; UAE Competition and Consumer Protection Department notices re: "key partnership clauses" in Partner subscription agreements; Iraq competition authority inquiry (no formal complaint) (TLB-026, page 47)
- Qatar: restaurant-owner complaints led to a Ministry of Commerce and Industry investigation and imposition of a commission-rate price cap plus a ban on delivery registration fees (TLB-026, page 46)
- Relative category share: talabat's Foodservice sales lead the next-largest online peer by >10x to >2x depending on market (Jordan >4x, Egypt >10x, Iraq >2x in Non-GCC); G&R category leadership by >9x to >2x (Jordan >9x, Egypt >2x, Iraq nascent) (TLB-026, page 114)
- talabat is described as "the only multi-country leading platform player" in both Foodservice and Grocery & Retail delivery across its 8-country region; the next-largest player differs by country (TLB-026, page 114)
