# TLB-015 raw facts & entities

## Facts by topic

### Revenue
- Total management reporting revenue: $1,760M (2022) → $2,241M (2023); H1 2023 $1,038M → H1 2024 $1,360M (TLB-015, page 112).
- Revenue as % of GMV (GMV-to-revenue conversion): 37.0% (2022) → 34.1%/(alt. labelled) 2023, and 39.4% H1 2024 vs 37.1% H1 2023 (TLB-015, page 112).
- Revenue mix H1 2024 includes Advertising and listing fees, Delivery and Service fees, Subscription fees and Other Income, and Commission fees; Advertising business and Delivery & Service fee revenues grew 35% and 29% YoY respectively in H1 2024; tMart business represented 27% of revenue in H1 2024 (TLB-015, page 112).
- GCC countries contributed 85% of total revenue in H1 2024; Non-GCC 15% (TLB-015, page 112).
- In 2023, Egypt represented ~6% of total revenue and only ~1% of total Adj. EBITDA (TLB-015, page 116).
- 2024E revenue growth guidance: 28-30% YoY; 2025E: 18-20% YoY; medium-term: 15-17% (TLB-015, page 120).

### GMV
- Group GMV: $3,946M (2021) → $5,172M (2022) → $6,062M (2023); H1 2023 $2,865M (restated) → H1 2024 $3,455M (TLB-015, page 8, 22).
- GMV CAGR 2015-2023: ~51% (TLB-015, page 8).
- GMV CAGR 2021-2023: 24% reported growth (TLB-015, page 111).
- H1 2024 GMV split: GCC $2,978M (86%) vs Non-GCC $477M (14%); Non-GCC grew ~50% YoY vs GCC ~20% YoY (TLB-015, page 111).
- Grocery & Retail segment contributed ~25% of total GMV as of H1 2024 (TLB-015, page 111).
- 2023 GMV by category: Food/Foodservice $4.8Bn; Groceries $1.2Bn; Other adjacent verticals $0.1Bn (TLB-015, page 24, 102).
- 2024E GMV growth guidance: 22-23% YoY; 2025E: 17-18% YoY; medium-term: 14-15% (TLB-015, page 120).

### Orders
- Average order frequency: 6.5x per active customer (July 2024) (TLB-015, page 13, 48, 82).
- Multi-vertical (Food + Grocery & Retail) customers order 13.0 times/month; Food-only customers order 3.8 times/month (3.4x multiple) (TLB-015, page 18, 61).
- Orders generated through AdTech products: 9M (2021) → 18M (2022) → 33M (2023), 86% CAGR 2021-23 (TLB-015, page 50).
- talabat monthly order frequency for top 5% customers grew at 14% CAGR (Jan-19 to Jul-24) (TLB-015, page 100).
- Monthly orders per capita: GCC 1.28x, Non-GCC 0.13x, Group average 0.42x (2023 basis) (TLB-015, page 12, 24, 32, 101).

### Monthly Active Customers
- Active customers: ~2.2M (Jan-2020) → >6.0M (Jul-2024); CAGR ~25% (TLB-015, page 37).
- Monthly revenue per active customer trend shown 2020-2024 (exact $ figures not legible in extracted text) (TLB-015, page 37).
- Share of customers using multiple verticals: ~31% (Jul-2024) (TLB-015, page 37).
- New customers acquired: 27% YoY growth (2019-23 CAGR 19%), with a (8%) COVID-impacted dip noted; 11% and 56% growth figures also shown across the multi-year trend (TLB-015, page 100).

### Talabat Pro
- talabat pro penetration: 7.6% of user base (Jul-2024) vs ~48% global leading peer benchmark (US player, Dec-2023) (TLB-015, page 25, 104).
- Live in 6 of 8 countries — all except Egypt and Iraq (TLB-015, page 77).
- Retention uplift: 32% (12-month retention of talabat pro joiners vs lookalike non-members, cohort joined July 2023) (TLB-015, page 78, 104).
- Frequency uplift: 22% (July 2024 measurement) (TLB-015, page 25, 78, 104).
- Gross profit per customer uplift: 136% (30 days post- vs pre-subscription, July 2024 joiners) (TLB-015, page 78, 104).
- Partner-funded incentives for talabat pro users grew from $1.2M (Jan-23) to $4.2M (Jul-24) (TLB-015, page 104).
- talabat pro user CAGR (Jul-22 to Jul-24, UAE/first country of entry): 129% (TLB-015, page 25).
- talabat pro provides unlimited free delivery on select Food partners, tMart, and select Local shops (above a minimum order value), members-only discounts, tMart exclusive deals, 2x rewards points (UAE only), and Exclusive DineOut Deals (UAE only) (TLB-015, page 77).
- Total LTM partner-funded savings via talabat pro: $32M (TLB-015, page 77).
- Roadmap: "Launching in remaining categories" and "Deepening value proposition" listed as talabat pro's forward priorities (TLB-015, page 89).

### Advertising
- Annual AdTech revenue: $84M (2021) → $144M (2022) → $194M (2023), 52% CAGR 2021-23 (TLB-015, page 26, 50, 105).
- AdTech revenue as % of GMV: 2.1% (2021) → 2.8% (2022) → 3.2% (2023); global leading peer benchmark ~7% penetration (TLB-015, page 26, 86, 105).
- Partner ROAS (CPC only): 4.9x average, as of July 2024 (TLB-015, page 13, 47, 48, 86).
- AdTech product-level ROAS: Premium positioning 4.9x (27% adoption); Display ads 4.0x (1.1% adoption); Keyword ads 4.8x (2.2% adoption); Deal targeting 13.8x (22% adoption); GEMs 3.6x (25% adoption) (TLB-015, page 49).
- Number of AdTech products per partner: 1.17 (2021) → 1.20 (2022) → 1.29 (2023) → 1.40 (H1 2024), +20% (TLB-015, page 49).
- CPG advertising investment ratio for talabat: 8% (leading vs. typical CPG investment of 2%), a 4x differential (TLB-015, page 108).
- Global retail media ad spending CAGR 2019-25: 22% (TLB-015, page 107).

### Marketplace
- talabat operates a three-sided marketplace (customers, Partners, riders) driven by network effects — the "talabat flywheel" (TLB-015, page 13).
- Active Partners: >64k (Jul-2024), of which 9.3k Local shops, up from ~15k (Jan-2019, incl. 300 Local shops), +4.4x (TLB-015, page 7, 16, 46).
- Restaurant tiering by GMV contribution (Jul-2024): 4k+ global leading restaurants (15% of GMV), 17k+ regional favourites (44% of GMV), 34k+ boutique restaurants (17% of GMV), 9k+ Grocery & Retail branches (24% of GMV) (TLB-015, page 40, 46).
- #1 category position across all 8 countries; total addressable population 71.1M+ (TLB-015, page 12, 32).

### Customer Growth
- Active customers grew from ~2.2M (Jan-2020) to >6.0M (Jul-2024), ~25% CAGR (TLB-015, page 37).
- GMV per average customer grows ~4x within 5 years of acquisition (2019 cohort basis) (TLB-015, page 8, 15, 38).
- Cumulative orders per cohort show sustained increases through month 60 across 2019-2023 cohorts (TLB-015, page 15, 38).

### Retention
- talabat pro customer retention uplift: 32% (12 months post-joining vs lookalike non-members) (TLB-015, page 78, 104).
- talabat Rewards: "Encouraging consistent behaviour, increasing loyalty and reducing churn" (TLB-015, page 79).
- Customer contact rate (inverse proxy for service issues) fell from rebased 100 (2020) to 57 (H1 2024) (TLB-015, page 42).
- NPS ~20% higher than regional peers; >60% of users intend to keep using talabat; 2/3 would not switch despite a price increase (TLB-015, page 44).
- InstaShop customers order 5x/month with a 73% MoM retention rate (TLB-015, page 72).
- "Expanding loyalty programme in other countries beyond Jordan" listed as a non-GCC growth lever — implying Egypt/Iraq loyalty-programme expansion is a retention priority (TLB-015, page 34).

### Subscription
- talabat pro: monthly/annual subscription fee, unlimited free delivery, exclusive deals; live in 6 of 8 countries (excl. Egypt, Iraq) (TLB-015, page 77).
- Co-branded ADCB credit card (UAE only): no annual fee, unlimited free delivery, welcome bonus of AED 500 (min. spend AED 2,500/45 days), 35% back on talabat orders (first 10 transactions, then 1.25%), up to 1.25% back on other purchases (TLB-015, page 75).

### Logistics
- Active riders: ~13k (Dec-2019) → ~62k (Dec-2021) → ~87k (Dec-2023) → ~115k (Jul-2024), 64% CAGR 2019-24 (TLB-015, page 17, 55).
- Rider satisfaction rating: 4.2/5; Rider safety score: 94% (TLB-015, page 17, 55, 58, 85).
- ~90% of riders sourced via third-party partners; ~60% expats (TLB-015, page 17, 55).
- Average delivery time improved from ~37 min (2020) to ~31 min (H1 2024); % of orders on time improved from ~67% to ~78% over the same period (TLB-015, page 42).
- System reliability ~99.95%; delivery under 30 minutes for hot food (TLB-015, page 82).
- Riders earn 1.2x the daily income of other riders on comparable platforms, per rider survey (UAE, Kuwait, Qatar, Egypt) (TLB-015, page 56-57).

### AI
- Predictive AI Recommendations generate $14M+ p.a. incremental EBITDA (estimated, 2023-Jul 2024); ~$6M/year savings from efficient fraud detection (TLB-015, page 21).
- 13 Terabytes of data generated daily; 2 R&D centers (as of July 2024) (TLB-015, page 21, 87).
- "talabat's machine learning models allow it to introduce and cross-sell verticals, as well as initiatives such as talabat pro and talabat Rewards, at the right time for customers to benefit from" (TLB-015, page 84, echoing TLB-023).
- Personalisation requires 6 orders to activate ("6 orders needed to create personalisation") (TLB-015, page 84).
- Forward roadmap: "AI driven recommendations in new form factors," "Optimising timing, placement, and incentives of vertical and product cross-sells" (TLB-015, page 89).
- Tech stack able to support 2x current order level; releases every day, app store updates weekly (TLB-015, page 88).
- ~440 tech employees (FTEs) as of the deck (TLB-015, page 7, 90).

### Competition
- talabat's relative category share ranges from >3x to >10x the next-largest online peer in GCC countries, and >2x to >10x in non-GCC countries, per OC&C category analysis (TLB-015, page 12, 32).
- talabat NPS ~20% higher than "regional peers' average" (unnamed) (TLB-015, page 44).
- China comparator: Meituan cited with 72% category share (2022) and 90.0x monthly orders per capita vs talabat group average 0.4x, in a category-penetration benchmarking slide (TLB-015, page 100).
