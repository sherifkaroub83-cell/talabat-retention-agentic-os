# Subscription — Consolidated Facts

Subscription revenue lines and mechanics (primarily talabat pro).

Consolidated from 25 of 29 corpus documents (Phase 4 consolidation of `vault/Knowledge/Facts/_raw/`). Every bullet retains its original per-fact citation `(DocID, page N)`. Grouped by source document; no facts invented or added beyond what appears in `vault/Knowledge/Facts/_raw/<DocID>_facts.md`.

## TLB-001 — Annual Report 2024

- talabat pro is the primary subscription product (see Talabat Pro section above).
- Subscription fee & Other Income revenue line: USD 952mn in 2024 (+44% YoY) vs USD 663mn in 2023 (TLB-001, page 27).
- Standalone subscription fees (IFRS revenue line): USD 30,111,394 in 2024 vs USD 15,637,302 in 2023 (proforma) (TLB-001, page 93); USD 9,728,074 for the audited period 3 Sept–31 Dec 2024 (TLB-001, page 125).
- Co-branded credit card includes a "tailored version of the talabat pro loyalty subscription programme" providing free delivery (TLB-001, page 13).

## TLB-002 — Annual Report 2025

- (See Talabat Pro section above — subscription is talabat's terminology for talabat pro and related
  FinTech products.)
- Embedded FinTech: digital wallets for rapid refunds/corporate allowances; talabat Postpaid for
  deferred bill settlement; co-branded credit cards for tailored rewards — "lower transaction costs,
  boost conversion metrics, and cultivate a highly retentive customer base." (TLB-002, page 13)
- Egypt FinTech expansion: talabat PostPaid launched in Egypt; co-branded credit card launched with
  Commercial International Bank (CIB), Egypt's largest private-sector bank — first FinTech expansion
  outside the UAE. (TLB-002, pages 14, 19)
- Postpaid credit risk (audited): gross carrying amount USD 36,310,409; loss allowance
  USD 11,999,801; postpaid allows customers to purchase and pay within 30 days. (TLB-002, page 112)

## TLB-003 — Q4/FY2024 Preliminary Financials

- "Subscription fee & Other Income" grew 44% y/y to USD 952M for FY-2024 (fastest-growing management revenue line disclosed), USD 276M for Q4-2024 (+42% y/y) (TLB-003, page 3).

## TLB-004 — Q4/FY2024 Audited Financials

- Subscription fees revenue: USD 9,728,074 for the period since inception through 31 December 2024 (TLB-004, page 49).
- "For subscription programs offered to orderers and restaurants, the Group acts as principal. Revenue from subscription fees is recognised on a straight-line basis over the period of the subscription." (TLB-004, page 24).

## TLB-008 — Q4/FY2025 Audited Financials

- Subscription fees revenue: USD 83,790,496 for the period since inception through 31 December 2025, up from USD 9,728,074 disclosed in TLB-004 (FY2024, 120-day period) — periods are of different length (~16 months vs. 120 days) so not a like-for-like growth rate (TLB-008, page 52).
- "For subscription programs offered to orderers and restaurants, the Group acts as principal. Revenue from subscription fees is recognised on a straight-line basis over the period of the subscription." (policy reiterated from TLB-004) (TLB-008, Note 3.3).

## TLB-009 — Q4/FY2025 Results Press Release
