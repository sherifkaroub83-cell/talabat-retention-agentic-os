## Finding
Two official talabat press releases, both already collected in `Input_Data/04_Strategy_News/`, contain
Egypt-specific operational data not found anywhere in the 29 primary `TLB-XXX` vault documents:
1. **talabat Egypt inaugurates the largest quick-commerce distribution center in MENA** (28 Apr 2026):
   ~27,000 sqm facility, up to 1 million items/day, serves 100% of talabat mart's Egypt network across 12
   cities (expansion to 17 underway), in-house AI models forecast demand/optimize stock/automate
   replenishment, ~75,000 pallet positions. Egypt employs 3,100 people for talabat, including a 1,500-
   person Global Tech and Shared Services hub delivering 74% of the company's (Group-wide) shared
   services and developing 30% of its app features.
2. **talabat Egypt launches "t pro"** (18 Feb 2025): EGP 79/month or EGP 799/year, two-week free trial;
   free delivery thresholds (EGP 150 mart / 180 supermarkets / 200 hypermarkets); up to 30% off 400+
   mart products; 10,000+ Egypt Partners at launch; 50+ named strategic partnerships.
