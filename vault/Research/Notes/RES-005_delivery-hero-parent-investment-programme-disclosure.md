# RES-005 — Peer/parent investment-programme disclosure benchmark (Delivery Hero SE, FY2025 results & 2026 investment plans)

## Finding
Delivery Hero SE — talabat's parent and majority shareholder — discloses its group growth-investment
programme the same way talabat does: **by initiative type and broad geography with a guidance-level
margin/FCF impact, not by project-level allocation, approval threshold, or stage-gate mechanism.**
Specifics from the FY2025/Q4 2025 disclosures (published 27 February 2026):

- **FY2025 results:** adjusted EBITDA exceeded EUR 900 million (+30% y/y) "despite elevated growth
  investments in MENA and Asia as well as FX headwinds"; free cash flow exceeded EUR 200 million
  (reported as ~EUR 250 million by Investing.com) — both above guidance.
- **2026 guidance:** GMV growth 8–10%; adjusted EBITDA EUR 910–960 million (i.e. only ~1–6% growth,
  "up to a mid-single-digit %"), and free cash flow "slightly above EUR 200 million" — **below the
  2025 actual — explicitly because of stepped-up investment** in customer-facing initiatives.
- **Named 2026 investment directions ("lean in"):** "enhanced subscription, personalization, own
  delivery, Dmarts and assortment growth"; continued investment in key Asian and MENA markets "to
  ensure it continues to hold leading positions and retain higher-value customers"; additional
  Dmarts investments "especially in the Middle East" are stated to drive **higher CapEx and an
  increased lease footprint**; integrated verticals are guided to a "small positive" adjusted EBITDA
  in 2026 despite the Dmart expansion.
- **No group-level total investment-programme figure** (analogous to talabat's disclosed USD 175mn)
  is named in these releases; the allocation is disclosed only directionally.

**Benchmark insight for the capstone:** the parent's 2026 priorities (subscription, personalization,
own delivery, Dmart/quick-commerce density, MENA) map almost one-to-one onto talabat's Everyday App
components (tMart scaling, talabat pro, new verticals). This is external corroboration that
talabat's ~USD 120mn / ~USD 55mn split is consistent with parent-level group strategy — and that the
**governance-mechanics gap documented in [[Capital Allocation and Investment Governance]] is an
industry disclosure norm, not a talabat-specific omission**: even the parent discloses investment
outputs and margin bridges, not evaluation criteria or approval stages. The business plan's proposed
stage-gate framework therefore cannot be benchmarked against any disclosed peer mechanism either.

## Limitations
- No absolute EUR investment-programme total or per-market breakdown is disclosed — this source
  benchmarks *disclosure practice and strategic direction*, not allocation quantum.
- The Q4 2025 MENA GMV figure is contested between two secondary reports (see above) and must not be
  used as evidence until reconciled.
- Delivery Hero group figures span ~70 countries; nothing here is specific to talabat's eight-market
  set beyond the explicitly MENA-directed statements quoted.
- Parent-subsidiary alignment is corroborative, not causal: DH's stated priorities do not prove
  talabat's USD 120/55 split was derived from them, and DH is not a neutral observer of talabat
  (majority owner; officer overlap — see [[Corporate Structure]]).
- All content retrieved 2026-07-24 via WebSearch summaries; full-page verification pending
  (egress-policy block documented above).
