# RES-007 — Advertising (AdTech) monetization benchmarks for delivery platforms

## Finding
Three platform-disclosed reference points for advertising monetization on delivery platforms, all
retrieved 2026-07-24:

- **Delivery Hero SE (talabat's parent, group level):** AdTech revenue reached **~3.0% of group GMV
  in 2025 (3.2% in Q4 2025)**, coming "very close" to its stated ambition of >EUR 1.5 billion of
  2025 AdTech revenue (one report cites EUR 1.48 billion, a ~50% CAGR since 2021), held back by a
  slower rollout in South Korea. Stated **long-term target: >4% of GMV**. Return on ad spend for
  advertisers reportedly improved from 3.9x to 6x between 2022 and 2025. (Q4 2025 disclosures,
  27 Feb 2026.)
- **Uber (global, all segments):** advertising surpassed a **USD 2 billion annualized run-rate in
  FY2025** — reported as **>2% of gross bookings**, exceeding what management had earlier framed as
  a ~2% penetration ceiling; the run-rate was USD 1.5 billion in May 2025, +60% y/y.
- **DoorDash (global):** annualized advertising revenue run-rate **above USD 1 billion** (reported
  in 2025/2026 coverage; exact reference date not verifiable this session).

**Benchmark synthesis (reference band, not a forecast):** mature delivery platforms currently
monetize advertising at roughly **2–3% of GMV/gross bookings, with the most aggressive disclosed
target at >4%**. The most decision-relevant figure for the capstone is the parent's: talabat's own
AdTech revenue (disclosed in the corpus at Group level) can be honestly benchmarked against
Delivery Hero's 3.0% actual / >4% target trajectory, since talabat's AdTech sits inside that same
group programme.

## Source detail
Delivery Hero figures originate in its official Q4 2025 trading update / earnings call (27 Feb
2026), captured via Yahoo Finance earnings-call coverage and Delivery Hero newsroom search results.
Uber figures originate in Uber's FY2025 earnings disclosures, captured via Dealroom.co news
coverage (with Statista tracking the run-rate series). DoorDash's >USD 1bn run-rate is
management-disclosed, captured via industry press (Osmos/Modern Delivery coverage of 2025-26
earnings). All are company self-disclosures reported secondhand — none is an independent market
study.

## Limitations
- **Geography:** every figure is global platform-level — Delivery Hero group (~70 countries), Uber
  (including mobility, so "% of gross bookings" is not delivery-only), DoorDash (US-centric). None
  is specific to talabat's eight-market set, the GCC, or any talabat country. Usable only as
  reference points, never as talabat-market expectations.
- Denominators differ (DH GMV vs Uber total gross bookings vs DoorDash marketplace volume) — the
  2–3% band is indicative, not a like-for-like ratio.
- No figure here says anything about the *cost* of building an ads business (tech investment, sales
  force), which is what the USD 175mn allocation decision actually needs; these benchmark the
  revenue opportunity only.
- The EUR 1.48 billion DH AdTech figure appears in one search result; the corroborated form is the
  softer "very close to >EUR 1.5bn ambition" — prefer the latter phrasing until page-level
  verification.
