# RES-003 — Egypt total foodservice market size (Mordor Intelligence)

## Tooling note (read first)
Produced directly by `bp-orchestrator` in the research-agent role (no `Agent`/`WebSearch`/`WebFetch`
tool available this session — see RES-001). This note evaluates the already-collected secondary file
`Input_Data/02_Market_Research/2026_Mordor_egypt_foodservice_market.md`, retrieved 20 July 2026 from
Mordor's public report-overview page (paid report not accessed).

## Finding
Mordor Intelligence sizes Egypt's **total foodservice market** (dine-in + delivery + takeaway, i.e. a much
broader category than IMARC's "online food delivery" definition) at **USD 10.35bn (2025 base) / USD
11.83bn (2026)**, forecast to **USD 23.1bn by 2031** (CAGR 2026–2031: **14.32%**). Within this, **delivery
is the fastest-growing service type at 14.52% CAGR**, explicitly attributed in part to "Talabat, Akelni,
Mrsool platform expansion." Key restraint: food inflation/EGP depreciation (F&B prices +71.9% y/y cited),
worth an estimated −3.8pp on the category CAGR. Market is fragmented (concentration score 3/10); named
foodservice leaders are Americana Restaurants, Alamar Foods, Mansour Group, Mo'men Group, Olayan Group —
none of which are talabat's direct delivery-platform competitors (they are restaurant/foodservice
operators, not delivery platforms).

## Source detail
Mordor Intelligence is a named market-research firm, on the `external-research` skill's acceptable-
provider list. Figures are as published on Mordor's free report-overview page; full paid report and
underlying methodology were not accessed.

## Confidence rationale
**Medium.** Acceptable named provider, single source (no independent corroboration of the specific
dollar figures), free-page summary only (no visible primary-data methodology). Directionally consistent
with talabat's own disclosed non-GCC GMV growth story (Egypt within a fast-growing regional bloc), which
supports the qualitative macro framing (inflation as a defining constraint, delivery as fastest-growing
segment) more than it supports citing the specific dollar totals as precise.

## Limitations
- This is a **much broader denominator than IMARC's** (total foodservice, USD 10.35–11.83bn, vs. IMARC's
  online-food-delivery-only USD 542.9m) — the two are not measuring the same thing and cannot be added or
  averaged; using both requires explicit definitional labeling (see DEC-002).
- Contains at least one confirmed factual error flagged by the project's own secondary competitor corpus:
  Mordor's report cites talabat's InstaShop acquisition at "USD 360m" — this is actually Delivery Hero's
  2020 purchase price of InstaShop, not talabat's 2025 acquisition price (USD 32m, per
  `Input_Data/03_Competitors/2026-07-20_egypt_delivery_competitive_landscape.md` and
  `CLAUDE.md`'s own standing verification flag). This error does not appear to affect the headline
  foodservice market-size or CAGR figures directly, but it is a material reason to treat this report's
  talabat-specific claims with added caution even while its macro/category framing may still be useful.
- No live web verification performed this session (see Tooling note).
