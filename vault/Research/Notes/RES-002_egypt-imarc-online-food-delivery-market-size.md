## Finding
IMARC Group sizes Egypt's online food delivery market at **USD 542.9 million in 2025**, forecast to reach
**USD 827.2 million by 2034** (CAGR 2026–2034: **4.55%**). Report ID SR112026A23818, historical period
2020–2025. Named drivers: internet penetration (75.66m users, Jan 2022, per Egypt's IT industry
association) and Egypt's large student population (24m K-12 students, 2022).

## Source detail
IMARC Group is a named market-research firm, explicitly on the `external-research` skill's acceptable-
provider list. The figure comes from IMARC's free public report-overview page (full paid report not
accessed — figures are as published on that page only, no underlying methodology document reviewed).

## Confidence rationale
**Medium.** IMARC is an acceptable named provider, but this is a single source with no independent
corroboration of the specific USD 542.9m figure, and the report's own free-page summary gives no visible
methodology (survey-based vs. modeled, primary data collection vs. desk research). The figure is also, on
its face, difficult to reconcile with talabat's own disclosed Egypt segment revenue (USD 509.9m for
FY2025, per TLB-002/TLB-008 — see Limitations), which further caps confidence at Medium rather than High.

## Limitations
- **This figure does not reconcile with talabat's own disclosed Egypt revenue.** talabat's Egypt segment
  revenue alone (a fraction of GMV, since revenue is commission/fee take, not full order value) was
  USD 509.9m in FY2025 (TLB-002, TLB-008) — almost equal to IMARC's entire estimate of the *whole market's*
  online food delivery size (USD 542.9m). Revenue cannot exceed (or nearly equal) total market GMV for one
  player among several active competitors (Breadfast, Rabbit, elmenus per RES-001). This is a strong
  internal-consistency red flag, not a citation-ready reconciled figure.
- IMARC's own source file (`Input_Data/02_Market_Research/2026_IMARC_egypt_online_food_delivery_market.md`)
  flags this itself: "IMARC's USD ~543m (2025) figure and low 4.55% CAGR appear conservative vs talabat's
  own reported non-GCC growth (+57% GMV y/y in FY2025). Definitional differences likely (food-only vs
  multi-vertical GMV, USD conversion after EGP devaluation)."
- No live web verification performed this session (see Tooling note). Full paid report methodology was
  not reviewed.
- This is a secondary source; per `Input_Data/_CORPUS_INDEX.md`'s own data-gap flag #2, market-size
  figures across providers conflict by definition and a definition must be picked and reconciled
  explicitly, not blended silently.
