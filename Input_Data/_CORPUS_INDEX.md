## Known data gaps / verification flags

1. **Egypt-only financials are not disclosed** — talabat reports Egypt inside "non-GCC" (with Jordan, Iraq). Egypt-specific sizing must be estimated; show the logic.
2. **Market-size figures conflict by definition** (IMARC USD 543m online food delivery vs Mordor USD 10–12bn total foodservice). Pick one definition and reconcile.
3. **InstaShop price:** use USD 32m (2025, talabat) not USD 360m (2020, Delivery Hero).
4. Weekly-active-user and market-share numbers for Egypt come from low-authority sources — treat as directional until corroborated (e.g., via the Annual Report or app-analytics data).
