## 1. Rabbit (q-commerce, Egyptian startup)

- 20-minute grocery delivery; raised **USD 11m pre-seed** (region's largest at the time) — [KrASIA](https://kr-asia.com/egyptian-20-minute-grocery-delivery-startup-rabbit-raises-usd-11-million-in-regions-largest-pre-seed); total funding ~$11m over 2 rounds per [Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/rabbit/__o4C_4aoY_4qBErXOABxVpYxomgFYOA_za_22-4_jFms)
- **April 2025: secured new funding (undisclosed) to enter Saudi Arabia**, regional HQ in Riyadh; target of 20 million items delivered across all major Saudi cities by 2026 — [Disrupt Africa](https://disruptafrica.com/2025/04/15/egyptian-q-commerce-startup-rabbit-secures-funding-to-enter-saudi-market/), [Tech In Africa](https://www.techinafrica.com/egyptian-q-commerce-startup-rabbit-secures-funding-for-saudi-arabian-expansion/)
- Publishes its own "State of Quick Commerce in Egypt and MENA (2026)" — [Rabbit blog](https://www.rabbitmart.com/blog/business-en/state-of-quick-commerce-egypt-mena-2026/) (useful competitor-perspective source)
- Aug 2025: Rabbit and Talabat both expanded cloud kitchen partnerships, adding 200+ virtual restaurant brands across Greater Cairo — [IMARC/vocal coverage](https://vocal.media/futurism/egypt-online-food-delivery-market-trends-growth-drivers-and-future-prospects)

## 2. Breadfast (q-commerce / e-grocery, Egyptian startup)

- **USD 50m pre-Series C** (backers: Mubadala, Olayan Financing, Y Combinator, IFC, SBI Investment); larger Series C planned H1 2026, IPO ambitions — [fwdstart](https://www.fwdstart.me/p/egypt-breadfast-raises-50m-pre-series-c-backed-by-mubadala-saudi-s-olayan-family-y-combinator-and), [dabafinance](https://dabafinance.com/en/news/breadfast-egypt-pre-series-c-expansion-ipo)
- Valued ~**USD 382m** (mid-2025); **39 fulfillment centers, ~1 million orders/month, 300,000+ active users across four Egyptian cities** — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/breadfast/)
- Own-brand bakery/grocery + dark stores; strongest local pure-play in Egyptian q-commerce

## 3. elmenus (food discovery & delivery, Egyptian startup, founded 2011)

- Positioned as the #2 food delivery player in Egypt after talabat; differentiates via lower commission rates attractive to SME restaurants — [Slant POS comparison](https://blog.slantco.com/elmenus-vs-talabat-which-is-better-for-your-restaurant-in-egypt/), [In Practise analysis](https://inpractise.com/articles/elmenus-and-egyptian-food-delivery)
- Talabat cited with **945,000+ weekly active users** in Egypt (per [Menuviel 2025 app roundup](https://blog.menuviel.com/popular-food-delivery-apps-in-egypt/)) — treat as approximate

## 4. Other players

- **Akelni, Mrsool** — named by Mordor Intelligence among expanding delivery platforms in Egypt — [Mordor](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/egypt-foodservice-market)
- **Roboost** — Egyptian last-mile automation startup; partnered with McDonald's Egypt (Oct 2023) to automate delivery cycle — [IMARC](https://www.imarcgroup.com/egypt-online-food-delivery-market)
- Careem Food exited Egypt (2022); Uber Eats exited earlier — market consolidation around talabat + local players (verify independently)

## 5. talabat's own q-commerce consolidation — InstaShop facts (correction)

- talabat **completed acquisition of InstaShop from Delivery Hero for USD 32 million** (internal restructuring within the Delivery Hero group), consolidated from 25 Feb 2025; InstaShop continues as an independent brand within talabat's Grocery & Retail vertical — [MenaBytes](https://www.menabytes.com/talabat-instashop/), [Forbes ME](https://www.forbesmiddleeast.com/consumer/retail/talabat-acquires-instashop-for-$32m-to-expand-grocery-delivery-in-mena), [Wamda](https://www.wamda.com/2025/03/talabat-fully-acquires-instashop-32-million), [official PR](https://corporate.talabat.com/news/corporate-website-news-item-2/)
- **⚠️ The widely-cited "USD 360m" figure is Delivery Hero's 2020 purchase price of InstaShop, not talabat's 2025 acquisition price.** Some market reports (e.g., Mordor) conflate the two.
- talabat pro-forma G&R GMV 2024 exceeded USD 2.5bn — [Caterer ME](https://www.caterermiddleeast.com/business/everything-you-need-to-know-about-talabats-us32m-instashop-acquisition)

## Strategic read (hypothesis input, not a sourced fact)

Egypt is talabat's fastest-growing region (non-GCC +52–57% GMV y/y through FY2025–Q1 2026) but also its most contested: two well-funded local q-commerce players (Breadfast, Rabbit) compete on speed and price in Greater Cairo, while elmenus undercuts on restaurant commissions. talabat's response is infrastructure scale (MENA's largest distribution center, Apr 2026) + subscription lock-in (t pro, EGP 79/month) + AI-driven demand forecasting. A capstone problem statement could target any seam in this: basket economics under devaluation, restaurant-partner churn to lower-commission rivals, or dark-store network optimization.
