## Headline

**talabat delivers strong Q1 2026 performance, advances strategic investments and increases guidance**

- GMV grew 18% y/y (cFX) driven by an expanded customer base
- Adjusted EBITDA at 4.8% and net income at 3.2% of GMV
- Investment programme focused on building the **"Everyday App"** substantially on track
- FY guidance increased by USD 20m for net income to USD 300–330m; reaffirmed for remaining metrics (11–14% GMV growth, 14–17% revenue growth cFX, Adjusted EBITDA USD 510–540m, FCF USD 370–400m)
- Share buyback programme (up to 5% of issued share capital over two years) to commence soon after results; purchases disclosed daily

## Q1 2026 highlights

- **GMV USD 2.7bn, +19% y/y (+18% cFX)** — robust order volume growth, strong customer acquisition. Contributors: improved Ramadan operations, favourable Eid seasonality (Eid Al-Fitr fell in Q1 this year vs Q2 in comparison period), and positioning as a reliable multi-vertical provider amid the ongoing regional conflict. Increased "eat-at-home" consumption as employers adopted flexible work-from-home and schools shifted to distance learning across most markets.
  - GCC GMV USD 2.1bn, +12%, 79% of total (prior year: 84%)
  - **Non-GCC (Egypt, Jordan, Iraq) GMV USD 563m, +52%, 21% of total (prior year: 16%)**
- **Revenue USD 1.0bn, +23%**; GMV-to-revenue conversion 39% (prior year 38%) — higher talabat mart share more than offset lower commission rates and increased incentives to retain medium/high-value customers
- **Adjusted EBITDA USD 130m, −9% y/y, = 4.8% of GMV** (prior year 6.3%) — lower gross profit margins from GMV mix shift and deliberate margin investments for the "Everyday App"
- **Net income USD 87m, −18% y/y, = 3.2% of GMV** (prior year 4.7%)
- Free Cash Flow USD 104m, +7%, = 3.9% of GMV; Cash Conversion Ratio 81% (prior year 68%)

## "Everyday App" investment programme (USD 120m for 2026)

Three areas (revised from the USD 100m+ announced in Feb 2026):

1. **talabat mart**: store density (speed-led value proposition, future capacity), supply chain infrastructure (assortment, availability), affordability (customer adoption)
2. **talabat pro**: multi-vertical engagement engine — free delivery, food & grocery discounts, on-time guarantee, priority support, dine-out discounts, content-streaming and ride-hailing partner services
3. **New ventures**: new offerings in the retail space and additional everyday services

Q1 deployment: close to USD 25m in operating, capital and lease expenses. Planned margin investments in marketing and pricing were tempered by stronger demand and a **less aggressive competitive environment**.

## CEO commentary (Toon Gyssels)

Strong start to the year, ahead of expectations. Teams operated in "a dynamic environment of heightened uncertainty" prioritising service continuity and safety of people, riders, partners. Ambition: "the app that consumers rely on every day."
