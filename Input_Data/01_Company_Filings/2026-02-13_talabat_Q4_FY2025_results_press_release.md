## Headline

**talabat reports resilient results for Q4 2025, announces disciplined investment cycle to drive long-term growth**

- GMV grew 21% y/y driven by sustained order volume growth across all markets
- Robust margins with Adjusted EBITDA at 6.3% and net income at 5.0% (of GMV)
- Full year 2025 performance in line with guidance across GMV, Revenue and Adjusted EBITDA; net income and Adjusted FCF near guidance
- Board recommends USD 421 million in total dividends for 2025, exceeding guidance
- New 2026 guidance: 11–14% y/y GMV growth, Adjusted EBITDA USD 510–540m, net income USD 280–310m, FCF USD 370–400m
- More than USD 100 million of investments earmarked for 2026 to scale grocery integrated vertical (talabat mart) and enhance the loyalty subscription programme (talabat pro)

Dubai, UAE, 13 February 2026. Results are pro forma for the three-month and full-year period ended 31 December 2025; excludes instashop (consolidated in reported financials as of 25 Feb 2025).

## Q4 2025 highlights

- GMV USD 2.5bn, +21% y/y (same at constant currency) — robust order volume growth (customer acquisition and higher order frequency) across all markets, with a surge in talabat pro adoption
  - GCC GMV USD 2.0bn, +15%, 80% of total (prior year: 84%). GCC = UAE, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain, Oman
  - **Non-GCC GMV USD 501m, +57%, 20% of total (prior year: 16%). Non-GCC = Egypt, Jordan, Iraq**
  - Food GMV USD 1.7bn, +12%, 68% of total (prior year: 73%)
  - Grocery & Retail (G&R) GMV USD 788m, +45%, 32% of total (prior year: 27%). G&R = talabat mart dark stores (1P) + Local Shops (3P)
- Management Revenue USD 1.0bn, +26%; GMV-to-revenue conversion 42% (prior year 40%) — higher share of talabat mart and subscription revenues more than offset lower commission rates (lower due to higher G&R share of GMV)
- Adjusted EBITDA USD 156m, +13%, = 6.3% of GMV (prior year 6.8%) — lower gross profit margins from GMV product-mix shift, partly offset by improved operating cost margins
- Net income USD 123m, −11% y/y, = 5.0% of GMV (prior year 6.7%) — reflects increased corporate income tax rates of 15% in GCC markets and base effects (deferred tax income in prior year)
- Adjusted Net Income USD 124m, +1%, = 5.0% of GMV
- Adjusted Free Cash Flow USD 134m, +14%, = 5.4% of GMV; Cash Conversion Ratio 86% (prior year 85%)

## FY 2025 (constant currency)

- GMV +28% to USD 9.5bn; Revenue +33% to USD 3.9bn; Adjusted EBITDA USD 615m = 6.5% of GMV
- All three metrics achieved guidance (revised upwards during the year after strong H1)
- Net income USD 464m = 4.9% of GMV; Adjusted FCF USD 559m = 5.9% of GMV — both near guidance

## Dividends

Final dividend USD 219m recommended → total 2025 dividends USD 421m = 90% payout of reported net income, exceeding previous guidance of USD 400m. Subject to AGM approval.

## 2026 investment plan (>USD 100m)

1. **Scaling talabat mart (grocery integrated vertical):** improve affordability to accelerate customer adoption; increase store density to reinforce speed-led value proposition; expand supply chain infrastructure for assortment and availability. Investments expected to be offset over the long term through higher adtech / non-merchandising revenue; strong early traction in best-performing talabat mart markets.
2. **Strengthening talabat pro (loyalty subscription) as a multi-vertical engagement engine:** core free delivery plus booster discounts on Food best sellers, discounts on key value items in talabat mart, on-time guarantee, priority support, dine-out discounts, partner services (streaming, ride-hailing). Now available in all eight markets after successful 2025 launches in **Egypt and Iraq**.

Investments expected to weigh on near-term margins; Board and management aligned that this maximizes long-term shareholder value.

## CEO commentary

Toon Gyssels, newly-appointed CEO: 2025 delivered GMV growth of 28% cFX, Adjusted EBITDA margin 6.5%, net income margin 4.9% — "amongst the highest in the industry." 2026: >USD 100m ecosystem investments to expand multi-vertical subscriber base via talabat pro and talabat mart.

## Definitions (from footnotes)

- **GMV:** total value (incl. VAT) paid by end customers for goods/services sold through the platform (excl. rider tips and customer subscription fees)
- **Management Revenue:** IFRS revenue before deducting vouchers/discounts issued by talabat to customers
- **Adjusted EBITDA:** net income before current income tax, net finance costs, net FX loss, depreciation, other non-income-tax and non-operating effects (incl. share-based compensation)
- **Adjusted Net Income:** net income excluding FX income/loss (mainly non-cash unrealised FX loss from shareholder loan liability in **Delivery Hero Egypt SAE**) and interest on pre-IPO-capitalised shareholder loans/deposits
- **Adjusted FCF:** Adjusted EBITDA − Δworking capital − capex − IFRS 16 lease payments − tax
- **Cash Conversion Ratio:** Adjusted FCF / Adjusted EBITDA

## About talabat (company description)

Leading on-demand delivery platform in MENA: food, groceries, convenience. Founded in Kuwait 2004. Markets: UAE, Kuwait, Qatar, Egypt, Bahrain, Oman, Jordan, Iraq. Over 6.5 million active customers as of December 2024. HQ Dubai. IPO on DFM December 2024. Subsidiary of Delivery Hero SE.
