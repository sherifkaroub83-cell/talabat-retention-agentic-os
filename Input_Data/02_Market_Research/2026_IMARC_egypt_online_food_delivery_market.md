## Key drivers (per IMARC)

1. **Internet penetration:** 75.66 million internet users in Egypt in January 2022 (ITA). Wider reach into urban and suburban areas; growth of e-commerce habits, digital payments (cards, mobile wallets) alongside cash on delivery.
2. **Student population:** Egypt has the largest student population in MENA — 24 million K-12 students in 2022 (ITA). Tech-comfortable, busy schedules, group ordering, student discounts/promotions.

## Segmentation used by the report

- **Platform type:** mobile applications, website
- **Business model:** order-focused, logistics-based, full-service food delivery systems
- **Payment method:** online payment, cash on delivery
- **Regions:** Greater Cairo, Alexandria, Suez Canal, Delta, others
