## Talabat-relevant findings

- **Talabat's InstaShop acquisition (USD 360m)** cited as demonstrating delivery-platform / dark-store integration supporting both restaurant delivery and quick commerce. **⚠️ Correction:** USD 360m was Delivery Hero's 2020 purchase price of InstaShop; talabat's 2025 acquisition from Delivery Hero was USD 32m (internal group restructuring) — see competitor landscape note in 03_Competitors
- Delivery platforms (Talabat, Akelni, Mrsool) expanding beyond major urban centers into secondary cities; enabling cloud-kitchen operators where traditional restaurant economics fail
- Market concentration score 3/10 (fragmented) — leaders: Americana Restaurants, Alamar Foods, Mansour Group, Mo'men Group, Olayan Group
- Geographic concentration: Greater Cairo, Alexandria, Giza; secondary cities/rural emerging via delivery platforms but constrained by power reliability and cold-chain gaps
- Recent entries: Nobu Egypt (Aug 2025), Pizza Inn (Jun 2025, 7-store franchise), Pickl Cairo flagship (Oct 2024)
