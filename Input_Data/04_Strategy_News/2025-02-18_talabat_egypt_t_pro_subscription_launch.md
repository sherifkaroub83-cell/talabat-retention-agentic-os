# talabat Egypt Launches "t pro" Subscription Service

- **Source:** https://corporate.talabat.com/news/corporate-website-news-item-6/
- **Publisher:** talabat corporate newsroom (official press release)
- **Date:** 18 February 2025
- **Retrieved:** 20 July 2026

---

Cairo, February 2025: talabat Egypt launched the "t pro" subscription service.

## Offer structure

- **Pricing: EGP 79/month or EGP 799/year**, with a two-week free trial; subscribe/cancel in-app
- Unlimited free delivery from selected premium restaurants and talabat mart for orders above **EGP 150** (talabat mart), **EGP 180** (supermarkets), **EGP 200** (hypermarkets)
- Exclusive discounts up to **30% on 400+ products** in talabat mart

## Quotes

**Hadeer Shalaby, Managing Director of talabat Egypt:** "'t pro' service is part of talabat's comprehensive strategy to provide innovative services tailored to our users' fast-paced daily lives in Egypt… enables them to significantly reduce expenses while effortlessly fulfilling their daily needs."

## Egypt operational datapoints

- **10,000+ partners** in Egypt; strategic partnerships with **50+ leading companies**

## Context

- talabat pro launched in Egypt and Iraq in 2025, completing availability in all eight markets (per Q4 2025 results press release)
- Subscription fees & other income was the fastest-growing revenue line group-wide in FY2025 (+47%)
