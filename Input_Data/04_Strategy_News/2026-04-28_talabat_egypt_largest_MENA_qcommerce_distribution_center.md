# talabat Egypt Inaugurates the Largest Quick-Commerce Distribution Center in MENA

- **Source:** https://corporate.talabat.com/news/under-the-patronage-of-he-prime-minister-dr-mostafa-madbouly-talabat-egypt-inaugurates-the-largest-quick-commerce-distribution-center-in-the-middle-east-and-north-africa/
- **Publisher:** talabat corporate newsroom (official press release)
- **Date:** 28 April 2026
- **Retrieved:** 20 July 2026

---

Cairo, Egypt — 28 April 2026: talabat Egypt inaugurated the largest quick-commerce distribution center in MENA, under the patronage of PM Dr. Mostafa Madbouly. Attended by the Ministers of Communications & IT (Eng. Raafat Hendy, on behalf of the PM), Supply & Internal Trade, Finance, Investment & Foreign Trade, and Industry — underscoring the project's significance to Egypt's digital economy and logistics infrastructure.

## Facility facts

- Developed in partnership with **Hassan Allam Utilities** within the **"Yanmu East" logistics hub on the Cairo–Suez Road**
- ~**27,000 sqm**; supports up to **1 million items per day**
- Serves **100% of talabat mart's network across 12 cities, expansion to 17 underway**
- Fully integrated real-time systems connecting inventory, stores, and replenishment
- **In-house AI models forecast demand, optimize stock positioning, and automate replenishment** — improving accuracy, efficiency, responsiveness; reducing waste
- ~**75,000 pallet positions**, with plans to **double capacity within the same footprint** (vertical space utilization)

## Quotes

- **Toon Gyssels, CEO, talabat:** "This is a big step for us in Egypt and across the region… As quick commerce keeps growing, this kind of infrastructure is what allows us to stay ahead."
- **Eng. Mohamed Sekkina, Regional Director of talabat mart:** "Not just a storage facility, but smart infrastructure reshaping quick commerce in Egypt in line with global standards."

## Egypt as talent powerhouse (key operational datapoints)

- talabat employs **3,100 people in Egypt**
- **Global Tech and Shared Services hub in Egypt: 1,500 employees** dedicated to supporting all eight regional markets
- This offshore team delivers **74% of the company's shared services** and develops **30% of its app features**
